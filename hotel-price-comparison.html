Hotel Price Comparison From 195 Countries

Comparing a few booking sites is a start. Arbitrica searches the same hotel from 195 countries at checkout and finds the lowest price on Earth before you pay.

Search Before You Pay, Not After

Traditional hotel price comparison shows you a few booking sites side by side: Booking.com, Expedia, Hotels.com. That helps, but every result is still searched from your own country. It leaves out one of the biggest variables in hotel pricing: the country the search is made from.

Different countries get access to different inventory, channels, local rates and promotions. Searching the same hotel from another country can open up cheaper prices you cannot get from your own country. Arbitrica goes beyond comparison. When you reach checkout, it searches the same hotel from 195 countries and finds the lowest price on Earth for it, no matter who sells it.

Why Searching From Other Countries Matters

The same hotel is often 5-30% cheaper when searched from another country. You could try to find those prices yourself with a VPN, switching one country at a time and entering your dates again on each search. With 195 countries that is impossible in practice. Arbitrica runs the full search for you while you are on the checkout page.

A real example: One Arbitrica user was offered a hotel on Booking for $1,527.39. Arbitrica found the same hotel for $1,180.27 and earned the user $347.12, or 23%.

How It Works at Checkout

  1. You reach checkout. Arbitrica, a personal AI agent that lives in your browser, shows up when you are about to pay.
  2. It searches the same hotel, with your dates and room type, from 195 countries in real time.
  3. It validates that the lower price is for the identical hotel, includes all taxes and fees, and will not suddenly change.
  4. It prepares the booking at the lowest price automatically, so you only review and pay.

After You Book

Hotel prices keep moving after you pay. If your booking has free cancellation, a lower price found later is still worth having. The mechanism Arbitrica developed repeats the search from 195 countries at many points in time until the cancellation window ends, then prepares a re-booking at the lower price and cancels the original.

Not Just Hotels

The same mechanisms work beyond hotels: online shopping such as Amazon, flights, car rentals, event tickets, pre-orders and subscriptions. Anything priced differently from one country to another, or bought with a cancellation or return window, can be searched the same way.
