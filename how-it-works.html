How Arbitrica works

Arbitrica is your personal AI agent. When you are about to pay, it searches what you are buying from 195 countries, finds the lowest price on Earth for it and gets it for you at that price.

The short version

Arbitrica is your personal AI agent for buying things at the lowest price on Earth. It lives in your browser and shows up at the checkout when you are about to pay. It reads the price you have been given, searches the same hotel, product or tickets from 195 countries, and finds the lowest price that exists for it anywhere in the world, no matter who sells it. Then it gets it for you at that price.

For hotels, Arbitrica automatically prepares everything and you only review and pay. For online shopping and event tickets, once you say yes, the agent makes the purchase or books the tickets for you.

Why the same thing has more than one price

Most things you buy online have dozens of prices that exist at the same time. The one you are shown depends on which country you search from and which site you happen to be on. Different countries get access to different inventory, sales channels, local rates and promotions, so searching the same hotel, product or tickets from another country can open up a lower price that you cannot get from your own country.

Prices also move over time. A hotel, a flight or a pair of concert tickets can cost less next week than today. Arbitrica works on both differences:

  • Geographic arbitrage: the same item, searched from 195 countries, to find the lowest price on Earth.
  • Time arbitrage: the same search repeated at different points in time, at checkout (live arbitrage) and after you pay (multiple-time arbitrage).

Doing the country search by hand means a VPN and one country at a time. Nobody can do that for 195 countries before a checkout times out. Arbitrica does it for you. Read more in what geographic price arbitrage is and time arbitrage explained.

What happens at checkout, step by step

The three demo videos on our hotels, Amazon and events pages show the same flow. Each category has its own agent: Agent Butler for hotels, Agent Shoppy for online shopping and Agent Ticketo for event tickets.

  1. You shop the way you always do. Pick a hotel on Booking, a product on Amazon or seats on a ticket site and go to the checkout.
  2. Your agent shows up. A chat opens next to the page: “Hello, agent Butler from Arbitrica here. My job is to give you world’s lowest price for this hotel.” You can also open it yourself from the Arbitrica tab on the right edge of the page.
  3. It reads your price and confirms what you are buying. For a hotel that is the hotel, dates, guests, room type, cancellation terms and breakfast. For a product it is the exact item. For tickets it is the event, section, row and number of tickets. This is what stops a cheaper but different item from slipping in.
  4. It searches from 195 countries. You see the search run on a world map while the agent works. You can carry on with other things in the meantime.
  5. It validates the result. The agent checks that the lower price is for exactly the same hotel, product or tickets, that it includes all taxes and fees (and delivery, for products), and that the price will not suddenly change when you go to pay.
  6. It shows you what it found. “The price they gave you”, what the agent makes you, and “The world’s lowest price”, with the country it was found in and the kind of provider selling it. You can ask it to show you where it found the price.
  7. It gets it for you. Hotels: press “Prepare the booking” and Butler prepares everything in a new tab, so you only review and complete the payment. Shopping and tickets: press “Make the purchase” or “Book tickets” and the agent buys it for you. For a purchase, the confirmation is sent to your email.

After you pay: multiple-time arbitrage

Many purchases can still be cancelled or returned for a while: hotels with free cancellation, flights, car rentals, some event tickets, pre-orders and delayed-shipment orders, and subscriptions. Inside that window a lower price found later is still money you can have.

The mechanism Arbitrica developed repeats the search from 195 countries at many points in time after the purchase, until the cancellation or return window ends. When it finds the identical item for less, with all taxes and fees included, it prepares a new booking or purchase at the lower price and the original is cancelled. If the price drops, you hear about it by email. See hotel price drops after booking for how this plays out on hotels.

Where Arbitrica lives

Arbitrica is a personal AI agent, not a price list or a comparison site.
