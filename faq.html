About Arbitrica

What is Arbitrica?

Arbitrica is a personal AI agent that gets you what you are buying at the lowest price on Earth. It lives in your browser and shows up at the checkout when you are about to pay. It searches the same hotel, product or tickets from 195 countries, finds the lowest price that exists for it, no matter who sells it, and buys it for you at that price.

What are hidden prices?

Most things you buy online have dozens of prices that exist at the same time. The price people see depends on which country they search from, which site they are on, and what pricing algorithms are doing at that moment: dynamic pricing that moves with demand and timing, different rates for different markets and sales channels, and promotions that only some countries get. The others are “hidden” because you would never come across them from your own country. Arbitrica finds them. Read the guide to hidden hotel prices.

Do I need to install Arbitrica?

Arbitrica is a personal AI agent that lives in your browser. You can install it for free in two clicks as a browser extension.

How it works

How does Arbitrica find a lower price?

Different countries get access to different inventory, sales channels, local rates and promotions, so searching the same hotel, product or tickets from another country can open up a lower price that you cannot get from your own country. Arbitrica searches from 195 countries at once and finds the lowest price on Earth for the identical item. See how Arbitrica works for the full flow.

What is geographic price arbitrage?

Geographic price arbitrage means buying the same item at a lower price that is available from another country. Arbitrica does it automatically, from 195 countries, for hotels, online shopping and event tickets. Read what geographic price arbitrage is.

What happens at checkout?

Arbitrica’s agents open a chat next to the checkout, read the price you have been given and confirm exactly what you are buying. They search from 195 countries, validate the result and show you the price you were given, what the agent makes you and the world’s lowest price.

How does Arbitrica make sure it is the same hotel, product or tickets?

Before it shows you a result, the agent confirms the details that matter: for a hotel the hotel name, dates, guests, room type, cancellation terms and breakfast; for a product the exact item; for tickets the event, section, row and quantity. It also checks that the price includes all taxes and fees (and delivery, for products) and that it will not suddenly change when you pay.

What are live arbitrage and multiple-time arbitrage?

Both are forms of time arbitrage, which means repeating the search at different points in time. Live arbitrage happens at checkout, in real time, before you pay. Multiple-time arbitrage repeats the search after you pay, until the free-cancellation or return window ends. Combined with the search from 195 countries, the lowest price is found across both time and geography. Read time arbitrage explained.

What happens after I have already paid?

The mechanism Arbitrica developed keeps searching the same item from 195 countries at many points in time after the purchase, until the cancellation or return window ends. When the identical item becomes cheaper, with all taxes and fees included, it prepares a new booking or purchase at the lower price. The new booking is confirmed first, and only then is the original cancelled.

What if my booking cannot be cancelled?

Re-booking after you pay needs a free-cancellation or return window. The search at checkout works whatever the cancellation policy is, because you have not paid yet.

Why are hotel prices different in different countries?

Hotels and booking sites sell through different channels in different markets, with local rates, promotions and taxes that only apply in some countries. So the same hotel, on the same dates, can have a lower price when searched from another country. See Booking.com prices in different countries and hotel price discrimination by location.

Which country is cheapest to book hotels from?

There is no single cheapest country to book hotels from. It changes with the hotel, the dates and the seller, which is why Arbitrica searches the same hotel from all 195 countries every time. More in the cheapest country to book hotels from.

Why do hotel prices change daily?

Hotels use dynamic pricing: revenue management systems move rates with demand, occupancy, events, how far ahead people book and what competitors charge. That is why the same hotel can be cheaper next week than today. Read why hotel prices change daily and how hotel dynamic pricing works.

Hotels

Can I get a lower price after booking a hotel?

Often, yes. Hotel prices drop after booking more often than people expect, and with free cancellation you can rebook the same hotel at the lower price. But you often need to act immediately once the lower price shows up, or someone else takes it. That is why you need a personal agent that acts the moment it happens: Arbitrica’s approach repeats the search from 195 countries after you book and prepares the re-booking as soon as the price drops. See hotel price drop after booking.

Is Arbitrica a hotel price tracker?

Arbitrica goes further than a hotel price tracker or a hotel price drop alert. It searches the same hotel from 195 countries at checkout, then keeps searching after you book and prepares an automatic hotel rebooking at the lower price. See hotel price tracker and auto-rebook a hotel when the rate drops.

Does Booking.com show different prices in different countries?

Booking.com and other sites can show the same hotel at different prices depending on the country you search from, because of local rates, promotions and channels. Arbitrica checks the same hotel from 195 countries to find the lowest one. Read Booking.com different prices explained and Booking.com price match.

Does it work for hotels in my own country?

Yes. Searching from other countries can open up a lower price for a hotel in your own country too, because rates and promotions differ by the country you search from, wherever the hotel is.

Online shopping and event tickets

Do event ticket prices drop?

Often. Resale and primary ticket prices move over time and differ between sellers, so the same seats can be cheaper elsewhere or later. Arbitrica searches the same tickets at checkout and keeps looking after you buy where the tickets can be cancelled. See Arbitrica for event tickets and when event ticket prices drop.

Which sites does Arbitrica work with?

Arbitrica shows up at the checkout of the site you are buying on, and the lowest price can come from any legitimate seller: Booking, Expedia, Amazon, a ticket marketplace, the hotel itself or anyone else. What matters is that it is the identical item at a lower total price. The seller is never the story.
