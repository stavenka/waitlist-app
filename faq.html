About Arbitrica

What is Arbitrica?

Arbitrica is a personal AI agent that gets you what you are buying at the lowest price on Earth. It lives in your browser and shows up at the checkout when you are about to pay. It searches the same hotel, product or tickets from 195 countries, finds the lowest price that exists for it anywhere in the world, no matter who sells it, and gets it for you at that price.

Who is Agent Butler?

Agent Butler is Arbitrica’s agent for hotels. The same kind of agent handles online shopping such as Amazon and event tickets. You can see the agent at work in the demo videos on our hotels, Amazon and events pages.

What are hidden prices?

Most things you buy online have dozens of prices that exist at the same time. The one you see depends on which country you search from and which site you are on. The others are “hidden” only in the sense that you would never come across them from your own country. Arbitrica finds them.

Is Arbitrica a browser extension?

Arbitrica is a personal AI agent. It lives in your browser. What it does is act for you: it finds the lowest price on Earth for what you are buying and gets it for you.

How it works

How does Arbitrica find a lower price?

Different countries get access to different inventory, sales channels, local rates and promotions, so searching the same hotel, product or tickets from another country can open up a lower price that you cannot get from your own country. Arbitrica searches from 195 countries at once and finds the lowest price on Earth for the identical item. See how Arbitrica works for the full flow.

What happens at checkout?

Your agent opens a chat next to the checkout, reads the price you have been given and confirms exactly what you are buying. It searches from 195 countries, validates the result and shows you the price you were given, what it makes you and the world’s lowest price. For hotels it then prepares everything so you only review and pay. For shopping and tickets, once you say yes, it makes the purchase or books the tickets for you.

How does Arbitrica make sure it is the same hotel, product or tickets?

Before it shows you a result, the agent confirms the details that matter: for a hotel the dates, guests, room type, cancellation terms and breakfast; for a product the exact item; for tickets the event, section, row and quantity. It also checks that the price includes all taxes and fees (and delivery, for products) and that it will not suddenly change when you pay.

Does Arbitrica pay for me?

For hotels, Arbitrica automatically prepares everything and you review and complete the payment yourself. For online shopping and event tickets, the agent can make the purchase for you once you press “Make the purchase” or “Book tickets”. Nothing is bought without your go-ahead.

Why not just use a VPN?

A VPN lets you search from one country at a time. You would have to connect to server after server, check the price each time and work out which result is really the identical item at the lowest total. Doing that for 195 countries is impossible in practice. Arbitrica does it for you, then validates the result and gets it for you.

What are live arbitrage and multiple-time arbitrage?

Both are forms of time arbitrage, which means repeating the search at different points in time. Live arbitrage happens at checkout, in real time, before you pay. Multiple-time arbitrage repeats the search after you pay, until the free-cancellation or return window ends. Combined with the search from 195 countries, the lowest price is found across both time and geography. Read time arbitrage explained.

What happens after I have already paid?

The mechanism Arbitrica developed keeps searching the same item from 195 countries at many points in time after the purchase, until the cancellation or return window ends. When the identical item becomes cheaper, with all taxes and fees included, it emails you and prepares a new booking or purchase at the lower price. The new booking is confirmed first, and only then is the original cancelled.

What if my booking cannot be cancelled?

Re-booking after you pay needs a free-cancellation or return window. The search at checkout works whatever the cancellation policy is, because you have not paid yet.

Savings

How much can Arbitrica earn me?

It depends on what you buy, where and when. The same item is often 5-30% cheaper somewhere else. Research demonstrated that people earn on average $47 per hotel booking and up to $3,000 a year, $34 per online purchase and up to $3,000 a year, and $107 per event ticket purchase and up to $1,500 a year.

Can you show a real example?

In our demos, Hôtel Pont Royal Paris went from $1,332.40 to $1,034.62, a Samsung soundbar from $997.99 on Amazon to $747.99, and two concert tickets at Madison Square Garden from $1,300.00 to $1,070.00.

Does it work for hotels in my own country?

Yes. Searching from other countries can open up a lower price for a hotel in your own country too, because rates and promotions differ by the country you search from, wherever the hotel is.

What Arbitrica covers

Does Arbitrica only work for hotels?

No. Hotels, online shopping such as Amazon and event tickets are the three categories in our demos, and the same mechanisms apply to flights, car rental, cruises, rail, experiences, subscriptions such as streaming and software, games, online courses and pre-orders. Anything that is sold at different prices in different countries, or that can still be cancelled or returned after you buy, can be bought for less.

Which sites does Arbitrica work with?

Arbitrica shows up at the checkout of the site you are buying on, and the lowest price can come from any legitimate seller: Booking, Expedia, Amazon, a ticket marketplace, the hotel itself or anyone else. What matters is that it is the identical item at a lower total price. The seller is never the story.

Can companies use Arbitrica?

Yes. Arbitrica works for individuals and for companies: teams travelling to trade shows and conferences, operations managers and travel managers. It searches the same hotel from 195 countries at checkout and prepares the booking for review. See business travel hotel savings.
