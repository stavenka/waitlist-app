# Are hidden hotel offers available for specific room categories?

Published 2026-11-10 by Arbitrica. Canonical: https://arbitrica.com/blog/hidden-hotel-offers-specific-room-categories.html

- Yes. Hotels price each room category and rate plan separately, so an offer can exist for one room type and not another.

## Short answer

Yes. Hotels price each room category and each rate plan separately, and most targeted offers, such as country rates, member prices and package rates, are attached to the room types and rate plans the hotel picks. Booking's own partner help says the 10% Genius discount for a new Genius partner is applied automatically to its least expensive and most popular room type. So a lower price for the same hotel can exist for the standard double and not the suite, or the other way round, and the room you want has to be checked on its own.

## How hotels price room categories

A hotel does not have one price. It has a grid: every room category (standard, superior, deluxe, suite) is sold under several rate plans (room only or with breakfast, refundable or not), on several sites, in several markets. Each cell of that grid can carry its own price and its own availability, and [dynamic pricing](https://arbitrica.com/blog/how-hotel-dynamic-pricing-works.html) moves each one with demand.

That is why "hidden hotel prices" is not a single secret rate. It is the set of cells you do not see from your own country and your usual site, including lower prices for specific room categories.

## Which offers depend on the room (checked on November 10, 2026)

| Offer | Who sees it | Room categories it covers |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Country or geo rates | Travellers searching from selected countries; Booking's developer documentation describes them as rates available based on the traveller's country | Set by the hotel; channel-manager guides say they can be applied across room types, room rates and rate plans |
| Booking Genius discount | Signed-in Genius members | For a new Genius partner, applied automatically to the least expensive and most popular room type, per Booking's partner help |
| Booking mobile rates | People booking in the app or on a phone | A minimum 10% discount applied to all mobile room and rate plans, per a Booking connectivity partner |
| Hotel member rates | Members of the chain's loyalty programme booking direct | Set by the chain, often on selected room types and dates |
| Packages and meal plans | Anyone on the site that sells the package | Usually specific rooms, since breakfast or parking is priced per rate plan |

The pattern is the same everywhere: the hotel decides which rooms get which offer, and the site and country you search from decide which of those offers you see. More on where these rates come from in the [guide to hidden hotel prices](https://arbitrica.com/blog/hidden-hotel-prices-guide.html).

## Why the gap differs by room

- **Availability is per room type.** A category can be sold out on one site or in one market and still on sale in another.
- **Offers are per rate plan.** A country rate or package may sit on the breakfast rate of one category only.
- **Prices move separately.** When the lowest-priced category fills up, its price can rise while a higher category stays where it was.
- **Currency and taxes.** The same room can carry a different total in another currency or with different taxes included, so only the final total tells you which is lower.

The result is that a price gap you find from another country for one room says little about the next room. [Booking prices in different countries](https://arbitrica.com/blog/booking-com-prices-different-countries.html) differ for this reason too.

## How to find a lower price for the room you want

1. **Fix the exact room** Write down the room category, bed type, meal plan and cancellation policy you want.
2. **Check that room on several sites** The hotel's own site, Booking, Expedia and others can each carry different offers for it.
3. **Check it from several countries** Country rates and local promotions only show up from the market they are made for.
4. **Weigh the final total** Include taxes, fees and the currency you will be charged in, never the headline price.
5. **Book refundable and keep checking** If the same room gets a lower price later, you can [rebook it at the lower price](https://arbitrica.com/blog/rebook-hotel-at-lower-price.html) and cancel the original once the new booking is confirmed.

## Check the next category up

Because every category is priced on its own, a higher room category from another country or on another site can sometimes cost less than you expected, or not much more than the room you planned. It is worth checking the next category up with the same conditions before you pay.

The gaps are large enough to matter. ## Frequently asked questions

### Do member prices apply to every room type?
Not always. Hotels choose which room types and rate plans get a member price. Booking's partner help says a new Genius partner's 10% discount is applied automatically to its least expensive and most popular room type (checked on November 10, 2026).

### Are country rates available for suites?
They can be. Country rates are set by the hotel, and channel-manager guides say they can be applied across room types and rate plans, but each hotel decides. The only way to know is to check that suite from the country the rate is made for.

### Can a price guarantee give me a hidden offer for my room type?
Rarely. Price guarantees need exactly the same room type, meal plan and cancellation policy, and many exclude member, app and residency-based rates. It is simpler to book the lower price directly at the site that offers it.

### Why is one room priced lower from another country but not another room?
Each room category has its own availability, offers and dynamic pricing, on every site and in every market. A gap for one room says little about the next, so check the exact room you want.

### Does the room category matter for price drops after booking?
Yes. Rebooking only works for the same room type and conditions, so track the price of your exact room. With a free-cancellation rate, book the lower price first and cancel the original once the new booking is confirmed.
