If the price of your Expedia hotel drops after you book, Expedia will not refund the difference. Its Hotel Price Guarantee, the program that once paid members when they found a lower rate after booking, ended on July 28, 2026. What still works is simpler and more reliable: book a refundable rate, keep checking, rebook the identical hotel at the lower price before free cancellation ends, and search the same hotel from other countries before you pay.

Does Expedia refund a hotel price drop?

Not anymore. Expedia and Hotels.com used to offer a Hotel Price Guarantee to One Key members: if you found a lower publicly available rate for the same hotel after booking, you could claim the difference. AwardWallet and Upgraded Points both reported that Expedia eliminated the Hotel Price Guarantee on Expedia and Hotels.com effective July 28, 2026, as part of a wider overhaul of One Key, and Upgraded Points noted there is no replacement.

This was the second time Expedia stepped back from price matching. Skift reported that in October 2017 Expedia-branded sites dropped their long-standing promise to match a lower price found on other public sites within 24 hours of booking, which also came with a $50 travel coupon in the US. Old guides and forum posts still describe these programs, so if you read that "Expedia will match the price", check the date first.

Tip

Old Expedia help pages about the Best Price Guarantee can still be found online. The terms that apply are the ones in force when you book, so do not rely on a guarantee page you cannot find linked from your current booking.

What Expedia's Price Drop Protection covers (and what it does not)

Expedia still sells something called Price Drop Protection, and it causes a lot of confusion because the name sounds like a hotel refund. According to Expedia's own Price Drop Protection pages, it applies only to selected flights:

  • Standard Price Drop Protection is an add-on you pay for on eligible flights. If the fare for the exact same flight drops on Expedia, Expedia says it refunds the difference after your trip.
  • One Key Price Drop Protection is included for One Key Gold and Platinum members on selected flights booked in the Expedia app. Any drop is paid as OneKeyCash after the trip, which Expedia's terms say is not redeemable for cash and can only be used on Expedia, Hotels.com and Vrbo.

Both versions check the price automatically every 24 hours, and both only look at prices on Expedia itself. A lower fare on an airline's own site or on another travel site does not count. The terms also exclude Member Prices and flight plus hotel bundles, and changing the flight can cancel the protection.

None of this covers a standalone hotel booking. If you booked a hotel on Expedia and the rate falls, Price Drop Protection does nothing for you.

How to get a lower price after booking an Expedia hotel

Without a guarantee, the price you pay depends on one thing: whether your booking still has free cancellation. If it does, a price drop is money you can claim yourself by rebooking. If it cannot, your options are much weaker.

If you booked a refundable rate

  1. Find your cancellation deadlineOpen the booking in My Trips and note the exact date and time free cancellation ends, including the time zone shown.
  2. Check the same hotel againSearch the same hotel, dates, room type, board and cancellation terms. Compare the total with all taxes and fees, not the nightly rate.
  3. Book the lower price firstMake the new booking and wait for the confirmation email with a confirmation number.
  4. Then cancel the originalCancel the first booking only after the new one is confirmed, and keep the cancellation email until the refund reaches your card.

The new booking does not have to be on Expedia. The identical hotel may be lower at another travel site, at the hotel chain's own site, or from another country. What matters is that the hotel, dates, room, inclusions and cancellation terms match, so that you are moving to the same stay at a lower total.

If you booked a non-refundable rate

A non-refundable booking cannot be rebooked without losing what you paid. You can contact Expedia customer service, give your itinerary number and ask whether anything can be done, but with the Hotel Price Guarantee gone there is no published program that obliges Expedia to adjust the price. Treat any goodwill credit as a bonus, not a plan. For future trips, this is the strongest argument for paying a little more on day one for a flexible rate.

Refundable rates are your price guarantee now

A refundable rate does what the old guarantee promised, and more. The guarantee only paid when you found a lower public price and filed a claim, and it paid on the terms Expedia set. A refundable booking lets you act on any lower price for the identical hotel, wherever you find it, until the deadline passes. Search the same hotel from other countries

Waiting for a drop is one route. The other is to find the lower price before you book at all. The same hotel, on the same dates, can come in at very different prices depending on which country you search from, because different countries get access to different inventory, channels, local rates and promotions.

Expedia price match: what to say if you contact support

If you still want to ask Expedia about a lower price, be precise. Have your itinerary number, the exact hotel name, dates, room type and cancellation terms, and a screenshot of the lower total with taxes and fees. Ask a direct question: can the booking be adjusted, or can it be cancelled without a penalty so you can rebook? Do not cancel anything on the strength of a chat promise until you have it confirmed in writing.

The same applies to Hotels.com, which shared the same guarantee and ended it on the same date. The guide to the Hotels.com price guarantee covers the details for that site.

Frequently asked questions

Does Expedia refund the difference if my hotel price drops?

No. Expedia and Hotels.com ended the Hotel Price Guarantee on July 28, 2026, and no replacement has been announced. If your rate is refundable, you can rebook at the lower price and cancel the original.

Does Expedia price match?

Not for hotels. Expedia dropped its general price-match promise in 2017 and its Hotel Price Guarantee in July 2026. You can still contact customer service and ask, but there is no published obligation to adjust the price.

Does Expedia Price Drop Protection cover hotels?

No. According to Expedia, Price Drop Protection applies to selected flights only, and it only counts lower prices on Expedia for the exact same flight.

How do I rebook an Expedia hotel at a lower price?

Check that your booking still has free cancellation, book the identical hotel at the lower total, wait for the confirmation, then cancel the original in My Trips before the deadline.
