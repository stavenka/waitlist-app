A Booking.com price tracker watches the price of a hotel you are interested in, or one you have already booked, and tells you when it drops. That is useful, because Booking.com prices move all the time. But a tracker only watches one listing, on one site, as seen from one country, and the lower price you are looking for is often somewhere it never checks.

Why Booking.com hotel prices change

The price you see for a hotel on Booking.com is set by the hotel's revenue management system and the deals it runs on the platform. It reacts to how fast rooms are selling, what nearby hotels charge, events in the city and the number of days left before arrival. The same stay can be priced several times between the day you first look and the day you check in.

Some of the difference is also about who is looking. Booking.com's Genius loyalty program gives signed-in members discounts at participating properties, so a price checked while signed in can differ from one checked while signed out. If you track a price, keep the conditions the same every time, or you will be measuring your own settings instead of the hotel's rate.

How to track a Booking.com price by hand

You do not need any software to track one hotel. You need a consistent method and a few minutes every couple of days.

  1. Fix the exact searchSame hotel, check-in and check-out dates, number of adults and children, room type, meal plan and cancellation terms.
  2. Keep your sign-in state the sameCheck either signed in or signed out every time, so Genius discounts do not distort the record.
  3. Record the total, not the nightly rateGo far enough into the booking flow to see the final amount with taxes and fees, and write it down with the date.
  4. Check at a regular rhythmEvery two or three days is enough for most trips; more often in the last week before free cancellation ends.
  5. Act on a real dropIf the total falls for the identical stay and your booking is refundable, rebook first and cancel the original only after the new booking is confirmed.

A simple spreadsheet with one row per check is the best price history you can get for a specific stay. It also shows you how much the price moves, which tells you whether waiting is worth it.

Booking.com price drop alerts and tracking tools

If checking by hand sounds like work, several kinds of tools will watch prices for you. They fall into three groups.

Search engine price tracking

Google added individual hotel price tracking to Google Hotels in April 2026, according to 9to5Google. On a hotel's page you can switch on "Track this hotel" for your dates and receive email alerts when the price changes. At launch it was available to signed-in users in English and Spanish. It watches the prices Google shows for that hotel, which makes it a good starting point before you book.

Post-booking trackers

Post-booking trackers watch a reservation you already have and alert you when the rate falls. RoomHawk, for example, says it tracks your booking automatically and monitors more than 2 million rooms. Pruvo, one of the early names in this space, now focuses on business customers. Services in this group differ in how you add a booking, how often they check and whether they help with the rebooking itself, so read each one's own description before you rely on it.

Browser add-ons

Some browser extensions add a price history or a price-drop alert to Booking.com pages. They are convenient for watching one listing over time while you decide.

What a hotel price tracker can and cannot do

A tracker canA tracker cannot
Tell you when the price of a listing you watch goes downFind prices from countries it does not search from
Save you from checking every dayGuarantee that the price in an alert is still available when you click
Show how much a price moves over timeConfirm that the cheaper offer has the same room, inclusions and cancellation terms
Prompt you to rebook before free cancellation endsHelp with a non-refundable booking, which cannot be rebooked without a loss

The biggest gap is the first one. A tracker watches movement over time. It does not answer a different question: is the same hotel, on the same dates, available at a lower price right now from somewhere else?

Rebooking after a Booking.com price drop

An alert only becomes money if you can act on it. With a free cancellation booking, you can book the identical stay at the lower total, wait for the confirmation and then cancel the original before the deadline. The new booking does not have to be on Booking.com; the identical hotel may be lower at another site or at the hotel's own website. Make sure the room type, meal plan and cancellation terms match before you cancel anything. The full process is in the guide to rebooking a hotel at a lower price.

If you found a lower price elsewhere before your stay, Booking.com's own price match policy may also apply. The article on the Booking.com price match explains how it works and where it falls short.

Where trackers stop: the same hotel from other countries

Every tracker described above checks prices from one place. Yet the same hotel, on the same dates, can be offered at different prices depending on which country you search from. Different countries get access to different inventory, channels, local rates and promotions, so searching the same hotel from another country can open up lower prices you cannot get from your own country.

In a test of 1,000 hotels searched from 100 countries, 80% of countries showed a lower price than the traveler's own country, and the typical gap was 20-40%. That kind of difference exists at the same moment, so no amount of waiting for a drop on one listing will find it. The article on Booking.com prices in different countries explains this in detail.

A practical setup for your next booking

  1. Book a refundable rateA tracker or a repeat search is only useful if you can still change the booking.
  2. Search from other countries before you payThe same hotel is often 5-30% cheaper elsewhere, and the gap between countries is usually larger than a drop over time.
  3. Turn on one trackerGoogle's hotel tracking or a post-booking tracker is enough; several alerts for the same stay add noise.
  4. Check identical termsSame hotel, room, inclusions and cancellation terms, compared on the full total.
  5. Confirm before you cancelThe new booking must be confirmed before the original is cancelled.

Frequently asked questions

Can I track a Booking.com hotel price?

Yes. You can record the total for the same search every few days, turn on hotel price tracking in Google Hotels, or use a post-booking tracker that alerts you when the rate drops.

What should I do when a Booking.com price drops after I book?

If your booking has free cancellation, book the identical stay at the lower total, wait for the confirmation, then cancel the original before the deadline.

Why do I see different Booking.com prices when I check again?

Hotel prices change with demand and occupancy, and signed-in Genius members can see discounts at participating properties. Keep the same search and sign-in state each time you check.

Can a price tracker find the lowest price for a hotel?

Not on its own. A tracker watches one listing over time from one place. The same hotel searched from other countries can be lower at the same moment, which a tracker does not check.
