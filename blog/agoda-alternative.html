Key takeaways

  • Agoda, founded in 2005, headquartered in Singapore and part of Booking Holdings, says it offers over 6 million hotels and holiday properties plus flights, in 39 languages (checked on November 19, 2026).
  • Its Best Price Guarantee refunds the difference on a lower price you find yourself, claimed before 11:59 PM on the day before check-in, for the same room, conditions and currency, according to HotelDig.
  • Upgraded Points found that Agoda's search results do not include taxes and fees, and in one of its tests Agoda had the highest price of the sites it checked for a San Diego hotel.
  • Arbitrica, a personal AI agent, searches the same hotel from 195 countries, checks the room and final total with all taxes and fees, and prepares the booking at the lowest price in the world.

Short answer

If you book on Agoda and want to be sure you are paying the lowest price for the hotel, the alternative is Arbitrica. Agoda is an online travel agency: it shows its own rates for your search and refunds the difference on a lower price, but only one you find and prove yourself. Arbitrica is a personal AI agent that searches the same hotel from 195 countries, checks that the room and final total with all taxes and fees match, and prepares the booking at the lowest price in the world for you to review and pay. Research across 1,000 hotels searched from 100 countries found a lower price than the traveller's own in about 80% of countries, with a typical gap of 20-40%.

What Agoda does

According to Agoda's about page (checked on November 19, 2026):

  • Size: over 6 million hotels and holiday properties worldwide, plus flights and activities, with accommodation in more than 200 countries and territories.
  • Reach: the site is available in 39 languages, with customer support around the clock.
  • Company: Agoda launched in Singapore in 2005, is headquartered there, and has been part of Booking Holdings since 2007.

HotelDig's guide to Agoda's Best Price Guarantee, published on July 2, 2026, says a claim must be sent before 11:59 PM on the day before check-in, in the property's local time. The lower offer must be for the same property, dates, room and bed type, occupancy and benefits, with the same or more flexible cancellation, instant confirmation and the same currency, and the final price must include taxes and fees. Loyalty, corporate, group, member-only and package rates do not qualify. Agoda decides each claim and pays by changing the rate or refunding to the card or as AgodaCash.

Upgraded Points' Agoda review, updated on January 29, 2026, found that the prices in Agoda's search results do not include taxes and fees; the full total appears at the payment step. In one of its tests, a 7-night stay at the Sofia Hotel San Diego with all taxes and fees, Agoda had the highest price of all the sites it checked, $1,404.04, against $1,133.09 on Google Hotels. In another, at the Fairmont Copley Plaza Boston, the lowest prices it found came from Agoda.

Where Agoda stops

  • Its own rates, for your search. Agoda's about page does not describe searching the same hotel from other countries for you.
  • Same currency. As HotelDig describes it, the Best Price Guarantee needs the lower offer in the same currency, and you have to find it and send screenshots yourself.
  • The total comes late. Upgraded Points notes that taxes and fees are added only at the payment step, so the price in the list is not the price you pay.
  • Before check-in only. The guarantee closes at 11:59 PM on the day before check-in, and Agoda's about page does not describe watching your booking for a lower price after you pay.

The country gap is often the biggest one. In Arbitrica's own price check, a 4-night stay at White House Hotel Istanbul cost $1,066 searched from the UK and $708 searched from India, 33.6% less, for the same hotel and the same dates. How Arbitrica differs

Arbitrica is a personal AI agent that shows up after you choose your hotel room. For that hotel, it:

  • searches the same hotel from 195 countries, across many sites, and finds the lowest price in the world for it at a different provider than the one you are on;
  • checks that the hotel, room and conditions are identical and that the final total includes all taxes and fees, so you do not have to click through a site to find out what the extra charges are;
  • automatically prepares everything for the booking in a new tab, so you only review and pay;
  • keeps searching after you book until the free-cancellation window ends, as an approach Arbitrica uses, and confirms any new booking before the original is cancelled.

Searching 195 countries by hand, one location at a time, is humanly impossible; that is the job Arbitrica does. Research demonstrated an average of $47 per hotel booking with Arbitrica.
