## What Copilot does when you shop
Copilot started as Microsoft's AI chat assistant. For shopping it now does the following (checked on November 13, 2026):

- **Copilot Checkout:** Microsoft [announced Copilot Checkout on January 8, 2026](https://news.microsoft.com/source/2026/01/08/microsoft-propels-retail-forward-with-agentic-AI-capabilities-that-power-intelligent-automation-for-every-retail-function/), live in the US on Copilot.com. You can buy without being sent to another site, with PayPal, Shopify and Stripe behind the payment, from merchants such as Urban Outfitters, Anthropologie, Ashley Furniture and Etsy sellers. Merchants stay the merchant of record.

- **Mobile and catalog:** on April 22, 2026 [Windows Central reported](https://www.windowscentral.com/microsoft/windows-11/copilots-shopping-upgrade-brings-checkout-to-the-mobile-app-with-deeper-data-from-half-a-million-merchants) that checkout now works in the Copilot mobile app in the US, and that more than 500,000 merchants make up the catalog, according to Microsoft.

- **Product details:** Microsoft's [Shopping with Copilot page](https://support.microsoft.com/en-US/Microsoft-Copilot/shopping-with-microsoft-copilot), updated on October 19, 2026, says Copilot can show sizes, retailers, review summaries, return windows and price history, depending on what the merchant provides. Outside Copilot Checkout, Buy takes you to the retailer's purchase page.

- **In Edge:** Microsoft's [Edge support page](https://support.microsoft.com/en-US/edge/changes-to-shopping-in-edge) says price comparison, price history, price tracking and cash back now sit inside Copilot in Edge, rolling out in the US first.

Copilot can also act on travel sites. When Microsoft launched Copilot Actions in April 2025, [TechCrunch reported](https://techcrunch.com/2025/04/04/microsofts-copilot-can-now-browse-the-web-and-perform-actions-for-you/) day-one partners including Booking, Expedia, Kayak, Priceline, Tripadvisor, Skyscanner and Vrbo, and that Copilot can look for price drops on an item and send you a link to buy.

## How Copilot gets the price
Copilot does not set the price. Microsoft's support page says "Retailers set their own prices and availability and may change them without notice", and asks you to check pricing on the retailer's website. With Copilot Checkout the merchant stays the merchant of record, so you pay the price that merchant shows for your search. Microsoft's announcement and support pages do not describe searching the same product or hotel from other countries (checked on November 13, 2026).

That matters because the same product or hotel is often priced differently depending on where the search comes from. Retailers, hotels and booking sites agree prices, stock and promotions market by market, and dynamic pricing moves them all day. In [Arbitrica's own price check](https://arbitrica.com/blog/hotel-prices-by-country-data.html), a 4-night stay at White House Hotel Istanbul cost $1,066 searched from the UK and $708 searched from India, 33.6% less, for the same hotel and the same dates.

## How Arbitrica handles the price
Arbitrica is a personal AI agent that shows up when you are about to pay. For the product, hotel or event ticket you have chosen, it:

- searches the same item from 195 countries, on the site you are on and other sites;

- checks that it is identical (the same product, or the same hotel and room) and that the final total includes all taxes and fees;

- finds the lowest price in the world for it and automatically prepares everything in a new tab, so you only review and pay;

- keeps searching after you buy or book until the return or free-cancellation window ends, as an approach Arbitrica uses, and confirms any new booking before the original is cancelled.

Doing the same by hand means switching your location country by country and repeating every search. Across 195 countries that is humanly impossible, which is the job Arbitrica does. Research demonstrated an average of $34 per online purchase, $47 per hotel booking and $107 per event ticket purchase with Arbitrica.
