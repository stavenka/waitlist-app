## What Priceline does
[Priceline's Best Price Guarantee](https://img1.priceline.com/static-pages/best-price-guarantee.html) (checked on November 20, 2026) is for Priceline members and covers hotel, flight, car and cruise bookings. If you find a lower price within 24 hours of booking, Priceline refunds the difference. The lower price, including taxes and fees, must be available to the general public in the same currency, with the same form of payment and from the same point of sale, for the same hotel, dates, number of rooms and room type, with a comparable cancellation policy. Corporate, member, loyalty and package rates are excluded. For an Express Deal, Priceline refunds 200% of the difference on a lower price found up to midnight before you travel. You claim by contacting Priceline and telling it where you saw the price.

[Upgraded Points' Priceline guide](https://upgradedpoints.com/travel/priceline-guide/), updated on October 16, 2026, describes Priceline as part of Booking Holdings, with Booking.com, Agoda and Kayak, and says Name Your Own Price no longer exists. Express Deals can save up to 60%, but the hotel name is hidden until you book and bookings are prepaid, non-refundable and non-changeable. Taxes are not included in the rate on the results page or the hotel page; a toggle shows the total with taxes and fees. In its test of a 3-night refundable stay at the Conrad New York Downtown, Priceline was the lowest at $2,099.82, against $2,172.31 at Hilton and $2,216.29 on Booking.

[ShopBack's review](https://www.shopback.com/blog/travel/is-priceline-reliable-us-2026), published on July 30, 2026, calls Priceline primarily a US-market brand whose international inventory is thinner, and points travellers outside the US to Booking and Agoda.

## Where Priceline stops
- **Its own rates, for your search.** Priceline's site does not describe searching the same hotel from other countries for you.

- **Same point of sale.** The guarantee needs the lower price in the same currency and from the same point of sale, so a lower price found from another country does not qualify.

- **24 hours.** For a standard booking, a claim must start within 24 hours of booking, and you have to find the lower price and call it in yourself.

- **The total comes later.** Upgraded Points notes that taxes are not in the rate on the results page; you turn on a toggle or wait for checkout to see the full total.

The country gap is often the biggest one. In [Arbitrica's own price check](https://arbitrica.com/blog/hotel-prices-by-country-data.html), a 4-night stay at White House Hotel Istanbul cost $1,066 searched from the UK and $708 searched from India, 33.6% less, for the same hotel and the same dates. How the other big price guarantees handle this is covered in [how hotel booking price guarantees work](https://arbitrica.com/blog/how-do-hotel-booking-price-guarantees-work.html).

## Frequently asked questions

### What is a good alternative to Priceline?
Arbitrica, if you want the lowest price for the hotel you have chosen: it searches the same hotel from 195 countries and on other sites, checks the room and final total with all taxes and fees, and prepares the booking for you to review and pay.

### How does Priceline's Best Price Guarantee work?
If you find a lower price within 24 hours of booking, for the same hotel, dates and room, in the same currency and from the same point of sale, Priceline refunds the difference. For an Express Deal it refunds 200% of the difference up to midnight before you travel.

### Are Priceline Express Deals refundable?
No. Upgraded Points reports that Express Deals are prepaid, non-refundable, non-changeable and non-transferable, and the hotel name is shown only after you book.

### Does Priceline show prices with taxes?
Upgraded Points reports that taxes are not included in the rate on the results page or the hotel page, and that a toggle above the results shows the total with taxes and fees.

### Who owns Priceline?
Priceline is part of Booking Holdings, the group that also owns Booking.com, Agoda and Kayak.

### Is Priceline always the lowest price?
Not always. It was the lowest in Upgraded Points' Conrad New York Downtown test, at $2,099.82, and Arbitrica's own data shows the same hotel can cost 33.6% less searched from another country.

### Does Arbitrica keep checking the price after I book?
Yes. Its approach repeats the search after you book until the free-cancellation window ends, and a new booking is confirmed before the original one is cancelled.
