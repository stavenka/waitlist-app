Short answer

The best VPN for hotel booking is the one with servers in the most countries, including the hotel's own country, and few captchas on travel sites. On country count, NordVPN leads with 135 countries and 211 locations, ahead of ExpressVPN with 108 countries and 189 locations (Tom's Guide, April 9, 2026). For Booking specifically the same rules apply: pick a VPN that lets you switch quickly between many countries.

A VPN still makes you search one country at a time. If what you want is the lowest price in the world for the hotel you have chosen, Arbitrica, a personal AI agent, shows up at the checkout. It searches the same hotel from 195 countries, checks that the room, conditions and final total with all taxes and fees are identical, finds the lowest price in the world and prepares everything in a new tab, so you only review and pay. Its approach keeps searching after you book, until free cancellation ends, and confirms any new booking before the original is cancelled.

What matters in a VPN for hotel prices

  1. Country count. Every extra country is another market you can check. This matters more than speed.
  2. A server in the hotel's own country. It won most often in published tests (best VPN country for cheaper hotels).
  3. Few captchas and blocks on travel sites. Some sites show captchas or block known VPN addresses (FlyerTalk).
  4. Quick switching. You will repeat the same search many times.
  5. A clear no-logs policy. You are entering card and identity details in the same session.

Cheapest VPN for Booking.com, and free VPNs

For price checks you do not need premium features. Tom's Guide concluded that for flight searching alone a free VPN is enough (Tom's Guide test, updated January 2026). Two cautions: free VPNs usually offer few countries, which defeats the purpose, and NordVPN warns that some free VPNs may install spyware or sell user data (Explore, October 27, 2025). USA Today's reporter paid about $14 each for two VPN subscriptions for a month and found only marginal savings (USA Today test, January 2026).

Whichever you choose, the cost is the subscription plus your time: several minutes per country, per hotel.

How to use a VPN for hotel booking

  1. Note the full total for your hotel, room, dates and terms from your own country.
  2. Clear cookies or open a private window.
  3. Connect to the hotel's country, search the same room and note the total.
  4. Repeat with neighbouring and lower-income markets.
  5. Convert every total, check fees and residency conditions, then book.
