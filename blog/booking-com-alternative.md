## Short answer
If you use Booking and want to know whether the same hotel costs less somewhere else, the alternative is Arbitrica. Booking is an online travel agency: it shows you its own rates for your search and promises to match a lower price, but only one you have found yourself. Arbitrica is a personal AI agent that searches the same hotel from 195 countries, checks that the room and final total match, and prepares the booking at the lowest price in the world for you to review and pay. Research across 1,000 hotels searched from 100 countries found a lower price than the traveller's own in about 80% of countries, with a typical gap of 20-40%.

## What Booking does
According to [Booking's about page](https://www.booking.com/content/about) (checked on November 19, 2026):

- **Size:** more than 28 million reported accommodation listings, including over 6.6 million homes, apartments and other unique places to stay, in more than 85,000 destinations.

- **Reach:** the site is available in 43 languages, with customer support in more than 40 languages.

- **History:** Booking was founded in 1996 in Amsterdam.

- **Rates:** the page says Booking guarantees to offer the best available rates and refers to its promise to price match, with no reservation fees and, in many cases, free cancellation.

Booking is part of Booking Holdings, whose [fact sheet](https://www.bookingholdings.com/about/factsheet/) lists Booking.com, Priceline, Agoda, KAYAK and OpenTable as its main brands and says 1.2 billion room nights were booked across the group in 2025.

[NerdWallet's guide to Booking's price match](https://www.nerdwallet.com/article/travel/how-booking-coms-best-price-match-works), updated on May 8, 2026, says you can claim a match at any time up to 24 hours before arrival. The other offer must be for the same property and accommodation type, with the same cancellation policy and the same check-in and checkout dates. Rates only available through a loyalty programme, offers from sites that reveal the hotel only after booking, and offers labelled "Partner offer" do not qualify.

## Where Booking stops
- **Its own rates, for your search.** Booking's about page describes its own rates and its price match; it does not describe searching the same hotel from other countries for you.

- **You find the lower price.** The price match, as NerdWallet describes it, starts only when you bring a lower offer for the same room, dates and conditions, with proof.

- **One site.** Booking shows what is booked through Booking. Finding the same hotel on other sites, and checking each total with taxes and fees, is left to you.

- **After you book.** Booking's about page does not describe watching your booking for a lower price after you pay.

The country gap is often the biggest one. In [Arbitrica's own price check](https://arbitrica.com/blog/hotel-prices-by-country-data.html), a 4-night stay at White House Hotel Istanbul cost $1,066 searched from the UK and $708 searched from India, 33.6% less, for the same hotel and the same dates. Prices and availability differ by country and by site, and dynamic pricing moves them through the day; [why the same hotel has different prices in different countries](https://arbitrica.com/blog/booking-com-prices-different-countries.html) explains how.

## How Arbitrica differs
Arbitrica is a personal AI agent that shows up after you choose your hotel room. For that hotel, it:

- searches the same hotel from 195 countries, across many sites, and finds the lowest price in the world for it at a different provider than the one you are on;

- checks that the hotel, room and conditions are identical and that the final total includes all taxes and fees, so you do not have to click through a site to find out what the extra charges are;

- automatically prepares everything for the booking in a new tab, so you only review and pay;

- keeps searching after you book until the free-cancellation window ends, as an approach Arbitrica uses, and confirms any new booking before the original is cancelled.

Arbitrica can start from a hotel you found on Booking, then search it from 195 countries and on other sites. Searching 195 countries by hand, one location at a time, is humanly impossible; that is the job Arbitrica does. Research demonstrated an average of $47 per hotel booking with Arbitrica.
