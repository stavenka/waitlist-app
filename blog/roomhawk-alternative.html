RoomHawk alternative that also checks the price from 195 countries

RoomHawk tracks the hotel you have chosen and sends an alert when the price drops, so you can cancel and rebook. Its pages do not describe searching the same hotel from other countries. Here is what each one does and when to use which.

Key takeaways

  • RoomHawk calls itself a hotel rebooking service and a hotel price tracker that searches and tracks over 2M rooms; its site says it is in beta (checked on November 22, 2026).
  • It works in three steps: set a Hawk for your stay, get an alert with a link when the price drops, then cancel your booking and rebook at the lower rate yourself.
  • On November 22, 2026 RoomHawk's Trustpilot profile showed 4.6 from 23 reviews; one reviewer notes you need to know which hotel you want to track.
  • Arbitrica, a personal AI agent, searches the same hotel from 195 countries before you pay, checks the room and final total, prepares the booking at the lowest price in the world, and keeps searching after you book until free cancellation ends.

Short answer

If you like RoomHawk's price drop alerts but want the price from other countries too, and the booking prepared for you, the alternative is Arbitrica. RoomHawk tracks a hotel stay you have chosen or booked and sends you an alert when the price drops; you then cancel and rebook at the lower rate yourself. Arbitrica is a personal AI agent that searches the same hotel from 195 countries, on the site you are booking and other sites, checks that the room and final total match, and prepares the booking at the lowest price in the world for you to review and pay. Research across 1,000 hotels searched from 100 countries found a lower price than the traveller's own in about 80% of countries, with a typical gap of 20-40%.

What RoomHawk does

According to RoomHawk's site, its about page and its how it works page (checked on November 22, 2026; roomhawk.app now redirects to roomhawk.com):

  • Search and tracking: it describes itself as an AI hotel price tracker to search and track over 2M rooms, using prices from verified sources online.
  • After you book: the about page calls RoomHawk a hotel rebooking service and says the gap it fills is tracking price drops after a booking was made.
  • How it works: you set a Hawk with your travel details and existing reservation, RoomHawk sends an alert with a link when it detects a lower price, and you cancel your booking and rebook at the lower rate.
  • Savings claim: "Save 5-30% On Every Trip", according to its homepage.
  • Status: the about page says RoomHawk is currently in beta testing. Its co-founders are Ian Dollamore and Samuel Olsson.

On Trustpilot (checked on November 22, 2026) RoomHawk lists a London address and has a TrustScore of 4.6 from 23 reviews, all 5-star, 7 of them in the last 12 months. Trustpilot notes that the company has not sent recent review invitations. Reviewers like the price drop notifications; one reports a Barcelona hotel that dropped by £19 within a day of tracking, and another notes that you need to know which hotel you want to track. For a longer side-by-side, see Arbitrica vs. RoomHawk.

Where RoomHawk stops

  • Your own country. RoomHawk's pages do not describe searching the same hotel from other countries (checked on November 22, 2026); it tracks the price for your search.
  • You do the rebooking. Its about page describes manual rebooking: you click the alert, cancel the old booking and book the new rate yourself.
  • Sites not named. RoomHawk's pages refer to verified sources and top booking sites without naming which ones.
  • Beta. The about page says the service is still in beta testing.

The gap between countries is often the biggest one. In Arbitrica's own price check, a 4-night stay at White House Hotel Istanbul cost $1,066 searched from the UK and $708 searched from India, 33.6% less, for the same hotel and the same dates. Prices and room availability differ by country and by site, and dynamic pricing moves them through the day.

How Arbitrica differs

Arbitrica is a personal AI agent that shows up after you choose your hotel room. For the hotel you have chosen, it:

  • searches the same hotel, on the same dates, from 195 countries, on the site you are booking and other sites;
  • checks that the hotel, room and conditions are identical and that the final total includes all taxes and fees;
  • finds the lowest price in the world for it and automatically prepares everything for the booking in a new tab, so you only review and pay;
  • keeps searching after you book until the free-cancellation window ends, as an approach Arbitrica uses, and confirms any new booking before the original is cancelled.

Price drops after booking often have to be acted on at once, before someone else takes the room at that rate, which is why a personal agent that acts instantly helps. Searching 195 countries by hand is humanly impossible; that is the job Arbitrica does. Research demonstrated an average of $47 per hotel booking with Arbitrica.

Which one to use

  • Use RoomHawk if you already know the hotel, have a refundable booking and are happy to cancel and rebook yourself when an alert arrives.
  • Use Arbitrica when you want the lowest price in the world for the hotel you have chosen, searched from 195 countries before you pay, the booking prepared for you, and a watch after you book until free cancellation ends.
