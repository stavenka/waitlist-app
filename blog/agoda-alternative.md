## What Agoda does
According to [Agoda's about page](https://www.agoda.com/about-agoda/) (checked on November 19, 2026):

- **Size:** over 6 million hotels and holiday properties worldwide, plus flights and activities, with accommodation in more than 200 countries and territories.

- **Reach:** the site is available in 39 languages, with customer support around the clock.

- **Company:** Agoda launched in Singapore in 2005, is headquartered there, and has been part of Booking Holdings since 2007.

[HotelDig's guide to Agoda's Best Price Guarantee](https://www.hoteldig.com/en/agoda-best-price-guarantee/), published on July 2, 2026, says a claim must be sent before 11:59 PM on the day before check-in, in the property's local time. The lower offer must be for the same property, dates, room and bed type, occupancy and benefits, with the same or more flexible cancellation, instant confirmation and the same currency, and the final price must include taxes and fees. Loyalty, corporate, group, member-only and package rates do not qualify. Agoda decides each claim and pays by changing the rate or refunding to the card or as AgodaCash.

[Upgraded Points' Agoda review](https://upgradedpoints.com/travel/agoda/), updated on January 29, 2026, found that the prices in Agoda's search results do not include taxes and fees; the full total appears at the payment step. In one of its tests, a 7-night stay at the Sofia Hotel San Diego with all taxes and fees, Agoda had the highest price of all the sites it checked, $1,404.04, against $1,133.09 on Google Hotels. In another, at the Fairmont Copley Plaza Boston, the lowest prices it found came from Agoda.

## Where Agoda stops
- **Its own rates, for your search.** Agoda's about page does not describe searching the same hotel from other countries for you.

- **Same currency.** As HotelDig describes it, the Best Price Guarantee needs the lower offer in the same currency, and you have to find it and send screenshots yourself.

- **The total comes late.** Upgraded Points notes that taxes and fees are added only at the payment step, so the price in the list is not the price you pay.

- **Before check-in only.** The guarantee closes at 11:59 PM on the day before check-in, and Agoda's about page does not describe watching your booking for a lower price after you pay.

The country gap is often the biggest one. In [Arbitrica's own price check](https://arbitrica.com/blog/hotel-prices-by-country-data.html), a 4-night stay at White House Hotel Istanbul cost $1,066 searched from the UK and $708 searched from India, 33.6% less, for the same hotel and the same dates. Why changing your location on Agoda by hand is slow and patchy is covered in [Agoda VPN: is Agoda cheaper from another country?](https://arbitrica.com/blog/agoda-vpn.html).
