# Is it cheaper to book a hotel direct or through Booking or Expedia?

Updated 2026-11-04 by Arbitrica. Canonical: https://arbitrica.com/blog/book-hotel-direct-or-booking-expedia.html

## Key takeaways
- Neither the hotel's own website nor booking sites like Booking, Expedia and Agoda are always lower; it changes by hotel, date and country.
- In Arbitrica's checks, the hotel's own website was higher than the booking-site price for 2 of 4 hotels and lower for the other 2.
- Booking sites are often lower because they pay for member discounts themselves (Booking Genius 10-20%, Expedia Member Prices 10% or more, AgodaVIP up to 25%) or sell app-only, package or wholesale rates.
- Compare the final total with the same room and cancellation conditions, and check the same room from other countries.

## Short answer
Neither is always lower. The same room can carry a lower total on the hotel's own website one day and on Booking, Expedia or Agoda the next, and the country you search from changes the answer again. The only reliable way to know is to check the same room, with the same conditions, on both, and from more than one country. ## When booking direct is often lower
- Member rates for signed-in loyalty members.
- No commission, so some hotels offer their best price or extras directly.
- EU Court of Justice case C-264/23 (September 2024): wide and narrow price-parity clauses fall under EU competition rules, giving hotels more room to price their own site lower.
- EU Digital Markets Act: Booking removed contractual parity requirements in the European Economic Area in July 2024 and has had to comply with all relevant obligations since 14 November 2024, so hotels there can set prices freely on and off Booking, including a lower price on their own website (European Commission factsheet, 28 September 2026; checked November 4, 2026).

## Why is Agoda or Booking cheaper than the hotel's own website?
Usually because the booking site, not the hotel, is paying for the discount, or because the lower price is a different product: a member, app, package or wholesale rate, a rate with different conditions, a rate for another country, or a total shown without taxes. The hotel's own rate has often not changed at all.

### Can a hotel in Europe sell its rooms for less than on Booking?

Yes. Under the EU Digital Markets Act, Booking removed contractual parity requirements in the European Economic Area in July 2024 and has had to comply with all relevant obligations since 14 November 2024. According to the European Commission, hotels there can set their prices freely on and off Booking, including a lower price on their own website.

### Will a hotel match a booking site's price if I book direct?

Some hotels and chains match or beat a lower public price for direct bookings, especially for loyalty members. Ask the hotel before booking and have the booking site's final total ready.

### Does the country I search from change the price?

Often. Booking sites sell rates agreed for specific markets, so the same room can carry a different total from another country. In Arbitrica's checks, 4 of 10 hotels had a lower total from another country than from the UK.
