# Is it cheaper to book a hotel direct or through Booking or Expedia?

Updated 2026-11-01 by Arbitrica. Canonical: https://arbitrica.com/blog/book-hotel-direct-or-booking-expedia.html

## Key takeaways
- Neither the hotel's own website nor booking sites like Booking, Expedia and Agoda are always lower; it changes by hotel, date and country.
- In Arbitrica's checks, the hotel's own website was higher than the booking-site price for 2 of 4 hotels and lower for the other 2.
- Compare the final total with the same room and cancellation conditions, and check the same room from other countries.

## Short answer
Neither is always lower. The same room can carry a lower total on the hotel's own website one day and on Booking, Expedia or Agoda the next, and the country you search from changes the answer again. Check the same room, with the same conditions, on both, and from more than one country.

## When booking direct is often lower
- Member rates for signed-in loyalty members.
- No commission, so some hotels offer their best price or extras directly.
- EU Court of Justice case C-264/23 (September 2024): wide and narrow price-parity clauses fall under EU competition rules, giving hotels more room to price their own site lower.

## Why is Agoda or Booking cheaper than the hotel's own website?
- The booking site pays for the discount (loyalty programmes, promotions).
- A different rate (non-refundable, room-only, different room type).
- A different market (rates agreed for specific countries).
- Taxes and fees shown differently.

## How to check before you book
1. Compare the final total with taxes and fees.
2. Match room type, meal plan, guests and cancellation policy.
3. Check the hotel's site as a member and at least two booking sites.
4. Check the same room from other countries.
5. Book with free cancellation and keep checking until the deadline.

## Frequently asked questions

### Why is Booking.com cheaper than the hotel?

When it is, it is usually because Booking.com funds a discount such as a Genius rate, the lower price is a different rate (for example non-refundable or room-only), it is a rate for a different market, or the two sites show taxes and fees differently. It is not always lower: in Arbitrica's checks the hotel's own website was lower for 2 of 4 hotels.

### Is it cheaper to book a hotel direct or through Booking or Expedia?

Neither is always lower. In Arbitrica's checks the hotel's own website was higher than the booking-site price for 2 of 4 hotels and lower for the other 2. Compare the final total with the same conditions, and check other countries too.

### Why is Agoda or Booking cheaper than the hotel's own website?

Usually because the booking site funds a discount, the lower price is a different rate (for example non-refundable or room-only), it is a rate for a different market, or taxes and fees are shown differently.

### Will a hotel match a booking site's price if I book direct?

Some hotels and chains match or beat a lower public price for direct bookings, especially for loyalty members. Ask the hotel before booking and have the booking site's final total ready.

### Does the country I search from change the price?

Often. Booking sites sell rates agreed for specific markets, so the same room can carry a different total from another country. In Arbitrica's checks, 4 of 10 hotels had a lower total from another country than from the UK.
