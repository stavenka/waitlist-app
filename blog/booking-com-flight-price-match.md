## Does Booking.com price match flights?

Booking.com's customer terms (version dated August 25, 2025, checked November 2, 2026) place "We Price Match" in the section on accommodation: if you find the same accommodation with the same conditions for less on another website after booking, Booking.com refunds the difference. The promise does not mention flights. Independent guides read it the same way: NerdWallet (updated May 2026) says the guarantee applies to accommodation bookings only, and Ajust (updated April 2026) adds that flights, car rentals and bundled flight-plus-hotel offers are excluded.

For a stay, the usual rules apply: the lower price has to be public and for the same property, room, dates and cancellation policy, and guides report that claims are accepted until 24 hours before check-in. The full process is in [Booking.com price match: how it works](https://arbitrica.com/blog/booking-com-price-match.html).

## Who sells you a flight on Booking.com?

Booking.com's terms say that most flights on its platform are provided through a third-party aggregator that acts as an intermediary to the airline, that Booking.com acts as the platform, and that when you book, your contract is directly with the airline. In practice that means three things:

- The fare rules of the ticket decide whether you can cancel, change or get money back, not a Booking.com promise.

- A price drop on the same flight after you book is only worth something if your fare can be cancelled or changed without a fee that eats the gap.

- The same flight can be sold with different conditions on different sites, so read the fare conditions and baggage rules at checkout.

## Does Skyscanner price match flights?

Skyscanner is a search engine: it shows fares from airlines and travel agencies and sends you to one of them to book. In October 2026 it ran a limited-time "best price guarantee". These were the conditions on its own page, dated October 2, 2026:

- Bookings completed between October 5 and October 16, 2026 (UK time), by residents of the UK, US, Canada or India aged 18 or over.

- Only bookings where Skyscanner showed the guarantee before redirecting you; some offers also required being signed in.

- A claim within 24 hours of the booking confirmation, with a screenshot and link to the same flight at a lower public price in the same currency.

- The same airline, flight numbers, times, fare class and conditions, with no account, membership, app or promo code needed for the lower fare.

- Refunds capped at USD 270, GBP 200, CAD 375 or INR 25,500 per booking, with a minimum difference of USD 7 or GBP 5. Skyscanner said it reviews claims within 15 working days.

Skyscanner kept the right to pause or withdraw the guarantee in any market at any time, so check whether it is shown on your search before relying on it.

## Found a lower fare after booking? What to do

1. Check the fare rules first. If the ticket is non-refundable and changes carry a fee, the lower fare may not be worth chasing.

2. Use any free cancellation window. In the US, the Department of Transportation requires airlines to let you hold a reservation or cancel it without penalty within 24 hours of booking when you book at least seven days before departure. Ask the seller how that applies if you bought through a travel agency.

3. Book the lower fare first, then cancel the original, so you are never left without a seat.

4. Count the whole total. Bags, seat selection and payment fees can turn a lower headline fare into a higher one.

## Frequently asked questions

### Does Booking.com price match flights?

No. Booking.com's "We Price Match" promise covers accommodation only. Flights, car rentals and flight-plus-hotel bundles are excluded, so a lower fare on another site cannot be claimed from Booking.com.

### Does Booking.com price match Skyscanner?

Not for flights. For a stay, a lower public price for the same property, room, dates and conditions on another website can be claimed, including a rate you reach through Skyscanner's hotel search. For flights there is no Booking.com price match at all.

### Does Skyscanner price match flights?

Only through a limited-time best price guarantee: flights booked October 5 to 16, 2026 by residents of the UK, US, Canada or India, where the guarantee was shown before the redirect, claimed within 24 hours and capped at USD 270 or GBP 200 per booking.

### Who is my contract with when I book a flight on Booking.com?

With the airline. Booking.com's terms say most flights on its platform come through a third-party aggregator acting as an intermediary to the airline, and that Booking.com acts as the platform. The ticket's fare rules decide refunds and changes.

### Can I get money back if my flight price drops after I book?

Only if your fare allows it. With a refundable or freely changeable fare, book the lower fare and then cancel the original. In the US, airlines must allow a penalty-free cancellation within 24 hours of booking when you book at least seven days before departure.
