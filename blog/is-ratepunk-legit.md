## Who makes RatePunk
On [its own review page](https://www.ratepunk.com/blog/ratepunk-review-is-it-a-scam-or-legit), updated on November 11, 2026, RatePunk describes itself as a real company based in Vilnius, Lithuania, founded in 2020, with a team of 30+ and more than 250,000 paying members. [Its homepage](https://www.ratepunk.com/) now leads with flights: "Save Up to 90% on Flights", with a service that scans flight prices around the clock and sends deal alerts. The flight membership costs $23.99 for the first year and renews at $104 a year, with a 14-day money-back guarantee on the first purchase, according to its review page (checked on November 22, 2026).

The hotel tool is listed in the browser extension store as [RatePunk Hotel Booking Deals](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/gdaioanblcnghddimngklkhgcbomfdck). On November 22, 2026 the listing showed:

- **Publisher:** UAB Yard Venture, Vilnius, Lithuania.

- **Users:** 50,000.

- **Rating:** 3.7 out of 5 from 133 ratings.

- **Version:** 1.49.8, updated on April 21, 2026.

- **Features named:** the best price from other booking sites, price drop alerts after booking and cashback on stays.

- **How it earns:** the listing says RatePunk earns a commission when you book hotels through the extension.

- **Data:** it lists website content as the data it handles, and the developer declares that the data is not sold to third parties outside approved use cases.

## What users and reviewers say
On [Trustpilot](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/ratepunk.com) (checked on November 22, 2026) RatePunk has a TrustScore of 4.2 from 7,890 reviews: 57% 5-star, 26% 4-star, 6% 3-star, 2% 2-star and 9% 1-star. RatePunk describes itself there as scanning 100+ providers around the clock and says it replies to 99% of negative reviews.

- **Praise:** an easy app, useful alerts and real discounts.

- **Complaints:** prices that change when you go to book, fixed dates that are inconvenient, and renewals or extra charges without clear notice. A 1-star review of October 29, 2026 describes unexpected Premium and Bundle memberships of about $176 CAD and a partial refund.

[A guest review on Resident](https://resident.com/resource-guide/2025/11/13/ratepunk-review-the-flight-deal-searcher-that-actually-saved-me-money), published on November 13, 2025 (the site notes it may be compensated by partners), used RatePunk for about three months: roughly 40% of 40-50 alerts were useful, with estimated savings of $380-400, and alerts worked less well for flexible, mid-range dates.

So the tool is real and the savings people report are real. The thing to watch is the membership: check what you are signing up for and when it renews.

## Where RatePunk stops
- **Other sites, your own country.** The hotel listing describes finding the best price across booking sites. It does not describe searching the same hotel from 195 countries.

- **You finish the job.** RatePunk shows the offer; you go to that site and book it yourself.

- **The final total.** Some Trustpilot reviewers say the price changed when they went to book, so the total still needs checking.

- **Hotel tool updates.** The hotel extension was last updated on April 21, 2026, while RatePunk's homepage now centres on flights.

The gap between countries is often the biggest one. In [Arbitrica's own price check](https://arbitrica.com/blog/hotel-prices-by-country-data.html), a 4-night stay at White House Hotel Istanbul cost $1,066 searched from the UK and $708 searched from India, 33.6% less, for the same hotel and the same dates. Prices and room availability differ by country and by site, and dynamic pricing moves them through the day.
