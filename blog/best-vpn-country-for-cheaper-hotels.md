## Short answer
The best VPN country for a lower hotel price is usually the hotel's own country, then a mix of others that change with every hotel and date. Published tests found winners in Indonesia, Italy, Greece, Georgia, the Netherlands and Taiwan, and VPN companies suggest Turkey, Poland, Croatia, Moldova and Japan. None wins every time, which is why the most reliable answer is to check far more countries than a VPN lets you.

## Countries worth trying, and why
- **The hotel's own country.** Local rates and promotions are set for that market. It won four of the six tests above.
- **Neighbouring countries in the same region.** Regional sites and channels often carry their own allotments; Malaysia vs Singapore showed 10-18% on Agoda in [NordVPN Southeast Asia study, May 2025](https://nordvpn.com/zh/blog/genggai-ip-dizhi-jiesheng-feiyong/).
- **Lower-income markets.** India beat the UK by 33.6% in Arbitrica's Istanbul check. NordVPN's CTO suggested Turkey, Poland, Croatia and Moldova ([USA Today test, January 2026](https://www.yahoo.com/lifestyle/articles/tried-using-vpn-score-cheaper-100134258.html)).
- **Avoid assuming the US or Switzerland.** Both showed some of the highest totals in the tests, and a VPNpro writer quoted by [Explore, October 27, 2025](https://www.explore.com/2004000/does-using-vpn-book-airbnb-prevent-unnecessary-fees-price-increases) found a US address usually gave her the highest price.

Whatever you try, convert the full total including taxes and fees, and check whether the rate needs local residency. ## Frequently asked questions

### What is the cheapest country to book hotels from with a VPN?
There is no fixed answer. In published tests the lowest prices came from the hotel's own country most often, and from Taiwan, Georgia and others for specific hotels. In Arbitrica's check, India beat the UK by 33.6% for an Istanbul hotel.

### Is the hotel's own country always the lowest?
No. It won in four of six published tests, but Taiwan beat Switzerland for a Prague hotel, and other countries win for other hotels and dates.

### Why is the US often the most expensive country to search from?
Hotels and booking sites set rates by market, and markets with higher willingness to pay tend to see higher prices. In the tests, US and Swiss searches often showed the highest totals.

### How many countries should I try with a VPN?
The published tests tried at most 10, because each search takes several minutes. Arbitrica searches 195 countries at once at the checkout.

### Can I use a VPN country for a hotel rate meant for residents?
Some local rates require proof of residency, such as a local address or ID, at booking or check-in. Read the rate conditions before you book.
