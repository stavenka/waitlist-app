## Key takeaways
- ChatGPT books hotels through partner apps: Booking and Expedia since October 2025, with Hyatt, Accor, Wyndham and Hilton announced in April 2026.
- Since March 24, 2026, ChatGPT purchases finish in the partner's own checkout, so the price is the one that partner shows for your search.
- ChatGPT does not search the same hotel from other countries before you book.

## Short answer
Not on its own. ChatGPT can recommend a hotel and hand you to Booking, Expedia or a hotel chain's app to book it, at the price that app shows for your search. It does not search the same hotel from other countries. The alternative is to let ChatGPT help you choose, then let Arbitrica, a personal AI agent, find the lowest price in the world for that hotel at the checkout: it searches the same hotel from 195 countries, checks the room and final total, and prepares the booking for you. Research across 1,000 hotels searched from 100 countries found a lower price than the traveller's own in about 80% of countries, with a typical gap of 20-40%.

## How ChatGPT books hotels
- **Apps:** Booking and Expedia apps arrived in ChatGPT on October 6, 2025; Hyatt, Accor, Wyndham and Hilton were announced on April 28, 2026 ([Conduit, 2026](https://www.conduit.ai/blog/how-ai-agents-book-hotels)).

- **Checkout:** ChatGPT's Instant Checkout launched in September 2025 and was replaced on March 24, 2026. Purchases now finish in the merchant's own checkout, in an in-app browser on mobile or a link on desktop ([Sanbi tracker, October 23, 2026](https://sanbi.ai/blog/agentic-commerce-tracker-ai-checkout)).

So the price you pay is the partner's price for your dates, location and currency, plus any member deal you have with that partner.

## What ChatGPT misses on price
The same hotel is often priced differently depending on the country the search comes from, because hotels and booking sites agree rates, room allotments and promotions market by market. A single app searched from your location cannot show that.

In [Arbitrica's own price check](https://arbitrica.com/blog/hotel-prices-by-country-data.html), 4 of 10 hotels had a lower total from another country than from the UK, by up to 33.6%: White House Hotel Istanbul was $1,066 from the UK and $708 from India for the same 4 nights. Booking all 10 hotels at their lowest location would have cost $7,349 instead of $8,028.
