Alternative to Meta's Muse for finding the best hotel price

Muse plans and books for you. It does not search the same hotel from other countries. Here is what Muse does with the price, and the agent to use when the price is the point.

Key takeaways

  • Muse, Meta's personal AI agent, launched in the US on September 8, 2026 and in Singapore on October 28, 2026. It books hotels through connected partners such as Expedia, Hotels.com and Agoda.
  • Muse checks out for you once you approve the total, paying with a single-use Stripe Link card.
  • Meta has not said that Muse searches the same hotel from other countries, so the price you get is the one its booking partner shows in your market.
  • Arbitrica is a personal AI agent built around the price: it searches the same hotel from 195 countries, finds the lowest price in the world and prepares the booking for you to review and pay.

Short answer

If you want the lowest price for a hotel, the alternative to Muse is Arbitrica. Muse is a general personal agent: it plans a trip, picks a hotel and books it through a connected partner such as Expedia, Hotels.com or Agoda, at the price that partner gives you. Arbitrica is a personal AI agent that does one thing Muse does not describe doing: it searches the same hotel from 195 countries, checks that the room and the final total match, and prepares the booking at the lowest price in the world. Research across 1,000 hotels searched from 100 countries found a lower price than the traveller's own in about 80% of countries, with a typical gap of 20-40%.

What Muse is

Muse is Meta's personal AI agent. It carries out tasks for you rather than just answering questions. It launched in the US on September 8, 2026 and in Singapore on October 28, 2026, the day Agoda announced its Muse connector for Singapore and Japan.

For hotels, Muse works with partners:

  • US: Expedia and Hotels.com, which Muse first used through a browser (Conduit, 2026).
  • Singapore and Japan: a direct connector to Agoda's own AI travel assistant, plus Trip.com, Singapore Airlines and Klook as early Singapore partners (Skift, October 28, 2026).

At checkout, Muse completes the purchase after you approve the total in the chat, using a single-use card from Stripe Link where needed (Sanbi agentic commerce tracker, October 23, 2026). Amazon has blocked Muse and other outside agents from its store since September 20, 2026, according to the same tracker.

How Muse gets a hotel price

Muse does not set hotel prices. It books through a partner, so the price you see is the price that partner shows for your search: your dates, your country, your currency and the partner's own deals. Meta and its partners have not published any feature that searches the same hotel from other countries or across every booking site before Muse books (checked on November 8, 2026).

That matters because the same hotel is often priced differently depending on where the search comes from. Hotels and booking sites agree rates, room allotments and promotions market by market, and dynamic pricing moves them all day. In Arbitrica's own price check, a 4-night stay at White House Hotel Istanbul cost $1,066 searched from the UK and $708 searched from India, 33.6% less, for the same hotel and the same dates.

How Arbitrica handles the price

Arbitrica is a personal AI agent that shows up when you are about to pay. For the hotel you have chosen, it:

  • searches the same hotel from 195 countries, on the site you are booking and other sites;
  • checks that the hotel, room and conditions are identical and that the final total includes all taxes and fees;
  • finds the lowest price in the world for it and automatically prepares everything for the booking in a new tab, so you only review and pay;
  • keeps searching after you book until the free-cancellation window ends, as an approach Arbitrica uses, and confirms any new booking before the original is cancelled.

Doing the same by hand means switching your location country by country with a VPN and repeating every search. Across 195 countries that is humanly impossible, which is the job Arbitrica does.

Which one to use

  • Use Muse if you want an agent to plan the trip, shortlist hotels and handle the booking inside Meta's apps, and the partner's price is fine.
  • Use Arbitrica when the hotel is decided and you want the lowest price in the world for it, before you pay and after you book.
