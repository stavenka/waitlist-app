Booking.com Price Match: How It Works (and the Better Alternative)

The full guide to Booking.com’s We Price Match guarantee, why many claims fail, and what to use instead.

Short version: Booking.com’s price match guarantee is real but narrow. It covers a lower price you find yourself on another public site, for a booking with exactly the same conditions, and you have to file a claim. Does Booking.com have a price match guarantee?

Short answer: Yes. Booking.com’s “We Price Match” promise says that if you find the same booking cheaper on another website, Booking.com will refund the difference. The lower price has to be for the same hotel and the same dates. It also has to be for the same room type, with the same cancellation and booking conditions, and it has to be publicly bookable. Claims go through Booking.com customer service and must be made within the deadline in Booking.com’s terms, so check the current terms for your reservation.

How do I claim Booking.com’s price match?

Short answer: Find the lower total price on a public site, document it, and send it to Booking.com customer service before the deadline.

  1. Find the lower price on a public site. It has to be bookable, not just listed.
  2. Confirm it is the same hotel on the same dates. The room type, cancellation policy and extras such as breakfast have to match too.
  3. Take a screenshot showing the URL, the date and the final total with taxes and fees.
  4. Contact Booking.com customer service from your reservation or the help center, with your booking number, the link to the lower price and the screenshot.
  5. Wait for the review. If the claim is approved, Booking.com refunds the difference.

Why are many Booking.com price match claims rejected?

Short answer: Booking.com’s definition of “the same booking” is stricter than most travelers expect. The usual reasons:

  • The cheaper rate has a different cancellation policy.
  • It is the same hotel, but the room type, board or occupancy does not match exactly.
  • The cheaper price is a member, loyalty, app-only or coupon price, which does not count as public.
  • The claim is filed after the deadline.
  • The price changed by the time Booking.com checked it.
  • The two totals are not comparable because taxes and fees are shown differently.

Why the price match misses prices from other countries

Short answer: The guarantee only works on a lower price you have already found and documented, and most travelers only search from their own country. It does nothing to help you find the inventory, local rates and promotions that open up when the same hotel is searched from another country, and that is often where the bigger gaps are. What if I have already booked on Booking.com and the price dropped?

Short answer: If the lower price is on another public site and you are within the claim deadline, file the price match claim. If your rate has free cancellation, you can also book the lower price and cancel the original before the cancellation deadline. Book the new one first and cancel second, so you are never left without a hotel.

This is the idea behind multiple-time arbitrage. Arbitrica’s approach repeats the search after you book, until the free-cancellation window ends. Common questions about Booking.com price match

Does Booking.com price match work for non-refundable bookings?

The cheaper offer has to have the same conditions as your booking. A non-refundable booking can only be matched against an offer with the same non-refundable conditions. Check Booking.com’s current terms for your reservation.

What sites count as “public” for a price match claim?

A booking site that anyone can reach without a login, member account or coupon code. Loyalty rates, member-only prices and coupon-gated prices do not qualify. Other major booking sites such as Expedia, Hotels.com and Agoda do.

Does the price match help me find prices from other countries?

No. The guarantee depends on a lower price you find and document yourself. Arbitrica works the other way around: it searches the same hotel from 195 countries before you pay, so you book the lower price from the start and there is nothing to claim.
