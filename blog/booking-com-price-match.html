Does Booking.com have a price match guarantee?

Yes. Booking.com's "We Price Match" promise says that if you find the same booking cheaper on another website, Booking.com will refund the difference. The lower price has to be for the same hotel and the same dates. It also has to be for the same room type, with the same cancellation and booking conditions, and it has to be publicly bookable. Claims go through Booking.com customer service and must be made within the deadline in Booking.com's terms, so check the current terms for your reservation.

How to claim Booking.com's price match

Find the lower total price on a public site, document it, and send it to Booking.com customer service before the deadline.

  1. Find the lower price on a public siteIt has to be bookable, not just listed.
  2. Confirm it is the same bookingSame hotel on the same dates. The room type, cancellation policy and extras such as breakfast have to match too.
  3. Take a screenshotShow the URL, the date and the final total with taxes and fees.
  4. Contact customer serviceUse your reservation or the help center, and include your booking number, the link to the lower price and the screenshot.
  5. Wait for the reviewIf the claim is approved, Booking.com refunds the difference.

Why many Booking.com price match claims are rejected

Booking.com's definition of "the same booking" is stricter than most travelers expect. The usual reasons a claim fails:

  • The cheaper rate has a different cancellation policy.
  • It is the same hotel, but the room type, board or occupancy does not match exactly.
  • The cheaper price is a member, loyalty, app-only or coupon price, which does not count as public.
  • The claim is filed after the deadline.
  • The price changed by the time Booking.com checked it.
  • The two totals are not comparable because taxes and fees are shown differently.
Tip

Capture the lower price with taxes and fees visible on the final step before payment. A screenshot of a search results page rarely proves the total.

Why the price match misses prices from other countries

The guarantee only works on a lower price you have already found and documented, and most travelers only search from their own country. It does nothing to help you find the inventory, local rates and promotions that open up when the same hotel is searched from another country, and that is often where the bigger gaps are. What if you have already booked and the price dropped?

If the lower price is on another public site and you are within the claim deadline, file the price match claim. If your rate has free cancellation, you can also book the lower price and cancel the original before the cancellation deadline. Book the new one first and cancel second, so you are never left without a hotel.

This is the idea behind multiple-time arbitrage. Arbitrica's approach repeats the search after you book, until the free-cancellation window ends. When it finds the identical hotel for less, the mechanism prepares a re-booking at the lower price, and the new booking is confirmed before the original is cancelled. Price match or search first: a quick decision guide

  • You have not paid yet: search the same hotel from other countries first. There is nothing to claim if you book the lower price from the start.
  • You already booked and found a lower public price with identical terms: file the price match claim before the deadline.
  • You booked a refundable rate and the price fell anywhere: rebook at the lower price, and cancel only after the new booking is confirmed.
  • You booked a non-refundable rate: the price match is your only route, and only against an offer with the same non-refundable conditions.

Frequently asked questions

Does Booking.com price match work for non-refundable bookings?

The cheaper offer has to have the same conditions as your booking. A non-refundable booking can only be matched against an offer with the same non-refundable conditions. Check Booking.com's current terms for your reservation.

What sites count as "public" for a price match claim?

A booking site that anyone can reach without a login, member account or coupon code. Loyalty rates, member-only prices and coupon-gated prices do not qualify. Other major booking sites such as Expedia, Hotels.com and Agoda do.

Does the price match help me find prices from other countries?

No. The guarantee depends on a lower price you find and document yourself. Arbitrica works the other way around: it searches the same hotel from 195 countries before you pay, so you book the lower price from the start and there is nothing to claim.
