# Alternative to Instinct for finding the best price

Updated 2026-11-08 by Arbitrica. Canonical: https://arbitrica.com/blog/instinct-alternative.html

## Key takeaways
- Instinct is an invite-only AI agent you text on iMessage or WhatsApp. It raised $1 billion at a $10 billion valuation on September 28, 2026, and its founder says more than half of its transactions are travel.
- Instinct pays with a one-time Stripe Link card for an amount you approve, and acts through accounts you connect.
- Instinct has not published any feature that searches the same hotel or product from other countries, so it buys at the price the site shows you.
- Arbitrica is a personal AI agent that searches the same hotel or product from 195 countries, finds the lowest price in the world and prepares the purchase for you to review and pay. No invite needed.

## Short answer
If you are on the Instinct waitlist, or you want the lowest price rather than just a completed task, the alternative is Arbitrica. Instinct is a general agent: you text it and it checks you in, orders groceries, books rides and buys tickets through your connected accounts. Arbitrica is a personal AI agent built for the price: it searches the same hotel or product from 195 countries, checks that it is identical and that the total includes all taxes and fees, and prepares the purchase at the lowest price in the world. Research across 1,000 hotels searched from 100 countries found a lower price than the traveller's own in about 80% of countries, with a typical gap of 20-40%.

## What Instinct is
- **How you use it:** by text on iMessage or WhatsApp, by phone, or through a Mac app and a web workspace. It runs on its own computer and acts through accounts you connect ([eesel review, 2026](https://www.eesel.ai/blog/instinct-ai-review)).

- **Access:** invite-only beta, with a waitlist at app.instinct.com.

- **Payments:** since late August 2026 it requests a one-time Stripe Link card for an amount you approve, so the merchant never sees your real card. A Shopify partnership announced on September 28, 2026 lets it check out with Shop Pay.

- **Scale:** founder Noah Shinn said more than 50% of transactions are travel, and Instinct raised $1 billion at a $10 billion valuation on September 28, 2026 ([TechCrunch, September 29, 2026](https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/29/instinct-founder-said-more-than-50-of-transactions-on-the-platform-are-travel-related/)).

Instinct's terms say actions may not always be reversible and records may not be accurate, so it is worth approving purchases carefully.

## How Instinct gets a price
Instinct buys where you ask it to, or where it finds the item, at the price that site shows for your account and location. Neither Instinct nor the reviews above describe it searching the same hotel or product from other countries before it pays (checked on November 8, 2026).

That leaves money on the table. Different countries get different inventory, sales channels, local rates and promotions, so the same hotel or product often has a lower price elsewhere. In [Arbitrica's own price check](https://arbitrica.com/blog/hotel-prices-by-country-data.html), the same Istanbul hotel and dates cost $1,066 searched from the UK and $708 from India.
