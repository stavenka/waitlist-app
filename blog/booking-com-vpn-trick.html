Short answer

The Booking.com VPN trick is real but patchy. Hotels on Booking set different rates and promotions for different markets, so the same room can show a lower total from another country. In the published tests the gap ranged from nothing to 27%, and the best country was different for almost every hotel. There is no single best VPN location for Booking.

What the tests found on Booking

The clearest Booking numbers come from Switzerland. NordVPN's analysis, checked by the Swiss newspaper 20 Minuten, found Booking prices up to 27% higher for people booking from Swiss cities than from elsewhere, for the same hotel, room and dates (IamExpat on the NordVPN and 20 Minuten findings).

Two other studies point the same way with smaller or no gaps. NordVPN's NordVPN UK study, April 2024 named Booking among the sites with lower prices outside the UK. Its NordVPN Southeast Asia study, May 2025 found little or no difference on Booking for three Southeast Asian hotels, while Agoda showed 10-18%.

Booking's answer to the Swiss report: prices change constantly, hotels have "full control over their prices including discounts", and special deals, time of year and exchange rates all affect the price. The Swiss Federal Price Monitor said it took the findings seriously.

Best VPN location for Booking.com

People search for one best country. The tests show why there is not one:

  • Start with the hotel's own country. It won in four of the six tests above.
  • Then try a different region. Taiwan beat Switzerland for Prague. NordVPN's CTO suggested Turkey, Poland, Croatia and Moldova to USA Today; a VPNpro writer quoted by Explore, October 27, 2025 found her best results from Japan or Poland and her highest from the US.
  • Check the full total. Taxes and fees display differently by market, so convert the final amount, not the nightly rate.

Arbitrica's own data shows the same spread: In Arbitrica's own price check, 4 nights at White House Hotel Istanbul cost $1,066 searched from the UK and $708 searched from India, 33.6% less, for the same hotel and the same dates. More in cheapest country to book hotels from.

How to do the Booking VPN trick

  1. Find the hotel on Booking and note the final total for your exact room, dates, guests and cancellation terms.
  2. Clear cookies or open a private window.
  3. Connect the VPN to the hotel's country and search the same room again.
  4. Convert the new total to your currency and note it.
  5. Repeat with 3 to 5 other countries, then read the payment and residency conditions before booking.

What can go wrong: some sites show a captcha or block VPN addresses (FlyerTalk), and some local rates are meant for residents. See do hotels and booking sites block VPNs? and is it legal to use a VPN to book?

Frequently asked questions

Is Booking.com cheaper in different countries?

Sometimes. The same Booking room was up to 27% lower from another country than from Switzerland in the NordVPN and 20 Minuten checks, while other tests found little difference. Hotels set rates and promotions by market, and Booking says hotels control their prices.

What is the best VPN location for Booking.com?

There is no single best location. The hotel's own country won most often in published tests, but Taiwan, Japan and Poland also produced the lowest prices for some hotels. Arbitrica searches all 195 countries at once.

What is the cheapest VPN country for Booking.com?

It changes with every hotel and date. In the tests above the lowest totals came from Georgia, Taiwan, the Netherlands, Indonesia, Greece and Italy, each for a different hotel.

Does the Booking.com VPN trick really work, according to Reddit?

Threads such as r/VPN's "Easy trick that helps to book cheaper hotels with VPN" describe savings on specific stays. The published tests found gaps from 0% to 27% depending on the hotel, so results vary.

Does Booking.com block VPNs?

Booking does not publish a VPN policy. Some travel sites show a captcha or block known VPN addresses, and some local rates require residency, so check the conditions before you book.
