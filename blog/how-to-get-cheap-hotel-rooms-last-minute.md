## Short answer
For most city trips, the last two weeks are when hotel prices are lowest: hotels that still have rooms cut rates to fill them. Hotels.com found the sweet spot 8-14 days out and a 23% average saving for last-minute bookers against people who booked four or more months ahead ([Hotels.com 2026 Hotel Price Index, June 9, 2026](https://www.expedia.com/newsroom/2026-hotel-price-index-how-americans-are-shopping-smarter-this-summer/)). The tactics below get the most out of that window.

## The 8 tactics
- **Search 8-14 days out first.** That was the 2026 sweet spot in the Hotels.com index.
- **Shift the check-in to Sunday.** US stays starting Sunday were 15% lower than those starting Friday.
- **Open a last-minute app for tonight.** HotelTonight beat Booking on 10 of 16 hotels in NerdWallet's test, by up to 23%, and lost on 6, by up to 31% ([NerdWallet, May 28, 2026](https://www.nerdwallet.com/travel/learn/hotel-tonight-what-to-know-before-booking)). Check both.
- **Check the hotel's own site signed in.** Member rates and same-day offers sometimes appear only there. Marriott's member rate is at least 2% lower on weekdays and up to 5% at weekends ([Marriott, It Pays to Book Direct](https://www.marriott.com/online-hotel-booking.mi)).
- **Search the same hotel from other countries.** Late discounts are often released market by market. Research across 1,000 hotels searched from 100 countries found a lower price than the traveller's own in about 80% of countries, with a typical gap of 20-40%.
- **Widen the map by one neighbourhood.** The hotels just outside the busiest area are the ones still discounting.
- **Read the final total.** Late bookings are often nonrefundable and prepaid, so check the taxes, fees and currency before you pay ([pay in the hotel's local currency](https://arbitrica.com/blog/pay-hotel-in-local-currency.html)).
- **Keep checking until the day if your rate is refundable.** Rebook if it drops: [rebook a hotel at a lower price](https://arbitrica.com/blog/rebook-hotel-at-lower-price.html).

## When waiting backfires
Last-minute pricing only works when supply is left. On dates with a festival, a final, a trade fair or a holiday weekend, hotels fill and prices climb. For those dates book a refundable rate as early as you can, then keep checking and rebook if it drops. The Hotels.com index also found the second week of October the priciest week of the year, so October city breaks are a poor bet for waiting.

## Frequently asked questions

### How do I get cheap hotel rooms last minute?
Search 8-14 days out, check in on a Sunday if you can, try a last-minute app for same-night stays, check the hotel's member rate, and search the same hotel from other countries before you pay. Arbitrica does that country search from 195 countries at the checkout.

### Are hotels cheaper last minute?
Often. Hotels.com's 2026 index found last-minute bookers paid 23% less on average than people who booked four or more months out. On event dates the opposite happens, because rooms sell out.

### Is HotelTonight cheaper than Booking.com?
Sometimes. In a NerdWallet test in St. Petersburg, Florida, 10 of 16 hotels were up to 23% lower on HotelTonight and 6 were up to 31% higher. Most HotelTonight bookings are nonrefundable.

### What is the best day to book a last-minute hotel?
The check-in day matters more than the booking day: US stays starting on a Sunday were 15% lower than those starting on a Friday in the Hotels.com 2026 index.
