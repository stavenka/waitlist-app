## Does Booking.com have a price match guarantee?

Yes. Booking.com's "We Price Match" promise says that if you find the same booking cheaper on another website, Booking.com will refund the difference. The lower price has to be for the same hotel and the same dates. It also has to be for the same room type, with the same cancellation and booking conditions, and it has to be publicly bookable. Claims go through Booking.com customer service and must be made within the deadline in Booking.com's terms, so check the current terms for your reservation.

## How to claim Booking.com's price match

Find the lower total price on a public site, document it, and send it to Booking.com customer service before the deadline.

- **Find the lower price on a public site**It has to be bookable, not just listed.

- **Confirm it is the same booking**Same hotel on the same dates. The room type, cancellation policy and extras such as breakfast have to match too.

- **Take a screenshot**Show the URL, the date and the final total with taxes and fees.

- **Contact customer service**Use your reservation or the help center, and include your booking number, the link to the lower price and the screenshot.

- **Wait for the review**If the claim is approved, Booking.com refunds the difference.

## Why many Booking.com price match claims are rejected

Booking.com's definition of "the same booking" is stricter than most travelers expect. The usual reasons a claim fails:

- The cheaper rate has a different cancellation policy.

- It is the same hotel, but the room type, board or occupancy does not match exactly.

- The cheaper price is a member, loyalty, app-only or coupon price, which does not count as public.

- The claim is filed after the deadline.

- The price changed by the time Booking.com checked it.

- The two totals are not comparable because taxes and fees are shown differently.

TipCapture the lower price with taxes and fees visible on the final step before payment. A screenshot of a search results page rarely proves the total.

## Frequently asked questions

### Does Booking.com price match work for non-refundable bookings?

The cheaper offer has to have the same conditions as your booking. A non-refundable booking can only be matched against an offer with the same non-refundable conditions. Check Booking.com's current terms for your reservation.

### What sites count as "public" for a price match claim?

A booking site that anyone can reach without a login, member account or coupon code. Loyalty rates, member-only prices and coupon-gated prices do not qualify. Other major booking sites such as Expedia, Hotels.com and Agoda do.
