# Same hotel, different price: why one room has many prices

Updated 2026-11-10 by Arbitrica. Canonical: https://arbitrica.com/blog/same-hotel-different-price.html

## Key takeaways
- One hotel room is sold through many sites and markets, each with its own rate, allotment and promotion, so many prices exist at the same time.
- Country matters most: Booking prices were up to 27% higher from Switzerland, and an Istanbul hotel was 33.6% less from India than from the UK.
- Site, member and app rates, taxes display, currency and dynamic pricing add more differences.
- Arbitrica searches the same hotel from 195 countries across sites and finds the lowest price in the world. Research across 1,000 hotels searched from 100 countries found a lower price than the traveller's own in about 80% of countries, with a typical gap of 20-40%.

## Short answer
The same hotel room has different prices because it is sold through many sites and in many markets at once, and each can carry its own rate. Hotels set rates and promotions by country and channel, sites add their own member and app deals, and dynamic pricing moves everything during the day. So the price you see depends on where you search from, which site and device you use, and when.

## The six reasons the same room has different prices
- **Country or market.** Different countries get different inventory, local rates and promotions. Booking says hotels have "full control over their prices including discounts" ([IamExpat on the NordVPN and 20 Minuten findings](https://www.iamexpat.ch/lifestyle/lifestyle-news/bookingcom-criticised-charging-travellers-from-switzerland-more)).
- **Site or channel.** The hotel's own site, Booking, Expedia, Agoda and wholesalers each sell from their own allotment and deals.
- **Member, app and mobile rates.** Logged-in, loyalty and app-only prices are lower on some sites ([The Traveler, June 30, 2026](https://www.thetraveler.org/why-agodas-prices-can-look-shockingly-different-from-other-sites/)).
- **Taxes and fees display.** Some markets show totals; others add taxes and fees at checkout.
- **Currency.** Conversion and currency fees can add 3-5% ([CardRatings, citing Visa, August 2026](https://www.cardratings.com/travel/using-a-credit-card-abroad-dcc-fees.html)).
- **Time.** Dynamic pricing changes rates with demand, sometimes several times a day ([how hotel dynamic pricing works](https://arbitrica.com/blog/how-hotel-dynamic-pricing-works.html)).

## Frequently asked questions

### Why is the same hotel room different prices?
Because the room is sold through many sites and markets, each with its own rate and promotion, and dynamic pricing changes them over time. Member rates, taxes display and currency add more differences.

### Why are hotel prices different in different countries?
Hotels and booking sites set rates, allotments and promotions market by market. In Arbitrica's check, an Istanbul hotel cost $1,066 from the UK and $708 from India for the same dates.

### Why are hotel prices different on different sites?
Each site sells from its own allotment and deals, adds its own member or app discounts, and may display taxes and fees differently. Always check the final total.

### Is the hotel's own website always the lowest?
No. It is sometimes lowest, especially with member rates, but other sites and other countries can be lower for the same room.

### How do I find the lowest price for the same hotel?
Check the final total across sites and countries. Arbitrica does this at the checkout from 195 countries and prepares the booking at the lowest price it finds.
