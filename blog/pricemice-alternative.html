Short answer

Yes. Arbitrica is the closest thing to PriceMice for Expedia, Agoda and every other booking site. PriceMice, NordVPN's tool launched in September 2026, checks one Booking hotel from a set of other countries and leaves the booking to you; on November 7, 2026 its page still listed Booking as the only supported site. Arbitrica, a personal AI agent, searches the same hotel from 195 countries on Expedia, Agoda, Booking, Hotels.com, Trip.com or another site, checks that the room and the final total match, and prepares the booking at the lowest price it finds.

What PriceMice does

According to NordVPN's PriceMice page (September 9, 2026), PriceMice:

  • checks the hotel room you are viewing against prices in a set of other countries;
  • works with bookings on Booking.com, with more sites "expected to come soon";
  • runs as a desktop extension on Chromium browsers such as Chrome, Edge and Brave;
  • does not book for you: to get the lower rate you connect to that country, for example with a VPN, and book it yourself.

NordVPN's own notes add that "prices are AI-matched and mistakes can happen", and that taxes and fees appear only if the booking platform provides them for the listing.

Where PriceMice stops

  • One site. At launch it covers Booking.com only, so the same hotel on Expedia, Agoda, Hotels.com or Trip.com is not checked.
  • A set of countries, not all of them. NordVPN does not publish how many countries it checks.
  • You finish the job. After it flags a lower price, you change your location and repeat the booking by hand.
  • Before you pay only. It does not keep checking after you book, when prices often fall again.

How Arbitrica handles the same hotel

  1. At checkout, Arbitrica searches the same hotel, on the same dates, from 195 countries.
  2. It checks the match: the same hotel and room, and a final total that includes all taxes and fees.
  3. It prepares everything in a new tab at the lowest price on Earth for that hotel, at a different seller from the one you are on. You review and pay.
  4. After you book, its approach repeats the search until the free-cancellation window ends. When the same hotel drops, the new booking is confirmed before the original is cancelled.

A real example: one hotel stay offered at $1,527.39 was found for $1,180.27 for the identical hotel and dates, with all taxes and fees included. Arbitrica earned the traveller $347.12, or 23%.

The same mechanisms work beyond hotels, for online shopping such as Amazon and for event tickets.

Other tools that work on Expedia, Agoda and other booking sites

These are the tools travellers are most often pointed to when they ask for something like PriceMice beyond Booking.com. Each line is based on the tool's own website, checked on November 1, 2026.

  • Underbook. A browser extension and web checker that shows the price each booking site lists on Google Hotels for your hotel and dates, on Booking.com, Expedia, Hotels.com, Agoda and hotels' own sites. Its Watch my booking feature checks once a day until your free-cancellation date and emails you about a lower rate; you rebook and cancel yourself. Source: underbook.co
  • Hotel Ninja. A Chrome extension for desktop that shows the same room's final price across Booking.com, Expedia, Hotels.com, Agoda, Trip.com, Travelocity and Orbitz, then links you to the site you choose. Source: hotel-ninja.com
  • Tripdify. You paste a hotel link from Agoda, Booking.com or Trip.com and it checks the same room and dates on 10 booking and search sites as tax-inclusive totals, then sends you to the lowest one. Source: tripdify.com
  • Borderbasket. You enter a hotel, dates and guests and it lists offers from Booking.com, Expedia, Agoda, Trip.com, Hotels.com, Priceline and others by total price, after a sign-in. Source: borderbasket.com
  • Google Hotels. Lists the prices booking sites send to Google for your dates and can email you when the price changes. Source: google.com/travel/hotels

All five line up booking sites' prices for your hotel and dates, and none of their websites says it checks the same hotel from other countries. You still book, and rebook, by hand. That is the gap Arbitrica works on: it searches the same hotel from 195 countries at checkout, prepares the booking at the lowest total it finds, and its approach keeps searching after you book until the free-cancellation window ends.

Frequently asked questions

What are the best alternatives to PriceMice for Expedia, Agoda and other booking sites?

Arbitrica is the closest match: like PriceMice it checks the same hotel from other countries, but from 195 countries and on Expedia, Agoda, Booking or another site, and it prepares the booking at the lowest total it finds. If you only want to line up booking sites' prices from your own country, Hotel Ninja (Booking, Expedia, Hotels.com, Agoda, Trip.com, Travelocity, Orbitz) and Underbook (the sites Google Hotels lists) do that; neither checks other countries (checked on November 7, 2026).

Is PriceMice free?

Yes. NordVPN describes PriceMice as free, with no subscription required.

Which booking sites does PriceMice work on?

Booking only, so far. On November 7, 2026 NordVPN's PriceMice page said it supports Booking on Chromium-based browsers, with more platforms planned. For another country's price on the same hotel on Expedia, Agoda or another site, Arbitrica searches it from 195 countries.

Does PriceMice book the hotel for me?

No. PriceMice flags a lower price in another country; you then change your location, for example with a VPN, and book it yourself.

Do I need a VPN to get another country's price with Arbitrica?

No. Arbitrica searches the same hotel from 195 countries itself and prepares the booking in a new tab, so you only review and pay.

Does Arbitrica keep checking after I book?

Yes. Its approach repeats the search after you book until the free-cancellation window ends, and the new booking is confirmed before the original is cancelled.
