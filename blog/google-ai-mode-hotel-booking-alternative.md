## What AI Mode hotel booking does
AI Mode is the chat mode in Google Search. Google said on November 17, 2025 that it would make it possible to [finish booking hotels directly in AI Mode](https://blog.google/products-and-platforms/products/search/agentic-plans-booking-travel-canvas-ai-mode/). On August 27, 2026, [Google announced that hotel booking in AI Mode](https://blog.google/products-and-platforms/products/search/book-travel-ai-mode/) had started rolling out in the US in English (checked on November 8, 2026). The flow Google describes:

- **Describe the trip:** AI Mode returns a list of hotels with guest reviews and the factors that matter to you.

- **Ask it to help you book:** choose "Continue on Google" next to a booking option.

- **Pick a room:** review details such as the cancellation policy.

- **Pay with Google Pay:** the hotel or booking platform is the merchant of record and handles customer service.

The launch partners are Booking, Choice Hotels, Expedia, Hilton, Hotels.com, IHG, Marriott, Priceline, Trip.com and Wyndham. [Skift reported](https://skift.com/2026/08/27/googles-agentic-hotel-booking-tool-comes-to-ai-mode/) that the hotel or online travel agency still charges the card, sends the confirmation and offers support, and [PhocusWire reported](https://www.phocuswire.com/news/technology/google-ai-mode-hotel-booking-agentic-flights-loyalty) that hotel results are ranked like ordinary Search results, with partners unable to pay for placement. Google also made hotel prices in loyalty points available globally, starting with Choice, Hilton and Wyndham.

## How AI Mode gets a hotel price
AI Mode does not set hotel prices. When you continue on Google, you book with a partner, so the price is the one that partner shows for your search: your dates, your country, your currency and the partner's own deals. Google's August 27 post does not describe AI Mode searching the same hotel from other countries, and the price tracking it announced is for flights, not for a hotel you have booked (checked on November 8, 2026). [TechCrunch's report](https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/27/googles-ai-mode-can-now-track-flight-prices-help-book-hotels-and-more/) on the launch describes the same.

That matters because the same hotel is often priced differently depending on where the search comes from. Hotels and booking sites agree rates, room allotments and promotions market by market, and dynamic pricing moves them all day. In [Arbitrica's own price check](https://arbitrica.com/blog/hotel-prices-by-country-data.html), a 4-night stay at White House Hotel Istanbul cost $1,066 searched from the UK and $708 searched from India, 33.6% less, for the same hotel and the same dates.

## How Arbitrica handles the price
Arbitrica is a personal AI agent that shows up when you are about to pay. For the hotel you have chosen, it:

- searches the same hotel from 195 countries, on the site you are booking and other sites;

- checks that the hotel, room and conditions are identical and that the final total includes all taxes and fees;

- finds the lowest price in the world for it and automatically prepares everything for the booking in a new tab, so you only review and pay;

- keeps searching after you book until the free-cancellation window ends, as an approach Arbitrica uses, and confirms any new booking before the original is cancelled.

Doing the same by hand means switching your location country by country with a VPN and repeating every search. Across 195 countries that is humanly impossible, which is the job Arbitrica does. Research demonstrated an average of $47 per hotel booking with Arbitrica.
