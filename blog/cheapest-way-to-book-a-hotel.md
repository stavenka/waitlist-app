# The cheapest way to book a hotel in 2026: direct, app or agent?

Updated 2026-11-11 by Arbitrica. Canonical: https://arbitrica.com/blog/cheapest-way-to-book-a-hotel.html

## Key takeaways
- There is no one cheapest booking site. The lowest final price for the same hotel moves between the hotel's own site, the big agencies and different country markets.
- Book direct when the chain's member rate or best rate guarantee applies: Marriott's member rate is at least 2% lower on weekdays and up to 5% at weekends.
- Agencies such as Booking, Expedia and Agoda win when they hold a promotional rate; NordVPN found 10-18% gaps on Agoda in Southeast Asia.
- For same-night stays, last-minute apps were up to 23% lower in a NerdWallet test, but some hotels were up to 31% higher.
- The widest gaps come from the country you search from. Research across 1,000 hotels searched from 100 countries found a lower price than the traveller's own in about 80% of countries, with a typical gap of 20-40%.

## When each way of booking wins
| Way of booking | When it tends to win | Watch out for |
|---|---|---|
| Hotel's own site, signed in | Chain hotels with member rates; when a best rate guarantee lets you claim a lower price found elsewhere | Member rates need an account; guarantee claims have tight time windows |
| Booking, Expedia, Hotels.com | Independent hotels, promotional rates, loyalty programmes such as Genius and One Key | Taxes and fees shown late; pay-at-hotel extras |
| Agoda, Trip.com | Asia-Pacific stays, where their inventory is deepest | Prices can swing a lot by country and by currency |
| Last-minute apps | Same-day and next-day stays | Mostly nonrefundable; some hotels higher than elsewhere |
| Another country's market | Any hotel sold in several markets | Residency rules on some local rates; checking by hand is slow |

## Booking direct: when it is lowest
Chains want direct bookings, so they reward members. Marriott says its member rates are at least 2% lower on weekdays and up to 5% at weekends, and its best rate guarantee matches a lower price found elsewhere and adds 25% off or 5,000 points, if you claim within 24 hours of booking and at least 24 hours before check-in ([Marriott, It Pays to Book Direct](https://www.marriott.com/online-hotel-booking.mi)). That makes direct the default for chain hotels when you know the guarantee rules.

## Agencies and regional sites
Big agencies hold promotional rates that a hotel's own site does not always show, and regional sites can be strongest in their home regions. In NordVPN's 2025 study of Southeast Asian stays, Agoda showed 10-18% gaps for the same trips while Booking and Airbnb showed little difference ([NordVPN Southeast Asia study, May 2025](https://nordvpn.com/zh/blog/genggai-ip-dizhi-jiesheng-feiyong/)). Expedia no longer refunds a price drop after booking: its Hotel Price Guarantee ended on July 28, 2026 ([Expedia price drops after booking](https://arbitrica.com/blog/expedia-price-drop-refund.html)).

## Last-minute apps
In NerdWallet's test of a midweek night in St. Petersburg, Florida, HotelTonight showed 16 hotels and Booking 52. Ten of the 16 were up to 23% lower on HotelTonight; six were up to 31% higher ([NerdWallet, May 28, 2026](https://www.nerdwallet.com/travel/learn/hotel-tonight-what-to-know-before-booking)). Good for a same-night bed, not a default. More in [how to get cheap hotel rooms last minute](https://arbitrica.com/blog/how-to-get-cheap-hotel-rooms-last-minute.html).

## Another country: the widest gaps
Research across 1,000 hotels searched from 100 countries found a lower price than the traveller's own in about 80% of countries, with a typical gap of 20-40%. In [Arbitrica's own price check](https://arbitrica.com/blog/hotel-prices-by-country-data.html), 4 nights at White House Hotel Istanbul cost $1,066 searched from the UK and $708 searched from India, 33.6% less, for the same hotel and the same dates. The gap comes from markets: each country can carry its own rates, promotions and allotments for the same hotel. ## Frequently asked questions

### What is the cheapest way to book a hotel?
Pick the hotel, then check the final total for the same room and terms on the hotel's own site as a member, on the main agencies and from other countries. The lowest total on the payment screen wins. Arbitrica searches the same hotel from 195 countries at the checkout and prepares the booking at the lowest price it finds.

### Is it cheaper to book a hotel direct?
Often for chain hotels, because of member rates and best rate guarantees. Marriott's member rate is at least 2% lower on weekdays and up to 5% at weekends. Independent hotels are more often lower on agencies during promotions.

### Which hotel booking site is cheapest?
None is always lowest. Agoda is often strong in Asia, Booking and Expedia run member promotions, and last-minute apps win some same-night stays. The same hotel can also be lower from another country on the same site.

### How do I book hotels cheaper?
Choose low-demand nights, book refundable, judge the final total, use member rates, pay in local currency and search the same hotel from other countries before you pay.
