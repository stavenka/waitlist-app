# Does a VPN make hotels cheaper? What the tests found

Updated 2026-11-10 by Arbitrica. Canonical: https://arbitrica.com/blog/does-vpn-make-hotels-cheaper.html

## Key takeaways
- A VPN can show a lower hotel price, but not reliably. NordVPN's own commissioned studies found 7.9% to 26% on individual hotels; USA Today found differences of a few dollars either way.
- The country you search from changes which markets, sites, local rates and promotions you see. Your IP address on its own does not reprice an identical listing.
- The VPN method is one country at a time, by hand, with fresh cookies each time. The published tests tried between 1 and 10 countries per trip.
- Arbitrica searches the same hotel from 195 countries at the checkout and prepares the booking at the lowest price it finds. Research across 1,000 hotels searched from 100 countries found a lower price than the traveller's own in about 80% of countries, with a typical gap of 20-40%.

Yes, sometimes, and usually by less than the headlines suggest. In the published tests a VPN set to another country showed a lower total for some hotels, a few dollars more for others, and no change for many. A VPN only lets you look at one market at a time.

## Why the results swing from 26% to nothing
A hotel does not have one price. It sells the same room through many sites and markets, and each market can carry its own rate, room allotment and promotion. Dynamic pricing then moves those rates with demand. When a VPN puts you in another country, the site may show you that market's offer, its currency and its taxes display. Sometimes that offer is lower, sometimes it is the same, and sometimes it is higher.

Three things decide whether you see a gap:

- **Which country.** In the tests the hotel's own country often won (Indonesia for Bali, Italy for Milan), but not always. NordVPN's CTO told USA Today to try less obvious countries such as Turkey, Poland, Croatia or Moldova.
- **Which site.** NordVPN's 2025 Southeast Asia test found gaps of 10-18% on Agoda and little or no difference on Booking and Airbnb for the same trips ([NordVPN Southeast Asia study, May 2025](https://nordvpn.com/zh/blog/genggai-ip-dizhi-jiesheng-feiyong/)).
- **Which rate.** Some local rates are meant for residents. A Phocuswright analyst told USA Today that some deals may ask for a home address or a driving licence from that country.

## How to test it yourself
If you want to try the VPN route for one hotel, this is the method the testers used:

- Pick the exact hotel, dates, guests, room type and cancellation terms.
- Note the full total with taxes and fees from your own country.
- Clear cookies or open a private window, then connect the VPN to one country.
- Search the same hotel and room on the same site and note the total, converted to your currency.
- Repeat for each country you want to try, then check payment and residency conditions before you book.

Each country takes several minutes. Trying 10 is an evening; trying 195 is not realistic by hand.

## Frequently asked questions

### Does changing your VPN make hotels cheaper?
Sometimes. In NordVPN's commissioned tests the same hotels were 7.9% to 26% lower through a server in another country; in USA Today's test the differences were a few dollars and sometimes higher. It depends on the hotel, the site, the dates and the country.

### Does a VPN change hotel prices on Booking.com?
It can change which market's offer you see. A NordVPN analysis reported by 20 Minuten found Booking prices up to 27% higher from Switzerland than from other countries for the same room and dates, while NordVPN's 2025 Southeast Asia test found little difference on Booking.

### What do people on Reddit say about using a VPN for cheaper hotels?
Popular threads such as r/LifeProTips' "It is possible to book rooms cheaper with a VPN" and r/VPN's "Easy trick that helps to book cheaper hotels with VPN" describe savings on specific bookings. One Reddit user quoted by Explore was redirected to a pricier site when paying with an American card. The published tests show the savings are inconsistent.

There is no single one. The hotel's own country often wins, but tests also found lower prices from Taiwan, Japan, Poland and others. Arbitrica searches all 195 countries so you do not have to guess.
