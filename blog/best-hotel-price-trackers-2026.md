## Why a hotel price tracker matters in 2026

Hotel pricing is more dynamic and more opaque than at any point since online travel agencies arrived. The same hotel can carry several different prices on the same night depending on the site, the currency, the member tier and the country you search from. Checking all of them by hand is not realistic. A hotel price tracker does that work for you, before or after you book.

The online travel agency model took off in the late 1990s, and pricing has only become more complex since. Most major chains run revenue-management systems that adjust rates through the day based on remaining inventory, search volume and competitor rates. Different countries also get access to different inventory, channels, local rates and promotions. The number on the screen when you book is rarely the lowest price available for that hotel.

A hotel price tracker watches some or all of this on your behalf. The category now has three sub-types: tools that look for a lower price for the same hotel before you pay; post-booking monitors that check for price drops after you have reserved; and full-flow agents that do both and prepare the rebooking for you.

## How the options were evaluated

Each option is reviewed against four criteria:

- **Search at checkout:** does it find a lower price for the same hotel before you book?

- **Search after booking:** does it watch for drops after you book?

- **Country coverage:** from how many countries does it search?

- **Rebooking:** does it handle the cancellation and re-reservation, or do you?

All eight were active when this guide was first published; Pruvo has since left the consumer market, as noted below.

## 1. Arbitrica

Arbitrica is a personal AI agent that lives in your browser. It meets you at the checkout when you are about to pay, searches the same hotel from 195 countries, and finds the lowest price on Earth for it, at a different seller from the one you are on. Different countries get access to different inventory, channels, local rates and promotions, so the same hotel searched from another country is often 5-30% cheaper. Arbitrica validates that the lower price is for the identical hotel, includes all taxes and fees, and will not suddenly change, then prepares the booking so you only review and pay.

Its multiple-time arbitrage approach repeats the search after you book, until the free-cancellation window ends, and prepares a re-booking at the lower price. The new booking is confirmed before the original is cancelled. The same mechanisms apply beyond hotels, to flights, event tickets, online shopping and anything else with a cancellation or return window.

**Best for:** anyone who wants the lowest price on a hotel with the least effort, from individual travelers to companies: teams traveling to trade shows and conferences, operations managers and travel managers.

**Limitation:** works on cash bookings, not points or award redemptions.

## 2. Pruvo

Pruvo was a post-booking tracker. You forwarded your booking confirmation by email, Pruvo read the property and dates, and checked the price on the same booking platform. When it found a drop, you cancelled and rebooked manually. Pruvo was founded in 2016, and the email-based onboarding was easy.

**Limitation:** single country, no geographic search, manual rebooking only.

**Update:** Web Travel Group has announced its acquisition of Pruvo, which now presents itself as a business-to-business platform with consumer bookings no longer supported. Arbitrica works for both consumers and companies, so travelers and businesses alike have an option that searches the same hotel from 195 countries. See [Arbitrica vs. Pruvo](https://arbitrica.com/blog/arbitrica-vs-pruvo.html).

## 3. RoomHawk

RoomHawk says it searches and tracks over 2 million hotel rooms and monitors price drops for a stay you choose. It also advertises AI-powered auto-rebooking, though its site does not explain how that step works.

**Limitation:** it does not describe searching the same hotel from other countries, and there is no search at checkout.

For the full comparison, see [Arbitrica vs. RoomHawk](https://arbitrica.com/blog/arbitrica-vs-roomhawk.html).

## 4. RatePunk

RatePunk is a browser extension focused on comparing platforms before checkout. When you reach a hotel listing on Booking.com, Expedia or Hotels.com, RatePunk shows the same hotel's price on other platforms. It is available for several browsers and as a mobile app, and it also covers flights. For users who want to know whether the same hotel is listed lower on another site right now, RatePunk is a clean experience.

**Limitation:** its hotel product works at one moment, before booking, and it is not built to search the same hotel from 195 countries.

## 5. Repriced

Repriced is one of the few post-booking trackers that handles rebooking for you. You connect your email or upload your bookings, Repriced monitors flight and hotel prices, and when they drop it rebooks or secures credits without manual effort, according to its April 2026 announcement. The same announcement said it would move to a paid subscription in May 2026.

**Limitation:** it works after booking only, with no search at checkout, and it does not describe searching the same hotel from many countries.

## 6. OneAir

OneAir is a travel membership that tracks flight and hotel prices. After you book a hotel, it watches for price drops and, according to its published coverage, can rebook the same stay at a lower rate. It also offers private hotel rates and flight deals.

**Limitation:** hotel tracking is one feature among many, and there is no geographic search from other countries.

## 7. Hotel Ninja

Hotel Ninja is a simple post-booking tracker that emails you when the price of a booked hotel drops.

**Limitation:** single country, no search at checkout, manual rebooking. Monitoring only starts once you have booked.

## 8. Directo

Directo is a browser extension that looks for a hotel's own booking website while you browse a listing on Booking.com, Airbnb or Vrbo, so you can book direct instead of through the platform. For loyalty-program members, booking direct sometimes brings member rates or perks such as breakfast or late checkout.

**Limitation:** narrow focus on direct-vs-platform booking. No post-booking monitoring and no geographic search. When it finds the hotel's site, you may need to enter your dates and guests again.

## Which hotel price tracker saves the most money?

The one that searches the same hotel from the most countries. Without geographic search, the most any tracker can find is a drop over time on one country's listing. With it, the same hotel is often 5-30% cheaper from another country before any drop happens. ## Which hotel price tracker should I choose?

Pick one that searches the same hotel from other countries. Of the options here, Arbitrica is the one that searches from all 195, and the difference between countries is the largest source of savings. If you only want alerts on a stay you have already booked, a post-booking monitor catches the drops over time.

Geographic arbitrage works whether the hotel is abroad or in your own country, because what you can get for the same hotel depends on the inventory, channels, local rates and promotions each country has access to.

If you do not want to cancel and rebook yourself, choose an option that prepares or handles the rebooking. Arbitrica combines the search from 195 countries at checkout with a booking prepared for you, and its multiple-time approach repeats the search until the free-cancellation window ends.

TipWhatever you use, book a refundable rate when the price gap is small. A tracker can only help after booking if you are free to cancel.

## Frequently asked questions

### Does Arbitrica work before or after I book?

Both. Live arbitrage runs at the checkout, when Arbitrica searches the same hotel from 195 countries. Its multiple-time approach repeats the search after you book until the free-cancellation window ends, and the new booking is confirmed before the original is cancelled.

### What if the lower price is in a different currency?

Arbitrica validates that the lower price is for the identical hotel, that it includes all taxes and fees, and that it will not suddenly change before you pay. A price that only looks lower before everything is counted does not count.

### Does Arbitrica only look at Booking.com and Expedia?

No. Arbitrica finds the lowest price for the identical hotel at a different seller from the one you are on: another booking site, the hotel chain or anyone else. The seller is never the point; the lowest total for the identical hotel is.

### Is there a hotel price tracker that handles award (points/miles) redemptions?

Award redemptions follow different inventory and pricing rules that cash-price trackers do not see. Arbitrica and the other options listed here are for cash bookings, so use a dedicated award-search tool for points.

### What is the difference between a hotel price tracker and Hopper?

Hopper is a booking app known for predicting price movement and offering price-freeze options. Arbitrica does not predict; it searches the same hotel from 195 countries for lower prices that already exist right now.
