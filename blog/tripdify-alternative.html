Short answer

If you like Tripdify's one-link check but want the same hotel searched from other countries too, the alternative is Arbitrica. Tripdify takes the hotel link you paste, lines the stay up on 10 booking and search sites as tax-inclusive totals and sends you to the lowest one, with its card-channel check working only for Agoda links. Arbitrica is a personal AI agent that searches the same hotel from 195 countries, on the site you are booking and other sites, checks that the room and final total match, and prepares the booking at the lowest price in the world for you to review and pay; its approach then keeps searching after you book until free cancellation ends. Research across 1,000 hotels searched from 100 countries found a lower price than the traveller's own in about 80% of countries, with a typical gap of 20-40%.

What Tripdify does

According to Tripdify's homepage and help page (checked on November 25, 2026):

  • One hotel link: you paste a property page from Agoda, Booking, Trip.com, Expedia, Hotels.com, Airbnb, Yanolja or another major site, and Tripdify lines the stay up on 10 hotel booking and search sites.
  • Tax-inclusive totals: the homepage says everything is compared as a tax-inclusive total; the help page adds that some hotels still collect a resort fee or city tax separately on site.
  • Card channels on Agoda: its help page says the channel price check, which reads prices offered through specific payment cards, is available only for Agoda links; other links get a cross-site panel.
  • Extension for automatic checks: the help page says automatic price comparison needs its Tripdify Saver browser extension; without it there is a link-only mode.
  • Daily watch: the extension's price watch re-runs the search once a day and notifies you when the best total falls by 2% or more.
  • Who runs it: its terms name the operator as DialogueForge, with an address in Gwanak-gu, Seoul; its About page says one person builds and runs it and that affiliate commission is its only income.

We searched for independent reviews of Tripdify on November 25, 2026 and found none published; the facts on this page come from Tripdify's own pages, linked above.

Where Tripdify stops

  • Your own country and currency. Tripdify's help page says results are shown in the currency the Agoda page displayed in your browser, with no conversion of its own. Its pages do not describe searching the same hotel from other countries (checked on November 25, 2026).
  • Agoda first. The deepest check, card channels, works only for Agoda links.
  • You book it yourself. Tripdify sends you to the site with the lowest total; its pages do not describe preparing the booking for you.
  • Rebooking by hand. Its help page suggests booking a free-cancellation rate and cancelling and rebooking yourself when a lower price appears.

The gap between countries is often the biggest one. In Arbitrica's own price check, a 4-night stay at White House Hotel Istanbul cost $1,066 searched from the UK and $708 searched from India, 33.6% less, for the same hotel and the same dates. Prices and room availability differ by country and by site, and dynamic pricing moves them through the day.

How Arbitrica differs

Arbitrica is a personal AI agent that shows up after you choose your hotel room. For the hotel you have chosen, it:

  • searches the same hotel, on the same dates, from 195 countries, on the site you are booking and other sites;
  • checks that the hotel, room and conditions are identical and that the final total includes all taxes and fees;
  • finds the lowest price in the world for it and automatically prepares everything for the booking in a new tab, so you only review and pay;
  • keeps searching after you book until the free-cancellation window ends, as an approach Arbitrica uses, and confirms any new booking before the original is cancelled.

The first search happens before you pay, so the lowest price in the world is in front of you at the moment you book. Price drops after booking often have to be acted on at once, before someone else takes the room at that rate, which is why a personal agent that acts instantly helps. Research demonstrated an average of $47 per hotel booking with Arbitrica.

Which one to use

  • Use Tripdify if you book on Agoda, want to see prices tied to your payment cards, and are happy to search from your own country and book yourself.
  • Use Arbitrica when you want the lowest price in the world for the hotel you have chosen, searched from 195 countries before you pay, the booking prepared for you, and a watch after you book until free cancellation ends.
