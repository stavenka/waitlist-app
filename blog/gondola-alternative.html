Gondola alternative that also searches the same hotel from 195 countries

Gondola books hotels direct with the big chains, pays cash back and can reprice a refundable stay automatically when the rate drops. Its pages do not describe searching the same hotel from other countries. Here is what each one does and when to use which.

Key takeaways

  • Gondola searches hotel chains such as Marriott, Hilton, Hyatt, IHG and Accor at once, shows cash and points rates side by side, and says it is not an OTA: stays are booked direct with the hotel.
  • Its AutoSave reprices refundable cash bookings made through Gondola (Hilton excluded) and Hyatt, Marriott and IHG cash bookings made elsewhere, only when the room, rate and cancellation policy match exactly (checked on November 21, 2026).
  • Gondola says its travellers save an average of $413 a year from AutoSave, and it added Flight AutoSave for United, Delta, Alaska and American on April 29, 2026.
  • Arbitrica, a personal AI agent, searches the same hotel from 195 countries before you pay, checks the room and final total, prepares the booking at the lowest price in the world, and keeps searching after you book until free cancellation ends.

Short answer

If you like Gondola's automatic repricing but want the price from other countries too, the alternative is Arbitrica. Gondola is a hotel booking app for the big chains: it shows cash and points rates side by side, books direct, pays cash back and, with AutoSave, reprices an eligible refundable stay when the same rate drops. Arbitrica is a personal AI agent that searches the same hotel from 195 countries, on the site you are booking and other sites, checks that the room and final total match, and prepares the booking at the lowest price in the world for you to review and pay. Research across 1,000 hotels searched from 100 countries found a lower price than the traveller's own in about 80% of countries, with a typical gap of 20-40%.

What Gondola does

According to Gondola's site and its AutoSave guide, last updated on July 13, 2026 (checked on November 21, 2026):

  • Search: it searches Marriott, Hilton, Hyatt, IHG, Accor and more at once, with cash and points rates side by side.
  • Booking: its App Store page says Gondola is not an OTA, and Gondola says it books directly with the major hotel chains and independents.
  • Rate monitoring: its help page says it continuously monitors refundable hotel bookings in your Trips for lower rates, cash and points stays alike, and emails you when the rate drops. You add bookings by linking your email, forwarding a confirmation or adding them by hand.
  • AutoSave: with a payment card linked, it reprices an eligible refundable reservation at the new rate. The hotel issues a new reservation at the lower rate and Gondola cancels the old one, and it never moves you to a rate with different cancellation terms.
  • Cash back: Gondola Cash of 3% for members, 5% for Silver and 7% for Gold, according to the App Store page.

Gondola says its travellers save an average of $413 a year from AutoSave. NerdWallet reported that Gondola launched Flight AutoSave on April 29, 2026; Gondola's help page says it covers bookings through United, Delta, Alaska and American, except basic economy. Gondola's founders are Skyler Erickson and Xan Tanner, and its about page lists Next View Ventures and Maven Ventures as backers. On November 21, 2026 its iPhone app showed a 4.9 rating from 281 ratings.

The Points Guy's guide to Gondola, published on June 10, 2026, describes stays booked through Gondola as direct bookings and notes that the reviewer found some inaccuracies in trip tracking, such as a misread Marriott Homes and Villas stay.

Where Gondola stops

  • Your own country. Gondola's pages do not describe searching the same hotel from other countries (checked on November 21, 2026); it reprices against the rates the hotel shows for your search.
  • Refundable stays only. Non-refundable rates are not eligible for AutoSave, and the original booking must be refundable to move it.
  • Eligible bookings. AutoSave covers refundable cash bookings made through Gondola except Hilton stays, plus Hyatt, Marriott and IHG cash bookings made elsewhere. Points stays are monitored, not AutoSaved.
  • The same rate only. It reprices only when the room, rate and cancellation policy match exactly.
  • A card on file. Gondola says a credit card is always required to turn AutoSave on.

The country gap is often the biggest one. In Arbitrica's own price check, a 4-night stay at White House Hotel Istanbul cost $1,066 searched from the UK and $708 searched from India, 33.6% less, for the same hotel and the same dates. Prices differ by country and by site, and dynamic pricing moves them through the day.

How Arbitrica differs

Arbitrica is a personal AI agent that shows up after you choose your hotel room. For the hotel you have chosen, it:

  • searches the same hotel from 195 countries, on the site you are booking and other sites;
  • checks that the hotel, room and conditions are identical and that the final total includes all taxes and fees;
  • finds the lowest price in the world for it and automatically prepares everything for the booking in a new tab, so you only review and pay;
  • keeps searching after you book until the free-cancellation window ends, as an approach Arbitrica uses, and confirms any new booking before the original is cancelled.

Price drops after booking often have to be acted on at once, before someone else takes the room at that rate, which is why a personal agent that acts instantly helps. Searching 195 countries by hand is humanly impossible; that is the job Arbitrica does. Research demonstrated an average of $47 per hotel booking with Arbitrica.

Frequently asked questions

What is a good Gondola alternative?

What does Gondola do?

Gondola searches hotel chains such as Marriott, Hilton, Hyatt, IHG and Accor, shows cash and points rates side by side, books direct, pays Gondola Cash of 3-7% and monitors refundable bookings for lower rates.

Does Gondola check prices from other countries?

Is Gondola an OTA?

No. Its App Store page says Gondola is not an OTA, and Gondola says it books directly with the major hotel chains and independents.

Does Arbitrica keep checking the price after I book?

Yes. Its approach repeats the search after you book until the free-cancellation window ends, and a new booking is confirmed before the original one is cancelled.
