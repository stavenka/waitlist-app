# Why is the price on Booking.com different when you go to make the reservation?

Published 2026-11-13 by Arbitrica. Canonical: https://arbitrica.com/blog/why-is-booking-com-price-different-when-you-reserve.html

## Key takeaways
- The search result shows one price for one room, rate and guest count; the reservation page prices the exact choice you made.
- Booking separates a base price, a book price with included charges, and a total with excluded charges such as city tax (Booking's partner documentation, checked on November 13, 2026).
- Whether a charge is included depends on the traveler's and the hotel's countries, so the same stay can show a different book price to different people.
- If the exchange rate moves before you pay, Booking's documentation says you will pay a different price to the one shown.
- A correct total is not the lowest total: research across 1,000 hotels found a lower price in about 80% of countries, with a typical gap of 20-40%.

## Short answer

The price in Booking's search results is a snapshot for one room, one rate and the guests you searched for, and it may not include every charge. When you go to make the reservation, Booking prices the exact room, rate and guests you picked, lists the charges the first number left out, such as city tax, and converts the amount at the current exchange rate, while a deal that sold out or does not apply to you drops away. The order total on the last page, plus anything marked as payable at the property, is the number you will actually pay.

## Six reasons the price changes at the reservation step

### 1. You picked a different room or rate

The search result usually shows the lowest option for the search you made. On the hotel page you may choose a bigger room, breakfast, or a free-cancellation rate instead of a non-refundable one, and each of those is a separate price. Two rooms for the same dates can be far apart even when the headline looked like one number.

### 2. The number of guests or rooms changed

Prices are set per occupancy. Adding a second adult, a child or an extra bed after the search, or booking two rooms, gives a new price at the reservation step.

### 3. Charges the first number left out

Booking's own documentation for partners splits a price into a base price, a book price that adds the included charges, and a total that adds the excluded ones. Excluded charges, often a city or tourist tax, are listed separately and can be payable at the property (checked on November 13, 2026). If you were looking at the base or the book price in the results, the total on the reservation page will be higher. What each site puts in its first price is covered in [which booking sites guarantee the final price upfront](https://arbitrica.com/blog/which-booking-sites-guarantee-final-price-upfront.html).

### 4. Currency and the exchange rate

Booking shows prices in the currency you select, but the hotel usually charges in its own. Booking's documentation says that if the exchange rate changes before the order is completed, the traveler will pay a different price to the one shown. Its terms add that when you pay in your own currency, Booking's conversion fee is expressed as a percentage over European Central Bank rates, and that your card issuer may charge a foreign transaction fee (checked on November 13, 2026). Which currency to pay in is explained in [should you pay for a hotel in local currency](https://arbitrica.com/blog/pay-hotel-in-local-currency.html).

### 5. The deal sold out or does not apply to you

Genius rates are discounted rates for members of Booking's Genius programme, so a Genius price seen while signed in will not hold if you are signed out, and some offers are only for the app or for a limited number of rooms. Hotels also change prices all the time with dynamic pricing, so the last room at the price you saw can be gone a few minutes later. Why rates move so often is explained in [how hotel dynamic pricing works](https://arbitrica.com/blog/how-hotel-dynamic-pricing-works.html).

### 6. Rounding and where you search from

Booking's terms say some prices may be rounded to the nearest whole number, and payment is based on the unrounded price. Its partner documentation also says whether a charge is included depends on the traveler's and the product's countries, so the book price for the same stay can differ by where the request comes from. Different countries also get different inventory, local rates and promotions, which is why the same hotel can cost less [on Booking from another country](https://arbitrica.com/blog/booking-com-prices-different-countries.html).

## How Booking builds a price (checked on November 13, 2026)

Booking's documentation for partners uses this example for one stay:

| Layer | What it contains | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Base price | The room rate with no extra charges | £153.40 |
| Book price | Base price plus included charges, such as VAT | £167.21 |
| Total | Book price plus excluded charges, such as city tax (£1.53 in the example) | £168.74 |
| Not in any of them | Conditional charges, such as a cleaning fee that only applies in some cases, usually paid at the property | Varies |

The same documentation asks partners to label each price, for example "Includes taxes and charges" or "Additional charges may apply", and to keep prices consistent from search results to the property page and the order page. So the label under a price tells you which layer you are looking at.

## Is it a mistake?

Usually not. Most changes come from the room, guests, charges or currency above. Booking's terms do cover real errors: obvious errors and misprints are not binding, and Booking says it removes obvious pricing errors as soon as it becomes aware of them (checked on November 13, 2026). If the same hotel with the same conditions has a lower price on another website, Booking's price match promises to refund the difference under its rules, explained in [does Booking.com price match](https://arbitrica.com/blog/booking-com-price-match.html). A lower price that has sold out on Booking itself is not covered.

## How to see the real total before you pay

1. **Search with the real guests and rooms.** Enter every adult, child and room before you look at prices, so the results price the stay you will book.
2. **Match the rate you saw.** Check the room type, meal plan and cancellation policy against the search result.
3. **Read the label under the price.** "Includes taxes and charges" and "Additional charges may apply" mean different things.
4. **Look for charges paid at the property.** City tax and conditional fees can sit outside the online total.
5. **Check the currency.** Note which currency you will be charged in and when, and whether a conversion fee applies.
6. **Book a rate with free cancellation if you can.** If a lower total appears later, you can [rebook at the lower price](https://arbitrica.com/blog/rebook-hotel-at-lower-price.html) and cancel the original once the new booking is confirmed.

## The reservation price is not the lowest price

Getting the reservation total right tells you what one site will charge from one country. The same hotel can cost less on another site or searched from another country, where inventory, sales channels, local rates and promotions differ, and dynamic pricing moves each of them separately. Research across 1,000 hotels demonstrated a lower price in about 80% of countries, with a typical gap of 20-40%. In [Arbitrica's own price checks](https://arbitrica.com/blog/hotel-prices-by-country-data.html), a 4-night stay at White House Hotel Istanbul cost $1,066 searched from the UK and $708 searched from India.

## Frequently asked questions

### Why did the Booking.com price go up when I clicked Reserve?
Usually because the reservation page prices the exact room, rate and number of guests you picked, while the search result showed the lowest option for the search you made. Excluded charges, a currency conversion or a deal that sold out in the meantime can also move it. The order total on the last page is the number that counts.

### Is city tax included in the Booking.com price?
Not always. Booking's own rules for partners split charges into included and excluded ones, and city tax is often excluded and paid at the property. Read the lines under the total before you pay.

### Can Booking.com charge more than the price shown?
The booked price is fixed, but the amount on your card can differ when the hotel charges in its own currency and the exchange rate moves before you pay, or when your card adds a foreign transaction fee. Booking's terms also say obvious errors and misprints are not binding (checked on November 13, 2026).

### Does Booking.com show different prices to different people?
Yes, the same stay can show a different price to different people. Genius rates are only for signed-in members, some deals are mobile-only, and whether taxes and charges are included depends on the traveler's and the hotel's countries. Different countries also get different inventory, local rates and promotions.

### Will Booking.com honor the lower price I saw earlier?
Not automatically. If the same hotel with the same conditions has a lower price on another website, Booking's price match promises to refund the difference under its rules. A lower price that has since sold out on Booking itself is not covered.
