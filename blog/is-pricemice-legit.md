## Does PriceMice work?
It works the way NordVPN describes it. According to [NordVPN's page](https://nordvpn.com/blog/pricemice/):

- you install it on a Chromium-based desktop browser and sign in with a Nord Account, which needs only an email;

- on a Booking room you click "Find better price", and it checks the same dates, guests and room from other locations;

- it shows the lowest option, with taxes and fees if the booking site provides them;

- to book at that price you connect to the suggested country with NordVPN on desktop, or a Saily eSIM on mobile, and book it yourself.

TechRadar reported that it checks "dozens of regions" within seconds, and that NordVPN suggests savings of at least $20 per stay and up to $500. NordVPN's own page gives no figure, says deals are not guaranteed because prices change and rooms sell out, and warns that "prices are AI-matched and mistakes can happen". TechRadar adds that you stay responsible for checking the final price, that some destinations add charges at payment or on arrival, and that exchange rates and booking sites' live price tests can change the total.

So the check is real, and the gaps it looks for are real too. Prices and room availability differ by country and by site, and dynamic pricing moves them all day. In [Arbitrica's own price check](https://arbitrica.com/blog/hotel-prices-by-country-data.html), a 4-night stay at White House Hotel Istanbul cost $1,066 searched from the UK and $708 searched from India, 33.6% less, for the same hotel and the same dates.

## Where PriceMice stops
- **One booking site.** NordVPN's page names Booking as the supported site, with more "expected" later (checked on November 10, 2026). The same hotel on Expedia, Agoda, Hotels.com or Trip.com is not described as covered.

- **A set of countries.** NordVPN does not publish how many countries PriceMice checks; TechRadar describes "dozens of regions".

- **You finish the job.** After it flags a lower price, you change your location with a VPN and repeat the booking by hand.

- **Before you pay only.** NordVPN's page does not describe any price check after you book.
