# Arbitrica: like Meta's Muse, built for the best price on anything

Updated 2026-11-10 by Arbitrica. Canonical: https://arbitrica.com/blog/arbitrica-muse-for-best-price.html

## Key takeaways
- Muse, Meta's personal AI agent, books hotels through connected partners such as Expedia, Hotels.com and Agoda, at the price that partner shows you.
- Arbitrica is a personal AI agent built for one job: the lowest price in the world for the hotel, product or tickets you are buying.
- Arbitrica uses a patented approach to finding the lowest price: it searches the same item from 195 countries at once, across the sites that sell it, and checks that the item and the final total are identical before it shows you a price.
- In Arbitrica's 2026 test of 1,000 international hotels, Arbitrica found a lower total than Muse in 90% of cases and was never higher; on average 5% of the total price lower.

## Short answer
Think of Arbitrica as Muse for the best price. Muse is a general personal agent: it plans, picks and books, using a partner's price. Arbitrica is a personal AI agent that does one thing very well: when you are about to pay for a hotel, a product or event tickets, it finds the lowest price in the world for exactly that item and prepares the purchase so you only review and pay.

Arbitrica uses a patented approach to finding the lowest price: it searches the same item from 195 countries at once, across the sites that sell it, and checks that the item and the final total are identical before it shows you a price. Meta has not published any Muse feature that searches the same hotel from other countries (checked on November 8, 2026).

## What our 2026 test found
In Arbitrica's 2026 test of 1,000 international hotels, across dates that included Christmas, weekends and workdays, Arbitrica found a lower total than Muse in 90% of cases and the same total in the rest. It was never higher. On average the price Arbitrica found was 5% of the total hotel price lower.

*Chart: Arbitrica's 2026 test of 1,000 international hotels on dates including Christmas, weekends and workdays: lower than Muse in 90% of cases, never higher, 5% of the total price lower on average.*

The reason is the method, not luck: Muse books at the price its partner or the site shows in your market, while Arbitrica's patented approach searches the same hotel from 195 countries across sites and takes the lowest identical offer.

## Why searching other countries matters
The same hotel is often priced differently depending on where the search comes from, because rates, allotments and promotions are set market by market. In [Arbitrica's own price check](https://arbitrica.com/blog/hotel-prices-by-country-data.html), 4 nights at White House Hotel Istanbul cost $1,066 searched from the UK and $708 searched from India, 33.6% less, for the same hotel and the same dates.

*Chart: In research across 1,000 hotels searched from 100 countries, 80% of countries showed a lower price than the traveller's own. The typical gap was 20-40%.*

## The best price on anything, not only hotels
Arbitrica's agents work at the checkout for hotels, online shopping such as [Amazon](https://arbitrica.com/amazon.html) and [event tickets](https://arbitrica.com/events.html).
