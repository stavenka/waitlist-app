The complete guide to hidden hotel prices

The price you see for a hotel is not the only price for that hotel. Search the same hotel, for the same dates, from a different country and the result can be noticeably lower. The same hotel is often 5-30% cheaper from somewhere else. This is not a glitch. It follows from the way hotels sell their inventory around the world, and once you know how that works, you can stop treating the first price you see as the final one.

Why the same hotel can cost less from another country

Hotels do not sell through one channel at one price. They distribute their inventory through global online travel agencies (OTAs), regional booking sites, wholesalers, bed banks and local partners, and each of those works in particular countries with its own contracts, rate plans and promotions.

Different countries get access to different inventory, channels, local rates and promotions. A booking site that operates in Thailand may carry a rate plan negotiated for the Southeast Asian market. A partner in Poland may run a local promotion that is not offered to travelers searching from the United States. Searching the same hotel from another country can therefore open up lower prices that you cannot get from your own country.

These are what people mean by hidden hotel prices. Nobody is hiding them in a drawer. They are real, bookable offers that simply are not shown to a search made from your own country.

How big the gap can be

In a test of 1,000 hotels searched from 100 countries, 80% of countries showed a lower price than the traveler's own country. The typical gap was 20-40%.

Independent research points the same way. A 2024 NordVPN study found that travel bookings made through US websites came out up to 70% higher than through websites elsewhere (its largest gap was a car rental). In one example, a seven-night stay in a triple room at a Milan hotel for a family of three cost about $1,216 searched from the US and $1,012 searched from Italy.

The gap is not the same for every hotel or every date. It moves with season, demand and which promotions are running where. That is why a country that was lowest for one trip is often not the lowest for the next.

What drives the gap

Local rates and market-specific promotions

Hotels and their partners create rates for specific markets: rates for domestic residents, campaigns aimed at one country, or promotions tied to a local holiday. These rates are real and bookable, and they are offered through channels in that market.

Different distribution channels

Wholesalers and regional booking sites contract inventory on different terms from global sites. Some of that inventory is sold only in certain countries, at prices that reflect those terms. Commission levels also differ between channels and markets, which affects the final price.

Taxes, fees and currency

Some markets display prices with taxes included, others add them at the end. Currency conversion and payment fees add more variation. A price that looks lower is only lower if it covers the identical hotel with all taxes and fees included, which is why every result has to be checked like for like.

Resident-only rates

Some local rates are limited to residents of a country, and the hotel can ask for proof at check-in. These are not a saving for a visitor, so they need to be filtered out rather than booked. For more on how hotels price by market, see hotel price discrimination by location.

What does not find hidden hotel prices

A few popular tips get repeated often but do little for this problem:

  • Incognito mode. A private window clears cookies and recent history on that site. It does not change the country you search from, so it cannot open up the offers available in other countries.
  • Checking more sites from your own country. Within one country, large booking sites often show similar prices for the same hotel. Five searches from the same country tend to give five similar numbers.
  • Waiting for the perfect day. Prices do move over time, but timing alone does not reach offers that are only sold in another market.

Why searching by hand does not work

In theory, you can look for these prices yourself. Connect a VPN to another country, search the hotel again, note the price, check the taxes and fees, and repeat. In practice, doing this for all 195 countries is impossible. Each search takes minutes, results come back in different currencies and with different tax treatment, and every time you have to confirm that the result is the identical hotel with the same conditions. Most people try two or three countries and stop, which misses most of the spread. We compare the two approaches in VPN vs Arbitrica for hotel booking.

How to check a few countries yourself

If you want to test the idea on your next trip, keep it like for like:

  1. Write down your baselineNote the exact hotel, dates, guests, room type, meal plan, cancellation terms and the final total with taxes and fees.
  2. Pick a few countriesTry the destination country, a neighboring country and one large market in another region.
  3. Search the same hotel againUse the same hotel, dates and terms each time, and include local booking sites where you can.
  4. Convert and compare totalsCompare the final amount you will be charged in your own currency, including card and conversion fees.
  5. Skip resident-only ratesRead the rate conditions and leave out anything you do not qualify for.

Even a handful of countries is often enough to see that the price from your own country is not the only one. Covering all 195 is the part that needs an agent.

Frequently asked questions

What are hidden hotel prices?

They are real, bookable offers for the same hotel that are sold in other countries through local rates, regional channels and market promotions, and that a search from your own country does not show.

Why is the same hotel cheaper from another country?

Different countries get access to different inventory, channels, local rates and promotions. Searching the same hotel from another country can open up prices you cannot get from your own country.

Does incognito mode find lower hotel prices?

Not for this. A private window clears cookies on that site, but it does not change the country you search from, so it cannot reach offers sold in other countries.

Can I book a rate offered in another country?

Usually yes, as long as it is openly offered. Check the rate conditions, because some rates are limited to residents and the hotel can ask for proof at check-in.
