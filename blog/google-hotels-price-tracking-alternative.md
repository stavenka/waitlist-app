# Google Hotels price tracking alternative that also checks 195 countries

Updated 2026-11-24 by Arbitrica. Canonical: https://arbitrica.com/blog/google-hotels-price-tracking-alternative.html

## What Google Hotels price tracking does

According to [9to5Google](https://9to5google.com/2026/04/17/google-individual-hotel-price-tracking/) and [Skift](https://skift.com/2026/04/17/google-hotel-price-tracking-individual-properties/) (both April 17, 2026), [Techlicious](https://www.techlicious.com/blog/stop-refreshing-hotel-prices-google-will-do-it-for-you/) and [Google's Travel help page on hotel prices](https://support.google.com/travel/answer/6276008) (all checked on November 24, 2026):

- **One hotel or a whole search:** since April 17, 2026 you can track an individual hotel. On desktop you open the hotel in Google Hotels with your dates and turn on the "Track this hotel" toggle on its Overview or Prices tab; on mobile it sits under Prices. The older "Track all hotels" option for a city and dates is still at the top of the results.
- **Email alerts:** the articles describe emailed price-drop alerts. You need to be signed in to a Google account, and tracked hotels are managed in your Google account settings.
- **Who gets it:** Skift and 9to5Google say it launched globally for signed-in users in English and Spanish; Techlicious says English.
- **Where prices come from:** Google's help page says prices come from partners such as hotel owners, online travel agencies and metasearch engines, and its price accuracy policy says partner prices must include taxes and fees and match the total on the partner's booking page.
- **Prices can differ by viewer:** the same help page says partner prices can vary by device type, by whether you are signed in, and by audience lists, and marks those prices with an asterisk.

## Where Google Hotels price tracking stops

- **Your own search.** The alert follows the prices Google shows for your search. The Google pages and launch coverage we read do not describe searching the same hotel from other countries (checked on November 24, 2026).
- **You do the booking.** Google lists partner prices and sends you to the partner's site. Booking, cancelling and booking again are up to you.
- **After you book.** None of the pages we read describe tracking a booking you already made or rebooking it when the price drops.
- **Timing.** An email reaches you after the drop. Low rates can go quickly, so by the time you open it the room may be gone at that price.

The gap between countries is often the biggest one. In [Arbitrica's own price check](https://arbitrica.com/blog/hotel-prices-by-country-data.html), a 4-night stay at White House Hotel Istanbul cost $1,066 searched from the UK and $708 searched from India, 33.6% less, for the same hotel and the same dates. Prices and room availability differ by country and by site, and dynamic pricing moves them through the day.

## How Arbitrica differs

Arbitrica is a personal AI agent that shows up after you choose your hotel room. For the hotel you have chosen, it:

Price drops after booking often have to be acted on at once, before someone else takes the room at that rate, which is why a personal agent that acts instantly helps. Searching 195 countries by hand is humanly impossible; that is the job Arbitrica does. Research demonstrated an average of $47 per hotel booking with Arbitrica.
