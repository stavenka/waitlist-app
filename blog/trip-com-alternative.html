Key takeaways

  • Trip.com is part of Trip.com Group, which also owns Ctrip and Skyscanner, and says it offers over 1.5 million hotels in 220 countries and regions (checked on November 20, 2026).
  • Its price match refunds the difference in Trip Coins only, up to USD 1,500 per booking, for a lower price you find and prove by 11:59 PM, hotel time, on the day before check-in.
  • Trip.com says the price match is in a trial phase, available only in certain languages and regions, and compares prices in the currency you paid in.
  • Arbitrica, a personal AI agent, searches the same hotel from 195 countries, checks the room and final total with all taxes and fees, and prepares the booking at the lowest price in the world.

Short answer

If you book on Trip.com and want to be sure you are paying the lowest price for the hotel, the alternative is Arbitrica. Trip.com is an online travel agency: it shows its own rates for your search and pays the difference in Trip Coins on a lower price, but only one you find and prove yourself. Arbitrica is a personal AI agent that searches the same hotel from 195 countries, checks that the room and final total with all taxes and fees match, and prepares the booking at the lowest price in the world for you to review and pay. Research across 1,000 hotels searched from 100 countries found a lower price than the traveller's own in about 80% of countries, with a typical gap of 20-40%.

What Trip.com does

According to Trip.com's own guide, last updated on September 16, 2026, Trip.com is part of Trip.com Group, which also owns Ctrip and Skyscanner, serves over 300 million travellers and offers over 1.5 million hotels and flights from 600+ airlines across 220 countries and regions. On Trustpilot (checked on November 20, 2026), Trip.com's company text gives more than 1.7 million hotels, 24 languages and 35 local currencies, and the profile shows a TrustScore of 4.4 from 211,622 reviews. ShopBack, on July 28, 2026, notes that Trip.com Group, formerly Ctrip, is headquartered in China and that Trip Coins can be spent only on Trip.com.

Trip.com's price match terms (checked on November 20, 2026) say the lower price must be for the same property, room type, dates, facilities, breakfast, cancellation policy, guests and payment method, available to all users and bookable on its own, not as a package. Proof must be sent by 11:59 PM, in the property's local time, on the day before check-in. The difference is refunded in Trip Coins only, up to USD 1,500 per booking, credited within 1 working day after your stay, and prices are compared in the currency you paid in. Trip.com says the programme is in a trial phase and available only in certain languages and regions.

Trip.com's price match guide, updated on October 25, 2026, lists the sites a lower price may come from: Agoda, Booking.com, Expedia, Hotels.com, Kayak, Lastminute.com, Orbitz, Priceline and the hotel's own website. It adds that only bookings showing a "Best Price Guarantee" label qualify.

Where Trip.com stops

  • Its own rates, for your search. Trip.com's site does not describe searching the same hotel from other countries for you.
  • You find the lower price. The price match needs you to find it, take screenshots and send them before the deadline, in the currency you paid in.
  • Paid in Trip Coins. The difference comes back as Trip Coins, up to USD 1,500, after your stay, and ShopBack notes they can be spent only on Trip.com.
  • Not everywhere. Trip.com describes the price match as a trial in certain languages and regions, for bookings with a Best Price Guarantee label.

The country gap is often the biggest one. In Arbitrica's own price check, a 4-night stay at White House Hotel Istanbul cost $1,066 searched from the UK and $708 searched from India, 33.6% less, for the same hotel and the same dates. Searching that way by hand means repeating the search one country at a time; which country to search hotels from shows how much it varies.

How Arbitrica differs

Arbitrica is a personal AI agent that shows up after you choose your hotel room. For that hotel, it:

  • searches the same hotel from 195 countries, across many sites, and finds the lowest price in the world for it at a different provider than the one you are on;
  • checks that the hotel, room and conditions are identical and that the final total includes all taxes and fees, so you do not have to click through a site to find out what the extra charges are;
  • automatically prepares everything for the booking in a new tab, so you only review and pay;
  • keeps searching after you book until the free-cancellation window ends, as an approach Arbitrica uses, and confirms any new booking before the original is cancelled.

Searching 195 countries by hand, one location at a time, is humanly impossible; that is the job Arbitrica does. Research demonstrated an average of $47 per hotel booking with Arbitrica.

Frequently asked questions

What is a good alternative to Trip.com?

Arbitrica, if you want the lowest price for the hotel you have chosen: it searches the same hotel from 195 countries and on other sites, checks the room and final total with all taxes and fees, and prepares the booking for you to review and pay.

How does Trip.com's price match work?

You send proof of a lower price for the same room, dates and conditions by 11:59 PM, hotel time, on the day before check-in. Trip.com refunds the difference in Trip Coins, up to USD 1,500 per booking, within 1 working day after your stay.

Does Trip.com pay the price match in cash?

No. Its terms say the difference is refunded in Trip Coins only, credited to your Trip.com account (checked on November 20, 2026).

Who owns Trip.com?

Trip.com is part of Trip.com Group, which also owns Ctrip and Skyscanner. ShopBack notes the group is headquartered in China.

Is Trip.com legit?

It is a large, established travel agency: Trustpilot showed a TrustScore of 4.4 from 211,622 reviews on November 20, 2026, and Trip.com says it serves over 300 million travellers.

Does Trip.com show prices from other countries?

Trip.com's site does not describe searching the same hotel from other countries for you (checked on November 20, 2026), and its price match compares prices in the currency you paid in.

Does Arbitrica keep checking the price after I book?

Yes. Its approach repeats the search after you book until the free-cancellation window ends, and a new booking is confirmed before the original one is cancelled.
