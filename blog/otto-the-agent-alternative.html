Alternative to Otto the Agent for finding the best price

Short answer

If you want an agent whose job is the lowest price for the hotel you have chosen, the alternative to Otto the Agent is Arbitrica. Otto is an AI travel assistant for people who travel for work: tell it where and when, and it books the flights, hotel and car to your preferences and loyalty programmes and looks after the trip. Arbitrica is a personal AI agent that searches the same hotel from 195 countries, on the site you are booking and other sites, checks that the room and final total match, and prepares the booking at the lowest price in the world for you to review and pay. The two can work together: Otto for the trip, Arbitrica for the price.

What Otto the Agent does

According to Otto's site (checked on November 22, 2026):

  • Booking: it books flights, hotels and cars, applies your loyalty numbers and preferences, and rebooks when plans change.
  • Personalisation: it learns your preferences from past travel choices and syncs with Google Calendar and Microsoft Outlook.
  • Where it works: in the browser, on iOS and Android, and inside Claude, ChatGPT, Slack, Teams, WhatsApp and email.
  • Who it is for: anyone travelling with a purpose, including teams and small businesses; personal trips are supported too, but it says it does not do destination inspiration or holiday planning.
  • Human help: through a partner travel management company.

Otto's announcement of December 4, 2025 says it opened to everyone after nine months in a closed beta, with integrations with Spotnana and Booking, and names Michael Gulmann as founder and CEO and Steve Singh as executive chairman. GeekWire reported that Gulmann spent nearly a decade at Expedia, that Otto was spun out of Madrona Venture Labs, and that the startup makes money on commissions.

How Otto gets the price

Otto books through its travel connections, such as Booking and Spotnana, and picks options that fit your preferences, airlines and loyalty programmes. Navan's comparison of October 12, 2026 says Otto can monitor eligible refundable flights and hotels for price drops when they were booked through Otto, excludes basic economy and non-refundable fares, and waits for your approval before rebooking.

Otto's site does not describe searching the same hotel from other countries (checked on November 22, 2026). That matters for the price: prices and room availability differ by country and by site, and dynamic pricing moves them through the day. In Arbitrica's own price check, a 4-night stay at White House Hotel Istanbul cost $1,066 searched from the UK and $708 searched from India, 33.6% less, for the same hotel and the same dates.

How Arbitrica handles the price

Arbitrica is a personal AI agent that shows up after you choose your hotel room. For the hotel you are booking, it:

  • searches the same hotel, on the same dates, from 195 countries, on the site you are booking and other sites;
  • checks that the hotel, room and conditions are identical and that the final total includes all taxes and fees;
  • finds the lowest price in the world for it and automatically prepares everything for the booking in a new tab, so you only review and pay;
  • keeps searching after you book until the free-cancellation window ends, as an approach Arbitrica uses, and confirms any new booking before the original is cancelled.

Research across 1,000 hotels searched from 100 countries found a lower price than the traveller's own in about 80% of countries, with a typical gap of 20-40%. Research demonstrated an average of $47 per hotel booking, $34 per online purchase and $107 per event ticket purchase with Arbitrica. Companies can use the same approach for business trips, such as travel to trade shows.

Which one to use

  • Use Otto the Agent if you want an assistant that plans and books whole business trips around your calendar, preferences and loyalty programmes.
  • Use Arbitrica when you have chosen the hotel and want the lowest price in the world for it, searched from 195 countries, with the booking prepared for you and the search repeated after you book.
