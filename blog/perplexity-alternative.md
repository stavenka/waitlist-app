## What Perplexity does when you book or buy
Perplexity started as an AI search engine that answers with sources. It now handles two kinds of purchases inside the chat (checked on November 10, 2026):

- **Hotels:** in March 2025 Perplexity added hotel booking through the startup Selfbook, which provides the booking and payment technology, with reviews from Tripadvisor. [Skift reported](https://skift.com/2025/03/27/perplexity-now-handles-hotel-bookings-what-our-tests-show/) a pool of 140,000 hotels and called the flow "glitchy at this very early stage". [Foster Garvey's travel law blog](https://www.foster.com/newsroom/blog/duff-on-hospitality-law/travel-tech-trends-perplexitys-booking-leap-airbnb-incentives-ai-powered-planning/) notes that hotels not on Selfbook are sent to third-party sites such as Skyscanner.

- **Hotel search:** Perplexity's [changelog of August 7, 2025](https://www.perplexity.ai/changelog/what-we-shipped-august-8th) added availability and price filtering for hotels, so you can enter dates or a budget and see prices in the results.

- **Shopping:** on November 25, 2025 Perplexity launched [a shopping experience for all US users](https://www.perplexity.ai/hub/blog/shopping-that-puts-you-first), with product cards and checkout through PayPal in the chat. Perplexity says "retailers stay at the center of the transaction as the merchant of record" and handle returns.

- **Instant Buy:** Perplexity's [help page](https://www.perplexity.ai/help-center/en/articles/10352906-what-is-instant-buy) says Instant Buy is available to all users in the United States and that your order is sent straight to the merchant. Its [PayPal page](https://www.perplexity.ai/help-center/en/articles/12932923-instant-buy-buy-with-paypal) names merchants such as Wayfair and Abercrombie & Fitch, plus thousands of stores on BigCommerce, Shopware and Wix.

Perplexity's Comet browser can also search Expedia, surface options and help you book, according to [PhocusWire's report on the Expedia partnership](https://www.phocuswire.com/expedia-group-perplexity-comet-partnership-ai). A [Conduit review of AI hotel booking](https://www.conduit.ai/blog/how-ai-agents-book-hotels) updated on October 30, 2026 still describes Perplexity's hotel payment as running on Selfbook's technology inside Perplexity.

## How Perplexity gets the price
Perplexity does not set the price. A hotel booked through Selfbook is sold at the rate that hotel offers for your search, and a product bought with Instant Buy is sold by the merchant at the merchant's price; Perplexity's help page sends price questions to the brand or retailer. Perplexity's blog and help pages do not describe searching the same hotel or product from other countries, or watching the price after you book or buy (checked on November 10, 2026).

That matters because the same hotel or product is often priced differently depending on where the search comes from. Hotels, booking sites and retailers agree rates, stock and promotions market by market, and dynamic pricing moves them all day. In [Arbitrica's own price check](https://arbitrica.com/blog/hotel-prices-by-country-data.html), a 4-night stay at White House Hotel Istanbul cost $1,066 searched from the UK and $708 searched from India, 33.6% less, for the same hotel and the same dates.

## How Arbitrica handles the price
Arbitrica is a personal AI agent that shows up when you are about to pay. For the hotel, product or event ticket you have chosen, it:

- searches the same item from 195 countries, on the site you are on and other sites;

- checks that it is identical (the same hotel and room, or the same product) and that the final total includes all taxes and fees;

- finds the lowest price in the world for it and automatically prepares everything in a new tab, so you only review and pay;

- keeps searching after you book until the free-cancellation or return window ends, as an approach Arbitrica uses, and confirms any new booking before the original is cancelled.

Doing the same by hand means switching your location country by country with a VPN and repeating every search. Across 195 countries that is humanly impossible, which is the job Arbitrica does. Research demonstrated an average of $47 per hotel booking, $34 per online purchase and $107 per event ticket purchase with Arbitrica.
