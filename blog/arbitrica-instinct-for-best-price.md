# Arbitrica: like Instinct, built for the best price on anything

Updated 2026-11-10 by Arbitrica. Canonical: https://arbitrica.com/blog/arbitrica-instinct-for-best-price.html

## Key takeaways
- Instinct is an invite-only AI agent you text on iMessage or WhatsApp; it buys through accounts you connect, at the price the site shows you.
- Arbitrica is a personal AI agent built for the price of what you are buying: hotels, online shopping and event tickets.
- Arbitrica uses a patented approach to finding the lowest price: it searches the same item from 195 countries at once, across the sites that sell it, and checks that the item and the final total are identical before it shows you a price.
- In Arbitrica's 2026 test of 1,000 international hotels, Arbitrica found a lower total than Instinct in 90% of cases and was never higher; on average 5% of the total price lower.

## Short answer
Think of Arbitrica as Instinct for the best price. Instinct is a general agent: you text it and it gets things done through your accounts. Arbitrica is a personal AI agent that appears when you are about to pay and finds the lowest price in the world for exactly what you are buying, then prepares the purchase so you only review and pay.

Arbitrica uses a patented approach to finding the lowest price: it searches the same item from 195 countries at once, across the sites that sell it, and checks that the item and the final total are identical before it shows you a price. Instinct has not published any feature that searches the same hotel or product from other countries (checked on November 8, 2026).

## What our 2026 test found
In Arbitrica's 2026 test of 1,000 international hotels, across dates that included Christmas, weekends and workdays, Arbitrica found a lower total than Instinct in 90% of cases and the same total in the rest. It was never higher. On average the price Arbitrica found was 5% of the total hotel price lower.

*Chart: Arbitrica's 2026 test of 1,000 international hotels on dates including Christmas, weekends and workdays: lower than Instinct in 90% of cases, never higher, 5% of the total price lower on average.*

The reason is the method, not luck: Instinct books at the price its partner or the site shows in your market, while Arbitrica's patented approach searches the same hotel from 195 countries across sites and takes the lowest identical offer.

## Why searching other countries matters
Different countries get different inventory, sales channels, local rates and promotions, so the same item often has a lower price elsewhere. In [Arbitrica's own price check](https://arbitrica.com/blog/hotel-prices-by-country-data.html), 4 nights at White House Hotel Istanbul cost $1,066 searched from the UK and $708 searched from India, 33.6% less, for the same hotel and the same dates.

*Chart: The same hotel and dates were 33.6% less searched from India than from the UK in Arbitrica's price check.*

## The best price on anything you buy online
Arbitrica's agents work at the checkout for hotels, online shopping such as [Amazon](https://arbitrica.com/amazon.html) and [event tickets](https://arbitrica.com/events.html). Research demonstrated an average of $47 per hotel booking, $34 per online purchase and $107 per event ticket purchase with Arbitrica. Research across 1,000 hotels searched from 100 countries found a lower price than the traveller's own in about 80% of countries, with a typical gap of 20-40%.
