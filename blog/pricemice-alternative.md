## What PriceMice does
Source: https://nordvpn.com/blog/pricemice/ (September 9, 2026). It checks the room you are viewing against prices in a set of other countries; works with Booking.com, with more sites "expected to come soon"; runs on Chromium desktop browsers; does not book for you (you connect to that country, for example with a VPN, and book yourself). NordVPN notes that "prices are AI-matched and mistakes can happen" and that taxes and fees appear only if the platform provides them.

## Where PriceMice stops
- One site (Booking.com at launch).
- A set of countries; the number is not published.
- You finish the booking by hand.
- Before you pay only; no checking after you book.

## How Arbitrica handles the same hotel
1. At checkout, it searches the same hotel on the same dates from 195 countries.
2. It checks the same hotel and room and a final total including all taxes and fees.
3. It prepares everything in a new tab at the lowest price on Earth, at a different seller; you review and pay.
4. After you book, its approach repeats the search until the free-cancellation window ends; the new booking is confirmed before the original is cancelled.

Real example: $1,527.39 offered, $1,180.27 found for the identical hotel and dates; Arbitrica earned the traveller $347.12 (23%).

Its approach keeps searching after you book until the free-cancellation window ends. The only tool in this list that checks other countries. Source: https://arbitrica.com/
- Underbook: a browser extension and web checker that shows the price each booking site lists on Google Hotels for your hotel and dates, on Booking.com, Expedia, Hotels.com, Agoda and hotels' own sites. Its Watch my booking feature checks once a day until your free-cancellation date and emails you about a lower rate; you rebook and cancel yourself. Source: https://underbook.co/
- Hotel Ninja: a Chrome extension for desktop that shows the same room's final price across Booking.com, Expedia, Hotels.com, Agoda, Trip.com, Travelocity and Orbitz, then links you to the site you choose. Source: https://www.hotel-ninja.com/
- Tripdify: you paste a hotel link from Agoda, Booking.com or Trip.com and it checks the same room and dates on 10 booking and search sites as tax-inclusive totals, then sends you to the lowest one. Source: https://tripdify.com/
- Borderbasket: you enter a hotel, dates and guests and it lists offers from Booking.com, Expedia, Agoda, Trip.com, Hotels.com, Priceline and others by total price, after a sign-in. Source: https://borderbasket.com/
- Google Hotels: lists the prices booking sites send to Google for your dates and can email you when the price changes. Source: https://www.google.com/travel/hotels
