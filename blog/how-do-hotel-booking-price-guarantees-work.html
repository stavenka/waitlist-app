How do hotel booking price guarantees work?

A guarantee pays only when you find an identical, public, lower offer and claim it in time. What Booking, Trip.com, Marriott, Hilton, Hyatt and IHG pay, the deadlines, and what to do instead.

Key takeaways

  • A price guarantee matches a lower price you find yourself, for exactly the same stay, if you file a claim before the deadline.
  • Hotel chains pay a bonus on top of the match when you book direct: an extra 25% off at Marriott and Hilton, 20% off or 5,000 points at Hyatt, and bonus points at IHG (checked on November 3, 2026).
  • Booking refunds the difference, Trip.com pays it in Trip Coins, and Expedia and Hotels.com removed their Hotel Price Guarantee on July 28, 2026.
  • Most claims fail on one mismatch: room type, cancellation policy, a member or coupon price, or a lower offer that has gone by the time it is checked.
  • A guarantee never looks for a lower price for you, including in other countries. Research across 1,000 hotels found a lower price in about 80% of countries, with a typical gap of 20-40%.

Short answer

A hotel price guarantee is a promise from the site or hotel chain you booked with: if you find exactly the same stay for less somewhere else and file a claim in time, it matches the lower price. Booking sites such as Booking and Trip.com refund the difference, while hotel chains such as Marriott, Hilton and Hyatt match the rate and add a bonus for booking direct. The guarantee never looks for the lower price for you, and it only pays for an offer it can check as identical, public and still bookable.

The two kinds of price guarantee

  • Booking-site price matches. Booking and Trip.com promise to refund the difference if you find the same accommodation for less on another website after you book with them. You claim through customer service or your booking page.
  • Hotel chain best rate guarantees. Marriott, Hilton, Hyatt and IHG promise that their own website has the best rate. If you book direct and find a lower qualifying rate on another site, they match it and add a reward, so the claim can leave you below the lower price you found.

Both work the same way underneath: you find the lower offer, you prove it, and the company that pays decides whether it qualifies.

How a claim works, step by step

  1. Book with the site or chain that offers the guaranteeChain guarantees only cover bookings made on the chain's own website or app.
  2. Find a lower offer for exactly the same staySame hotel, dates, guests, room type, meal plan and cancellation policy, on a public site that anyone can book without a login or code.
  3. Capture the evidenceSave the link and the final total with taxes and fees. Marriott does not accept screenshots, so the offer has to be live when it checks.
  4. Submit before the deadlineFor Marriott, Hilton and Hyatt that is within 24 hours of booking; for Trip.com, the day before check-in.
  5. Wait for the reviewThe company re-checks the offer. If it has gone, or anything differs, the claim is refused.

Why most claims fail

Every guarantee pays only for what the company decides is the same offer, and the company paying the claim writes that definition. The usual reasons for a refusal:

  • The lower rate has a different cancellation policy, room type, view or meal plan.
  • It is a member, app-only, coupon or corporate price, which none of these programmes treat as public.
  • The lower offer changed or disappeared before the company checked it.
  • The claim missed the window. Three of the four chains want it within 24 hours of booking.
  • The gap is too small, for example under 1% at Hilton, or the two prices are in different currencies.

Do price guarantees cover prices from other countries?

Not in practice. A guarantee only checks the offer you send, and the lowest price for the same hotel is often in a market you would never see from home. Hotels can set rates per country: Booking's developer documentation lists geo or country rates as "rates available based on the traveller's country" (checked on November 3, 2026). Some programmes also rule out rates that depend on where you live: LoyaltyLobby reported in May 2025 that Marriott excludes rates requiring a specific residency, ID or credit card, and IHG requires the lower rate to be in the same currency as your booking.

The gap is large enough to matter. Research across 1,000 hotels demonstrated a lower price in about 80% of countries, with a typical gap of 20-40%. In Arbitrica's own price checks, a 4-night stay at White House Hotel Istanbul cost $1,066 searched from the UK and $708 searched from India.

What to do instead (or as well)

  1. Find the lowest price before you pay. Check the same hotel from several countries and on several sites, comparing the final total with the same conditions.
  2. Book a rate with free cancellation. If the price falls later, you can rebook at the lower price and cancel the original once the new booking is confirmed, with no claim to file.
  3. Keep checking until the cancellation deadline. Prices often move after you book; see whether Expedia or Booking refund the difference.
  4. Use a chain guarantee when you book direct. If you found a lower public rate for a Marriott, Hilton, Hyatt or IHG hotel, booking direct and claiming within 24 hours can beat the lower price.

Frequently asked questions

Do hotel price guarantees actually work?

Yes, when every detail matches. They pay only for a public, bookable offer for exactly the same hotel, dates, room type, meal plan and cancellation policy, claimed before the deadline and still available when the company checks it. Most refusals come from one mismatch or a missed deadline.

Which hotel chain has the best price guarantee?

On their own pages checked on November 3, 2026, Marriott and Hilton add 25% off the room rate on top of the match, Hyatt adds 20% off or 5,000 points, and IHG adds 5 times the points on the room rate up to 40,000. Marriott, Hilton and Hyatt all want the claim within 24 hours of booking.

Does Booking.com refund the difference if the price drops after I book?

Booking's price match covers the same stay offered for less on another website, not a later drop in Booking's own price. If you booked a refundable rate, rebook at the lower price and cancel the original only after the new booking is confirmed.

Does Expedia still have a hotel price guarantee?

No. Expedia and Hotels.com removed the Hotel Price Guarantee with the One Key changes on July 28, 2026. Some older Expedia pages still describe it, so check the current terms.

Can I claim a lower price I found from another country?

Usually not. Guarantees check the offer you send against their own rules, and some exclude rates tied to residency or a different currency. It is simpler to book the lower price directly, before you pay, at whichever site offers it.
