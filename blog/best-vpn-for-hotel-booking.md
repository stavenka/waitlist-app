## What matters in a VPN for hotel prices
- **Country count.** Every extra country is another market you can check. This matters more than speed.
- **A server in the hotel's own country.** It won most often in published tests ([best VPN country for cheaper hotels](https://arbitrica.com/blog/best-vpn-country-for-cheaper-hotels.html)).
- **Few captchas and blocks on travel sites.** Some sites show captchas or block known VPN addresses ([FlyerTalk](https://www.flyertalk.com/forum/29638412-post4.html)).
- **Quick switching.** You will repeat the same search many times.
- **A clear no-logs policy.** You are entering card and identity details in the same session.

## Cheapest VPN for Booking.com, and free VPNs
For price checks you do not need premium features. Tom's Guide concluded that for flight searching alone a free VPN is enough ([Tom's Guide test, updated January 2026](https://www.tomsguide.com/computing/vpns/can-a-vpn-get-cheaper-flights)). Two cautions: free VPNs usually offer few countries, which defeats the purpose, and NordVPN warns that some free VPNs may install spyware or sell user data ([Explore, October 27, 2025](https://www.explore.com/2004000/does-using-vpn-book-airbnb-prevent-unnecessary-fees-price-increases)). USA Today's reporter paid about $14 each for two VPN subscriptions for a month and found only marginal savings ([USA Today test, January 2026](https://www.yahoo.com/lifestyle/articles/tried-using-vpn-score-cheaper-100134258.html)).

Whichever you choose, the cost is the subscription plus your time: several minutes per country, per hotel.

- Clear cookies or open a private window.

- Repeat with neighbouring and lower-income markets.
