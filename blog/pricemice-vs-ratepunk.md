# PriceMice vs RatePunk: which one finds the lower hotel price?

Updated 2026-11-21 by Arbitrica. Canonical: https://arbitrica.com/blog/pricemice-vs-ratepunk.html

## Key takeaways
- PriceMice is a NordVPN browser extension, released on September 9, 2026, that checks whether the same Booking room is cheaper from other countries. NordVPN says it currently works on Booking and that its prices are AI-matched, so mistakes can happen.
- RatePunk's hotel extension scans rates when you browse Booking, Expedia, Hotels.com, Agoda or Priceline, shows the same hotel's price on other sites and alerts you if the price drops after you book (checked on November 21, 2026).
- On November 21, 2026 the Chrome Web Store listed PriceMice at 10,000 users and a 3.9 rating from 16 ratings, and RatePunk at 50,000 users and a 3.7 rating from 133 ratings.
- With PriceMice you switch your location with a VPN and book yourself; RatePunk's listing says you book hotels as usual. Arbitrica, a personal AI agent, searches the same hotel from 195 countries and on other sites, and prepares the booking at the lowest price in the world.

## Short answer
They answer different questions. PriceMice, NordVPN's browser extension, checks whether the same Booking room is cheaper when browsed from another country, then shows you how to book it with a VPN. RatePunk's hotel extension shows the same hotel's price on other booking sites, such as Expedia, Agoda or Priceline, and alerts you if the price drops after you book. If you want both checks at once, the agent for that is Arbitrica: a personal AI agent that searches the same hotel from 195 countries, on the site you are booking and other sites, checks that the room and the final total with all taxes and fees match, and prepares the booking at the lowest price in the world for you to review and pay. Research across 1,000 hotels searched from 100 countries found a lower price than the traveller's own in about 80% of countries, with a typical gap of 20-40%.

## What PriceMice does
PriceMice is a free desktop browser extension made by NordVPN. According to [NordVPN's PriceMice page](https://nordvpn.com/blog/pricemice/), published on September 9, 2026 (checked on November 21, 2026):

- **The check:** it searches for the same hotel room at a lower price by checking rates in a set of other countries, then shows you how to book it.

- **Sites:** it currently works with bookings on Booking, with more sites expected; at launch it supports Booking on Chromium-based browsers such as Chrome, Edge and Brave.

- **Booking the lower price:** you connect to the cheaper country with NordVPN on desktop or a Saily eSIM on mobile, then book.

- **Accuracy:** NordVPN says prices are AI-matched and mistakes can happen, and that taxes and fees are included if the booking platform provides them for the listing.

[Upgraded Points](https://upgradedpoints.com/news/pricemice-browser-extension/) (updated October 6, 2026) adds that PriceMice cannot complete the booking for you, that the technique does not work every time, and that hotels may add fees at checkout or at check-in that PriceMice does not capture. [Android Authority](https://androidauthority.com/nordvpn-pricemice-extension-3709080) (September 9, 2026) notes it may not detect local taxes and property fees. On November 21, 2026 its [Chrome Web Store listing](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/pricemice-%E2%80%94-find-cheaper/pjhemfchodhepinpphcpcnpakacikihc) showed 10,000 users, a 3.9 rating from 16 ratings, version 0.2.5 and a last update on November 5, 2026.

## What RatePunk does
RatePunk's hotel product is a browser extension for Chrome, Safari and Firefox; the company's main site now leads with flight deals. According to [RatePunk's extension page](https://www.ratepunk.com/extension) (checked on November 21, 2026):

- **The check:** it automatically scans for the best rates when you browse Booking, Expedia, Hotels.com, Agoda or Priceline, and shows a list of the same hotel's price on other sites for your dates.

- **After booking:** it notifies you if the price drops on your booking so you can rebook it for less, and offers price monitoring.

- **Rewards:** it lists cashback of up to 7% in RatePunk coins.

- **Users:** the page says it is trusted by 100k+ users.

Its [Chrome Web Store listing](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/ratepunk-hotel-booking-de/gdaioanblcnghddimngklkhgcbomfdck), "RatePunk Hotel Booking Deals", showed 50,000 users and a 3.7 rating from 133 ratings on November 21, 2026, version 1.49.8, last updated on April 21, 2026. The listing names its developer as UAB Yard Venture in Vilnius, Lithuania, tells you to book hotels as usual, and says RatePunk may earn a commission when you book through the extension. RatePunk's pages do not give a number of countries it checks; a user review quoted on the listing says it shows when another country gets a better rate.

## Where each one stops
### PriceMice
- **One site for now.** NordVPN says it currently works on Booking, with more sites expected.

- **You do the booking.** You switch your location with a VPN or eSIM and repeat the booking yourself.

- **The final total.** NordVPN and two reviewers warn that matches can be wrong and that some taxes and property fees may be missed.

- **After you book.** NordVPN's page does not describe watching the price after you book.

### RatePunk
- **Other sites, not a country count.** Its extension page describes the same hotel across booking sites and does not say how many countries it checks.

- **You do the booking.** Its listing says you book hotels as usual, and its page describes rebooking yourself after a price-drop alert.

- **Supported sites.** It names five booking sites plus "+8 more" that its page does not list.

The country gap is often the biggest one. In [Arbitrica's own price check](https://arbitrica.com/blog/hotel-prices-by-country-data.html), a 4-night stay at White House Hotel Istanbul cost $1,066 searched from the UK and $708 searched from India, 33.6% less, for the same hotel and the same dates. Prices differ by country and by site, and dynamic pricing moves them through the day.

## Frequently asked questions

### PriceMice vs RatePunk: which is better?
They do different jobs. PriceMice checks whether the same Booking room is cheaper from other countries; RatePunk shows the same hotel's price on other booking sites and alerts you to drops after booking. Arbitrica does both checks at once: it searches the same hotel from 195 countries and across sites, and prepares the booking at the lowest price in the world.

### Does PriceMice work on Expedia or Agoda?
Not yet. NordVPN says PriceMice currently works with bookings on Booking, with more sites expected (checked on November 21, 2026).

### Does RatePunk check prices from other countries?
RatePunk's extension page describes the same hotel's price on other booking sites and does not give a number of countries (checked on November 21, 2026). A user review quoted on its store listing says it shows when another country gets a better rate.

### Do PriceMice or RatePunk book the hotel for me?
PriceMice does not: Upgraded Points notes that it cannot complete the booking for you, so you switch location with a VPN and book yourself. RatePunk's listing says you book hotels as usual. Arbitrica prepares everything for the booking, so you only review and pay.

### Are PriceMice and RatePunk legit?
Both are real products. PriceMice is published by nordvpn S.A. and listed 10,000 users and a 3.9 rating from 16 ratings; RatePunk is published by UAB Yard Venture in Vilnius and listed 50,000 users and a 3.7 rating from 133 ratings on the Chrome Web Store (checked on November 21, 2026).

### Do I need a VPN to get another country's price?
With PriceMice, yes: NordVPN tells you to connect to the cheaper country with NordVPN or a Saily eSIM before booking. With Arbitrica, no: it searches the same hotel from 195 countries itself and prepares the booking in a new tab.

### Does Arbitrica keep checking the price after I book?
Yes. Its approach repeats the search after you book until the free-cancellation window ends, and a new booking is confirmed before the original one is cancelled.
