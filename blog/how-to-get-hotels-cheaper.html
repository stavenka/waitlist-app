Short answer: how to get hotels cheaper

To get a hotel for less, change the things that actually set its price: the nights you stay, how far ahead you book, which site and which country you search from, which rate you pick and what you do after you book. Most people only change the first one. The 15 ways below are grouped in the order you meet them when you plan a trip.

  1. Check in on a Sunday instead of a Friday or Saturday.
  2. Book 1-2 weeks out for most city trips, earlier for event dates.
  3. Travel in the low months, January above all.
  4. Check the city's event calendar before you pick dates.
  5. Search the same hotel from other countries.
  6. Look at the hotel's own site and its member rate.
  7. Use the right site for the region, such as Agoda in Asia.
  8. Try a last-minute app for same-day stays.
  9. Look one neighbourhood over.
  10. Judge the final total, never the nightly rate.
  11. Pay in the hotel's local currency.
  12. Claim a best rate guarantee when you find a lower price.
  13. Book refundable and keep checking.
  14. Rebook when the price drops.
  15. Let an agent do the country-by-country search at the checkout.

Ways 1-4: when you stay

1. Check in on a Sunday. In the Hotels.com 2026 Hotel Price Index, June 9, 2026, US stays that started on a Sunday were 15% lower than stays that started on a Friday. Friday was the priciest check-in day in the US and Saturday the priciest for international stays. KAYAK UK, best time to book a hotel also names Sunday as the lowest day to stay.

2. Book 1-2 weeks out for most trips. Hotels.com found the sweet spot 8-14 days before travel, and travellers who booked last minute paid an average of 23% less than those who booked four or more months ahead. The exception is a date with a festival, a big match or a trade fair, when rooms run out; then book a refundable rate early and keep watching (way 13).

3. Travel in the low months. January was the lowest-priced month in the Hotels.com index, and the second week of October the priciest. KAYAK UK also found January the lowest month, with November close behind.

4. Check the event calendar first. One conference can lift every hotel in a city for its dates. Search the city with your month and words like "festival", "expo" or "marathon", and move by a few days if you can. More timing detail is in the best time to book a hotel guide.

Ways 5-9: where and how you search

5. Search the same hotel from other countries. Hotels sell the same room through many sites and many country markets, and each market can carry its own rate, allotment and promotion. Research across 1,000 hotels searched from 100 countries found a lower price than the traveller's own in about 80% of countries, with a typical gap of 20-40%. In Arbitrica's own price check, 4 nights at White House Hotel Istanbul cost $1,066 searched from the UK and $708 searched from India, 33.6% less, for the same hotel and the same dates. In NordVPN's commissioned tests a 14-night Bali stay was 26% lower searched from Indonesia than from the UK (NordVPN UK study, April 2024). The catch: checking country by country by hand takes minutes per country, which is why most people stop after one or two. The cheapest country to book hotels from and does a VPN make hotels cheaper pages explain the method and its limits.

6. Look at the hotel's own site and member rate. Chains reward direct bookings. Marriott says its member rates are at least 2% lower on weekdays and up to 5% lower at weekends (Marriott, It Pays to Book Direct), and Hotels.com advertises member prices of up to 20% off. Joining takes an email address. See whether to book direct or through Booking and Expedia.

7. Use the right site for the region. The same trip can price very differently by site. NordVPN's 2025 Southeast Asia study found gaps of 10-18% on Agoda and little difference on Booking and Airbnb for the same stays (NordVPN Southeast Asia study, May 2025). More in Agoda prices by country.

8. Try a last-minute app for same-day stays. In a NerdWallet test in St. Petersburg, Florida, 10 of 16 hotels on HotelTonight were up to 23% lower than on Booking for the same night, and 6 were up to 31% higher (NerdWallet, May 28, 2026). Most of those bookings are nonrefundable. The full method is in how to get cheap hotel rooms last minute.

9. Look one neighbourhood over. Hotels price against their nearest competitors, so a hotel one metro stop from the main sights often sits in a lower price band. Add transport time and fares before you decide.

Ways 10-12: how you book and pay

10. Judge the final total. Resort fees, city taxes, cleaning charges and service fees change the real price. On Airbnb the guest service fee is usually 14.1-16.5% of the booking subtotal (Airbnb Help Center). Go to the final payment screen for each option and write down the total for the whole stay. The guide to hidden hotel prices lists the fees to watch.

11. Pay in the hotel's local currency. When a site or a card terminal offers to charge you in your home currency, the conversion markup is often 3-5% (CardRatings, citing Visa, August 2026). Choose the local currency and a card with no foreign transaction fee. Details: pay in the hotel's local currency.

12. Claim a best rate guarantee. If you book a Marriott hotel direct and find the same hotel, room, dates and cancellation terms lower elsewhere, Marriott matches the rate and adds either 25% off or 5,000 Bonvoy points. The claim has to be made within 24 hours of booking and at least 24 hours before check-in (Marriott, It Pays to Book Direct). Other chains run similar programmes with their own rules; the guide to how to get the best hotel rates covers them.

Ways 13-15: after you book

13. Book refundable and keep checking. Hotel prices move every day. A refundable rate lets you act on a drop until the cancellation deadline. Expedia's Hotel Price Guarantee ended on July 28, 2026, so the checking is now up to you (Expedia price drops after booking).

14. Rebook when the price drops. Book the new rate first, confirm it, then cancel the old one inside its free cancellation window. Step-by-step: rebook a hotel at a lower price and what to do when the price drops after you book.

How do I get hotels cheaper?

How to get hotels cheaper on Reddit: what do people recommend?

The tips that come up most in travel threads are booking direct after checking the hotel's own member rate, using a VPN to search from another country, booking refundable and rebooking when the price drops, and calling the hotel to ask for a better rate. The published data supports the first three; results from calling vary by hotel.

How to get hotels cheaper last minute?

Hotels.com's 2026 index found last-minute bookers paid 23% less on average than people who booked four or more months out. For same-day stays, last-minute apps can help: in a NerdWallet test 10 of 16 hotels on HotelTonight were up to 23% lower than on Booking, but 6 were higher and most bookings are nonrefundable.

How to get hotels cheaper on Booking.com?

Sign in for Genius rates, filter by free cancellation, judge the final total including taxes, and check the same hotel from other countries, since Booking can show different offers in different markets. Arbitrica runs that country search for the hotel you picked and prepares the booking at the lowest price it finds.

How to get hotels cheaper on Expedia?

Use member prices, pick a refundable rate and keep checking, because Expedia's Hotel Price Guarantee ended on July 28, 2026 and it no longer refunds a drop after booking. Before paying, check whether the same hotel is lower on another site or from another country.

How to get hotels cheaper with a VPN?

Connect to another country, clear cookies, search the same hotel and room, and note the final total. NordVPN's commissioned tests found 7.9-26% on some hotels, while USA Today found differences of a few dollars. The country you appear to be in changes which market's offer you see; your IP address alone does not reprice an identical listing.

How to get cheap hotel rooms?

Pick low-demand nights, compare the full total for the same hotel across sites and countries, use member rates and best rate guarantees, and keep a refundable booking so you can rebook if the price drops.
