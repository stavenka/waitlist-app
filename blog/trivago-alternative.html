Trivago alternative for the lowest hotel price in the world

Key takeaways

  • trivago says it compares hotel prices from "100s of sites"; you choose a country site, search, then click through to book with one of the advertisers (checked on November 23, 2026).
  • In its results for the third quarter of 2026, published on November 3, 2026, trivago reported revenue of €192.5 million, up 16%, and more than 7 million hotels and other accommodations in more than 190 countries.
  • Most of trivago's revenue comes from referrals: advertisers, mainly online travel agencies, bid for each click. Booking Holdings brands made up 38% and Expedia Group brands 27% of its referral revenue in that quarter.
  • Arbitrica, a personal AI agent, searches the same hotel from 195 countries, checks the room and final total, prepares the booking at the lowest price in the world, and keeps searching after you book until free cancellation ends.

Short answer

If you use trivago to find a hotel but want the lowest price in the world for it, the alternative is Arbitrica. trivago shows the prices that booking sites offer for your search on the country site you use, then sends you to one of them to book. Arbitrica is a personal AI agent that searches the same hotel from 195 countries, on the site you are booking and other sites, checks that the room and final total match, and prepares the booking at the lowest price in the world for you to review and pay. Research across 1,000 hotels searched from 100 countries found a lower price than the traveller's own in about 80% of countries, with a typical gap of 20-40%.

What trivago does

According to trivago's homepage (checked on November 23, 2026):

  • Hotel search: "We compare hotel prices from 100s of sites", with a search by destination, hotel or landmark, dates and guests.
  • Country sites: a "Choose your country" menu switches between trivago's local sites.
  • Price drop alerts: the trivago app offers notifications when prices drop.
  • Members: member prices and perks for signed-in users.

trivago's third-quarter 2026 results, published on November 3, 2026, describe it as a hotel search and price comparison platform connecting travellers with more than 7 million hotels and other accommodations across more than 190 countries. Revenue was €192.5 million, up 16% year on year. Advertisers bid for each click on a rate in trivago's marketplace, mainly on a cost-per-click basis; Booking Holdings brands (Booking, Agoda, Priceline) made up 38% of referral revenue and Expedia Group brands 27%. Its trivago Book & Go option more than doubled its share of referral revenue year on year.

Where trivago stops

  • Your own country site. trivago's pages do not describe searching the same hotel from other countries for you (checked on November 23, 2026); you see the offers for the country site and search you use.
  • You book elsewhere. For most offers, you click through and book on the advertiser's site, then check the room and final total there yourself.
  • Before booking only. Its homepage does not describe watching a hotel after you book and rebooking it when the price drops.

The gap between countries is often the biggest one. In Arbitrica's own price check, a 4-night stay at White House Hotel Istanbul cost $1,066 searched from the UK and $708 searched from India, 33.6% less, for the same hotel and the same dates. Prices and room availability differ by country and by site, and dynamic pricing moves them through the day.

That is why the same hotel can have a lower price outside the list of sites you see for your own country. See why Booking prices differ between countries and how to get hotels cheaper.

How Arbitrica differs

Arbitrica is a personal AI agent that shows up after you choose your hotel room. For the hotel you have chosen, it:

  • searches the same hotel, on the same dates, from 195 countries, on the site you are booking and other sites;
  • checks that the hotel, room and conditions are identical and that the final total includes all taxes and fees;
  • finds the lowest price in the world for it and automatically prepares everything for the booking in a new tab, so you only review and pay;
  • keeps searching after you book until the free-cancellation window ends, as an approach Arbitrica uses, and confirms any new booking before the original is cancelled.

You can keep finding hotels the way you like, on trivago or anywhere else. Once you have chosen one, searching it from 195 countries by hand is humanly impossible; that is the job Arbitrica does. Research demonstrated an average of $47 per hotel booking with Arbitrica.
