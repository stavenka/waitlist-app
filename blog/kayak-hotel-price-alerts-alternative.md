## What KAYAK hotel price alerts do

According to KAYAK's [help page on creating a Price Alert](https://www.kayak.com/c/help/pricing/how-to-create-price-alert.62012.faq), its [guide to tracking hotel prices](https://www.kayak.com/news/track-hotel-prices/) (updated July 20, 2026) and its [page on how KAYAK gets prices](https://www.kayak.co.uk/c/help/about/how-does-kayak-get-prices.61916.faq) (all checked on November 24, 2026):

- **Destination or one hotel:** for a destination you enter the area, your dates and the number of rooms, and can add a star rating; for one hotel you tap the heart icon on its listing or add the hotel name and guests in your profile notifications. On the website, "Set Price Alert" sits at the top right of the hotel results.
- **Daily checks:** KAYAK says "Price changes are checked daily" and sends notifications to your inbox or your phone.
- **Account:** you need to sign in or create a KAYAK account before setting any Price Alert. Alerts are managed at kayak.com/alerts or in the app.
- **Where prices come from:** KAYAK describes itself as a travel search engine that processes results from over 5 million hotels and properties and hundreds of third-party sites. When you book, you book with the provider, not with KAYAK.
- **No price guarantee:** KAYAK says it does not set prices, that a price can change or disappear by the time you click through, and that it does not guarantee prices.
- **After you book:** its guide says hotel prices can drop at any time, including after you book, and suggests the free-cancellation filter so you can cancel and rebook.

## Where KAYAK hotel price alerts stop

- **Your own search.** KAYAK's pages do not describe searching the same hotel from other countries (checked on November 24, 2026). The alert follows the results for your search.
- **Once a day.** Checks run daily, so a drop that comes and goes within a day can be missed.
- **You do the rebooking.** KAYAK's own advice for a drop after booking is to cancel and book again yourself.
- **Final total.** The KAYAK pages we read do not say whether the alert price includes all taxes and fees; check the provider's final total before you pay.

The gap between countries is often the biggest one. In [Arbitrica's own price check](https://arbitrica.com/blog/hotel-prices-by-country-data.html), a 4-night stay at White House Hotel Istanbul cost $1,066 searched from the UK and $708 searched from India, 33.6% less, for the same hotel and the same dates. Prices and room availability differ by country and by site, and dynamic pricing moves them through the day.

## How Arbitrica differs

Arbitrica is a personal AI agent that shows up after you choose your hotel room. For the hotel you have chosen, it:

- searches the same hotel, on the same dates, from 195 countries, on the site you are booking and other sites;
- checks that the hotel, room and conditions are identical and that the final total includes all taxes and fees;
- finds the lowest price in the world for it and automatically prepares everything for the booking in a new tab, so you only review and pay;
- keeps searching after you book until the free-cancellation window ends, as an approach Arbitrica uses, and confirms any new booking before the original is cancelled.

Price drops after booking often have to be acted on at once, before someone else takes the room at that rate, which is why a personal agent that acts instantly helps. Searching 195 countries by hand is humanly impossible; that is the job Arbitrica does. Research demonstrated an average of $47 per hotel booking with Arbitrica.
