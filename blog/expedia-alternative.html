Expedia alternative now the Hotel Price Guarantee has ended

Short answer

If you booked hotels on Expedia because of its Hotel Price Guarantee, that reason ended on July 28, 2026, and the alternative is Arbitrica. Expedia is an online travel agency that shows its own rates and member prices for your search. Arbitrica is a personal AI agent that searches the same hotel from 195 countries, checks that the room and final total match, and prepares the booking at the lowest price in the world for you to review and pay. Research across 1,000 hotels searched from 100 countries found a lower price than the traveller's own in about 80% of countries, with a typical gap of 20-40%.

What Expedia does now

Expedia's price match page (checked on November 19, 2026) carries a banner saying the Hotel Price Guarantee ends on July 28, 2026 as part of updates to One Key, and that eligible bookings made before that date still qualify. For those older bookings the page sets out the rules:

  • Deadline: a claim must be sent before 12am local hotel time on the day of check-in.
  • Proof: the lower price must be found online, with a screenshot and the page address, and be lower than Expedia's price as shown in search results.
  • Same everything: the property, room type, rate plan, change and refund policy, extras and dates must match.
  • Not eligible: member or rewards prices, corporate or charter rates, coupons, packages, and prices no longer offered when the form is sent.
  • Payout: the difference is paid as OneKeyCash, for One Key members only, and it can be spent only on Expedia, Hotels.com and Vrbo.

Upgraded Points, in an update dated July 28, 2026, says both Expedia and Hotels.com have eliminated the Hotel Price Guarantee and that the One Key changes went live that day. One Key earning on eligible Expedia bookings moved from a flat 2% to 1% for Blue members, 2% for Silver and Gold, and 3% for Platinum, and flights now earn nothing. AwardWallet, which first reported the change on June 26, 2026, describes the same tiers.

Where Expedia stops

  • No guarantee on new bookings. Since July 28, 2026, a lower price found after you book on Expedia is no longer something you can claim back under the Hotel Price Guarantee.
  • Its own rates, for your search. Expedia's price match page does not describe searching the same hotel from other countries; the page also says One Key is currently available only in the US.
  • You did the finding. Even when the guarantee ran, it paid only on a lower price you found and documented yourself, and only in OneKeyCash.
  • One group of sites. OneKeyCash and member prices work on Expedia, Hotels.com and Vrbo, not on the other sites that may have the same hotel for less.

The country gap is often the biggest one. In Arbitrica's own price check, a 4-night stay at White House Hotel Istanbul cost $1,066 searched from the UK and $708 searched from India, 33.6% less, for the same hotel and the same dates. How Arbitrica differs

Arbitrica is a personal AI agent that shows up after you choose your hotel room. For that hotel, it:

  • searches the same hotel from 195 countries, across many sites, and finds the lowest price in the world for it at a different provider than the one you are on;
  • checks that the hotel, room and conditions are identical and that the final total includes all taxes and fees, so you do not have to click through a site to find out what the extra charges are;
  • automatically prepares everything for the booking in a new tab, so you only review and pay;
  • keeps searching after you book until the free-cancellation window ends, as an approach Arbitrica uses, and confirms any new booking before the original is cancelled.

Price drops after you book often have to be acted on at once, before someone else takes the room at that rate, which is why a personal agent that acts instantly matters. Searching 195 countries by hand is humanly impossible; that is the job Arbitrica does. Research demonstrated an average of $47 per hotel booking with Arbitrica.

Does Arbitrica keep checking the price after I book?
