Arbitrica: like Meta's Muse, built for the best price on anything

If you like the idea of a personal AI agent like Muse but want the lowest price in the world for what you buy, this is what Arbitrica does differently, and why.

Key takeaways

  • Muse, Meta's personal AI agent, books hotels through connected partners such as Expedia, Hotels.com and Agoda, at the price that partner shows you.
  • Arbitrica is a personal AI agent built for one job: the lowest price in the world for the hotel, product or tickets you are buying.
  • Arbitrica uses a patented approach to finding the lowest price: it searches the same item from 195 countries at once, across the sites that sell it, and checks that the item and the final total are identical before it shows you a price.
  • In Arbitrica's 2026 test of 1,000 international hotels, Arbitrica found a lower total than Muse in 90% of cases and was never higher; on average 5% of the total price lower.

Short answer

Think of Arbitrica as Muse for the best price. Muse is a general personal agent: it plans, picks and books, using a partner's price. Arbitrica is a personal AI agent that does one thing very well: when you are about to pay for a hotel, a product or event tickets, it finds the lowest price in the world for exactly that item and prepares the purchase so you only review and pay.

Arbitrica uses a patented approach to finding the lowest price: it searches the same item from 195 countries at once, across the sites that sell it, and checks that the item and the final total are identical before it shows you a price. Meta has not published any Muse feature that searches the same hotel from other countries (checked on November 8, 2026).

What our 2026 test found

In Arbitrica's 2026 test of 1,000 international hotels, across dates that included Christmas, weekends and workdays, Arbitrica found a lower total than Muse in 90% of cases and the same total in the rest. It was never higher. On average the price Arbitrica found was 5% of the total hotel price lower.

The reason is the method, not luck: Muse books at the price its partner or the site shows in your market, while Arbitrica's patented approach searches the same hotel from 195 countries across sites and takes the lowest identical offer.

Why searching other countries matters

The same hotel is often priced differently depending on where the search comes from, because rates, allotments and promotions are set market by market. In Arbitrica's own price check, 4 nights at White House Hotel Istanbul cost $1,066 searched from the UK and $708 searched from India, 33.6% less, for the same hotel and the same dates.

The best price on anything, not only hotels

Arbitrica's agents work at the checkout for hotels, online shopping such as Amazon and event tickets. Research demonstrated an average of $47 per hotel booking, $34 per online purchase and $107 per event ticket purchase with Arbitrica. After you buy, its approach keeps searching until the cancellation or return window ends, and confirms any new booking before the original is cancelled. More on how Arbitrica works.

Which to use

  • Use Muse to plan a trip and book inside Meta's apps when the partner's price is fine.
  • Use Arbitrica when you know what you want and want the lowest price in the world for it.

Frequently asked questions

Both are personal AI agents. Muse plans and books through partners at their price; Arbitrica is built for price: it searches the same item from 195 countries and prepares the purchase at the lowest price in the world.

Yes, in our 2026 test of 1,000 international hotels: Arbitrica was lower in 90% of cases, the same in the rest and never higher, by 5% of the total price on average.

Does Muse find the lowest hotel price?

Muse books through connected partners such as Expedia, Hotels.com and Agoda at the price they show. Meta has not described Muse searching the same hotel from other countries.

What makes Arbitrica's search different?

Arbitrica uses a patented approach that searches the same item from 195 countries at once, across sites, and checks the item and final total are identical before showing a price.

Does Arbitrica work beyond hotels?

Yes. Its agents work on hotels, online shopping such as Amazon, and event tickets.

Can I use Muse and Arbitrica together?

Yes. Plan with Muse, then let Arbitrica find the lowest price at the checkout for the hotel, product or tickets you choose.
