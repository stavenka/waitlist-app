Short answer

If you like StaySaavy's automatic rebooking but want the lowest price before you pay too, searched from other countries, the alternative is Arbitrica. StaySaavy starts after you book: you forward the confirmation or connect your email, it watches the price and rebooks when the rate drops. Arbitrica is a personal AI agent that searches the same hotel from 195 countries, on the site you are booking and other sites, checks that the room and final total match, and prepares the booking at the lowest price in the world for you to review and pay; its approach then keeps searching after you book until free cancellation ends. Research across 1,000 hotels searched from 100 countries found a lower price than the traveller's own in about 80% of countries, with a typical gap of 20-40%.

What StaySaavy does

According to StaySaavy's homepage and FAQ (checked on November 23, 2026):

  • Add your bookings: you forward hotel confirmation emails or connect your email account, and StaySaavy detects and tracks the reservations.
  • Monitoring: its FAQ says it monitors 40+ platforms, including Booking, Expedia, Hotels.com, Agoda and direct hotel websites.
  • Automatic rebooking: when the rate drops, StaySaavy handles the cancellation and rebooking; the FAQ says it can rebook within minutes of a significant drop and analyses cancellation policies first.
  • Fee: StaySaavy keeps 20% of the saving when it rebooks at a lower rate; if it saves nothing, there is no fee.
  • Guarantee: if it cannot cancel the original booking, its FAQ says it covers the difference so you do not pay for two rooms.

In a PhocusWire Startup Stage interview published on July 2, 2025, StaySaavy said it was founded in February 2025, is based in Tallinn, Estonia, and is led by founder and CEO Daniel Lochner, a hotel revenue strategist. It described itself as an email-connected platform that rebooks hotel reservations after booking, until the cancellation policy expires, and named B2B partnerships with OTAs, hotels and travel management companies as part of its plan.

Where StaySaavy stops

  • After booking only. StaySaavy works on reservations you have already made; its pages do not describe finding a lower price before you pay.
  • Your own country. StaySaavy's pages do not describe searching the same hotel from other countries (checked on November 23, 2026).
  • Email access. It needs your confirmation emails, forwarded or through a connected email account.
  • Refundable bookings. Rebooking depends on the original reservation's cancellation policy.

The gap between countries is often the biggest one. In Arbitrica's own price check, a 4-night stay at White House Hotel Istanbul cost $1,066 searched from the UK and $708 searched from India, 33.6% less, for the same hotel and the same dates. Prices and room availability differ by country and by site, and dynamic pricing moves them through the day.

How Arbitrica differs

Arbitrica is a personal AI agent that shows up after you choose your hotel room. For the hotel you have chosen, it:

  • searches the same hotel, on the same dates, from 195 countries, on the site you are booking and other sites;
  • checks that the hotel, room and conditions are identical and that the final total includes all taxes and fees;
  • finds the lowest price in the world for it and automatically prepares everything for the booking in a new tab, so you only review and pay;
  • keeps searching after you book until the free-cancellation window ends, as an approach Arbitrica uses, and confirms any new booking before the original is cancelled.

The first search happens before you pay, so the lowest price in the world is in front of you at the moment you book, not only after. Price drops after booking often have to be acted on at once, before someone else takes the room at that rate, which is why a personal agent that acts instantly helps. Research demonstrated an average of $47 per hotel booking with Arbitrica.
