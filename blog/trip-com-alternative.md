## What Trip.com does
According to [Trip.com's own guide](https://uk.trip.com/ask/questions/trip.com-reviews.html), last updated on September 16, 2026, Trip.com is part of Trip.com Group, which also owns Ctrip and Skyscanner, serves over 300 million travellers and offers over 1.5 million hotels and flights from 600+ airlines across 220 countries and regions. On [Trustpilot](https://uk.trustpilot.com/review/trip.com) (checked on November 20, 2026), Trip.com's company text gives more than 1.7 million hotels, 24 languages and 35 local currencies, and the profile shows a TrustScore of 4.4 from 211,622 reviews. [ShopBack](https://www.shopback.com/blog/travel/is-trip-com-cheaper-than-expedia-us-2026), on July 28, 2026, notes that Trip.com Group, formerly Ctrip, is headquartered in China and that Trip Coins can be spent only on Trip.com.

[Trip.com's price match terms](https://www.trip.com/hotels/pricematch) (checked on November 20, 2026) say the lower price must be for the same property, room type, dates, facilities, breakfast, cancellation policy, guests and payment method, available to all users and bookable on its own, not as a package. Proof must be sent by 11:59 PM, in the property's local time, on the day before check-in. The difference is refunded in Trip Coins only, up to USD 1,500 per booking, credited within 1 working day after your stay, and prices are compared in the currency you paid in. Trip.com says the programme is in a trial phase and available only in certain languages and regions.

[Trip.com's price match guide](https://uk.trip.com/ask/questions/price-match.html), updated on October 25, 2026, lists the sites a lower price may come from: Agoda, Booking.com, Expedia, Hotels.com, Kayak, Lastminute.com, Orbitz, Priceline and the hotel's own website. It adds that only bookings showing a "Best Price Guarantee" label qualify.

## Where Trip.com stops
- **Its own rates, for your search.** Trip.com's site does not describe searching the same hotel from other countries for you.

- **You find the lower price.** The price match needs you to find it, take screenshots and send them before the deadline, in the currency you paid in.

- **Paid in Trip Coins.** The difference comes back as Trip Coins, up to USD 1,500, after your stay, and ShopBack notes they can be spent only on Trip.com.

- **Not everywhere.** Trip.com describes the price match as a trial in certain languages and regions, for bookings with a Best Price Guarantee label.

The country gap is often the biggest one. In [Arbitrica's own price check](https://arbitrica.com/blog/hotel-prices-by-country-data.html), a 4-night stay at White House Hotel Istanbul cost $1,066 searched from the UK and $708 searched from India, 33.6% less, for the same hotel and the same dates. Searching that way by hand means repeating the search one country at a time; [which country to search hotels from](https://arbitrica.com/blog/cheapest-country-to-book-hotels-from.html) shows how much it varies.
