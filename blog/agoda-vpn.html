Short answer

Why Agoda shows bigger gaps

  • Market-specific deals. Some hotels restrict deep discounts to certain markets or to logged-in members.
  • App and member prices. Mobile-only prices, coupons and member rates can change the price you see.
  • Currency. Paying in a currency other than the hotel's can add about 3-5% according to reported cases.
  • Taxes and fees shown late. Agoda has historically shown base rates first and added taxes, service fees and local charges later.

Source for the list: The Traveler, June 30, 2026. More on paying for a hotel in local currency.

How to check Agoda from another country

  1. Note the final Agoda total for your room, dates and cancellation terms, through to the last screen before payment.
  2. Clear cookies or use a private window, then connect a VPN to the hotel's country or a neighbour.
  3. Search the same room again and take it to the final screen.
  4. Convert the totals to one currency and include every fee.
  5. Check the rate conditions and whether your card can pay in that market.

Frequently asked questions

Is Agoda cheaper with a VPN?

Often. NordVPN's 2025 test found the same Agoda hotels 10-18% lower from Singapore than from Malaysia. Results change by hotel, dates and country, and Agoda's fees can close part of the gap at checkout.

Which VPN country is cheapest for Agoda?

There is no single one. In the 2025 test Singapore beat Malaysia for all three hotels, and The Traveler reports Agoda is most often lowest in Asian markets. Arbitrica checks 195 countries.

Why does the Agoda price go up at checkout?

Agoda has historically shown base rates first, then added taxes, service fees and local charges later in the booking flow. Always take the booking to the final screen before you decide.

Should I pay Agoda in local currency?

Paying in a currency other than the hotel's can add about 3-5% in reported cases. Paying in the hotel's currency with a card that has no foreign transaction fee usually costs less.

Does Agoda block VPNs?

Agoda does not publish a VPN policy. Some travel sites show captchas to VPN addresses, and some market rates require residency, so read the conditions before you book.
