## Why Airbnb is different from hotel sites
A hotel sells the same room through many sites and markets, each with its own rates and promotions. An Airbnb listing usually has one host-set price. A Threads user quoted by [Explore, October 27, 2025](https://www.explore.com/2004000/does-using-vpn-book-airbnb-prevent-unnecessary-fees-price-increases) made the same point: hosts set their own rates. Reports of savings exist, but the same article says results varied and took trial and error, and one Reddit user was redirected to the more expensive English-language site when paying with an American card.

## What does change: fees and currency
Airbnb's [Airbnb Help Center](https://sq.airbnb.com/help/article/1857/what-are-airbnb-service-fees) says guests on the split-fee structure pay a service fee of 14.1% to 16.5% of the booking subtotal, and that when you pay in a different currency from the listing's, the guest fee includes an additional amount. Marketplace reported in February 2024 that Airbnb was adding a 2% fee for bookings in a different currency.

*Chart: What moves an Airbnb total, per the Airbnb Help Center. Card foreign transaction fees depend on your card.*

So the practical tips are: pay in the listing's currency where you can, use a card without foreign transaction fees, and check the full total including cleaning and service fees. More on [paying in local currency](https://arbitrica.com/blog/pay-hotel-in-local-currency.html).

## When a hotel gets you the lower price
Because hotel prices are set by market, the country switch matters far more for hotels than for Airbnb. In [Arbitrica's own price check](https://arbitrica.com/blog/hotel-prices-by-country-data.html), 4 nights at White House Hotel Istanbul cost $1,066 searched from the UK and $708 searched from India, 33.6% less, for the same hotel and the same dates. ## Frequently asked questions

### Does a VPN make Airbnb cheaper?
Usually not by much. Airbnb hosts set their own prices, and NordVPN's 2025 test found little or no meaningful difference on Airbnb by country. Currency and fees can change the total more than the country does.

### What do Reddit users say about Airbnb and VPNs?
Explore reports that results varied and took trial and error, and quotes a Reddit user who was redirected to the more expensive English-language site when paying with an American card.

### Can I get banned from Airbnb for using a VPN?
Airbnb does not publish a VPN rule in the sources we checked. Some users report worries about cancelled bookings, and Explore found no confirmed cases from reputable VPN users, but check the terms for your booking.

### Which currency should I pay Airbnb in?
Paying in the listing's currency avoids the extra amount Airbnb adds when you pay in a different currency. Use a card without foreign transaction fees.

### Is a hotel or Airbnb better for finding a lower price from another country?
Hotels. Hotel rates and promotions are set market by market, so searching other countries finds gaps of 20-40% in many cases. Arbitrica searches the same hotel from 195 countries.
