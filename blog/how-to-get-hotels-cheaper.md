## Short answer: how to get hotels cheaper
To get a hotel for less, first line up the booking sites for the same hotel and dates on a metasearch site such as Google Hotels, KAYAK or trivago, so you never take the first price you see. Then change the things that actually set its price: the nights you stay, how far ahead you book, which site and which country you search from, which rate you pick and what you do after you book. Most people only change the first one. The 15 ways below are grouped in the order you meet them when you plan a trip.

- Check in on a Sunday instead of a Friday or Saturday.
- Book 1-2 weeks out for most city trips, earlier for event dates.
- Travel in the low months, January above all.
- Check the city's event calendar before you pick dates.
- Search the same hotel from other countries.
- Look at the hotel's own site and its member rate.
- Use the right site for the region, such as Agoda in Asia.
- Try a last-minute app for same-day stays.
- Look one neighbourhood over.
- Judge the final total, never the nightly rate.
- Pay in the hotel's local currency.
- Claim a best rate guarantee when you find a lower price.
- Book refundable and keep checking.
- Rebook when the price drops.
- Let an agent do the country-by-country search at the checkout.

Before you pay, two more checks can take a little more off: call or email the hotel, and book through a cashback site (see below).

## Before the 15 ways: line up the booking sites and set a price alert
The first rule in most guides is never to book the first price you see. The quickest way to follow it is a metasearch site, which lists what different booking sites charge for the same hotel and dates. Open the two or three lowest offers and check the final total before you pick one.

- **Google Hotels.** Search the hotel by name and choose View prices to see offers from Google's partners for your dates ([Google Travel Help](https://support.google.com/travel/answer/6276008?hl=en)). Since April 17, 2026 you can also switch on price tracking for one specific hotel: on desktop it is a toggle on the hotel's result in Search, on mobile it sits under the Prices tab, and Google emails you if rates change significantly for your dates. It is available to signed-in users, in English and Spanish ([Google, April 17, 2026](https://blog.google/products-and-platforms/products/search/summer-travel-tips-google-search-ai/)).
- **KAYAK.** KAYAK says it searches hundreds of travel sites at once for hotel deals, and signed-in users can choose Set Price Alert on the hotel results ([KAYAK](https://www.kayak.com/hotels), checked on November 15, 2026).
- **trivago.** trivago says it compares hotel prices from hundreds of sites ([trivago](https://www.trivago.com/), checked on November 15, 2026).

A metasearch list still comes from one market: the one you search from. Google's own Hotel Center help lets a hotel set one rate rule per user country, such as us_rate, uk_rate and jp_rate ([Google Hotel Center Help](https://support.google.com/hotelprices/answer/14078201), checked on November 15, 2026), so the same hotel can carry a different price in another country. ## Ways 1-4: when you stay
**1. Check in on a Sunday.** In the [Hotels.com 2026 Hotel Price Index, June 9, 2026](https://www.expedia.com/newsroom/2026-hotel-price-index-how-americans-are-shopping-smarter-this-summer/), US stays that started on a Sunday were 15% lower than stays that started on a Friday. Friday was the priciest check-in day in the US and Saturday the priciest for international stays. [KAYAK UK, best time to book a hotel](https://www.kayak.co.uk/news/news/best-time-to-book-a-hotel/) also names Sunday as the lowest day to stay.

**2. Book 1-2 weeks out for most trips.** Hotels.com found the sweet spot 8-14 days before travel, and travellers who booked last minute paid an average of 23% less than those who booked four or more months ahead. The exception is a date with a festival, a big match or a trade fair, when rooms run out; then book a refundable rate early and keep watching (way 13).

**3. Travel in the low months.** January was the lowest-priced month in the Hotels.com index, and the second week of October the priciest. KAYAK UK also found January the lowest month, with November close behind.

**4. Check the event calendar first.** One conference can lift every hotel in a city for its dates. Search the city with your month and words like "festival", "expo" or "marathon", and move by a few days if you can. More timing detail is in the [best time to book a hotel](https://arbitrica.com/blog/best-time-to-book-hotels.html) guide.

## Ways 5-9: where and how you search
**5. Search the same hotel from other countries.** Hotels sell the same room through many sites and many country markets, and each market can carry its own rate, allotment and promotion. Research across 1,000 hotels searched from 100 countries found a lower price than the traveller's own in about 80% of countries, with a typical gap of 20-40%. In [Arbitrica's own price check](https://arbitrica.com/blog/hotel-prices-by-country-data.html), 4 nights at White House Hotel Istanbul cost $1,066 searched from the UK and $708 searched from India, 33.6% less, for the same hotel and the same dates. In NordVPN's commissioned tests a 14-night Bali stay was 26% lower searched from Indonesia than from the UK ([NordVPN UK study, April 2024](https://nordvpn.com/blog/price-difference-research-uk/)). The catch: checking country by country by hand takes minutes per country, which is why most people stop after one or two. The [cheapest country to book hotels from](https://arbitrica.com/blog/cheapest-country-to-book-hotels-from.html) and [does a VPN make hotels cheaper](https://arbitrica.com/blog/does-vpn-make-hotels-cheaper.html) pages explain the method and its limits.

**6. Look at the hotel's own site and member rate.** Chains reward direct bookings. Marriott says its member rates are at least 2% lower on weekdays and up to 5% lower at weekends ([Marriott, It Pays to Book Direct](https://www.marriott.com/online-hotel-booking.mi)), and Hotels.com advertises member prices of up to 20% off. Joining takes an email address. See whether to [book direct or through Booking and Expedia](https://arbitrica.com/blog/book-hotel-direct-or-booking-expedia.html).

**7. Use the right site for the region.** The same trip can price very differently by site. NordVPN's 2025 Southeast Asia study found gaps of 10-18% on Agoda and little difference on Booking and Airbnb for the same stays ([NordVPN Southeast Asia study, May 2025](https://nordvpn.com/zh/blog/genggai-ip-dizhi-jiesheng-feiyong/)). More in [Agoda prices by country](https://arbitrica.com/blog/agoda-vpn.html).

**8. Try a last-minute app for same-day stays.** In a NerdWallet test in St. Petersburg, Florida, 10 of 16 hotels on HotelTonight were up to 23% lower than on Booking for the same night, and 6 were up to 31% higher ([NerdWallet, May 28, 2026](https://www.nerdwallet.com/travel/learn/hotel-tonight-what-to-know-before-booking)). Most of those bookings are nonrefundable. The full method is in [how to get cheap hotel rooms last minute](https://arbitrica.com/blog/how-to-get-cheap-hotel-rooms-last-minute.html).

**9. Look one neighbourhood over.** Hotels price against their nearest competitors, so a hotel one metro stop from the main sights often sits in a lower price band. Add transport time and fares before you decide.

## Ways 10-12: how you book and pay
**10. Judge the final total.** Resort fees, city taxes, cleaning charges and service fees change the real price. On Airbnb the guest service fee is usually 14.1-16.5% of the booking subtotal ([Airbnb Help Center](https://sq.airbnb.com/help/article/1857/what-are-airbnb-service-fees)). Go to the final payment screen for each option and write down the total for the whole stay. The [guide to hidden hotel prices](https://arbitrica.com/blog/hidden-hotel-prices-guide.html) lists the fees to watch.

**11. Pay in the hotel's local currency.** When a site or a card terminal offers to charge you in your home currency, the conversion markup is often 3-5% ([CardRatings, citing Visa, August 2026](https://www.cardratings.com/travel/using-a-credit-card-abroad-dcc-fees.html)). Choose the local currency and a card with no foreign transaction fee. Details: [pay in the hotel's local currency](https://arbitrica.com/blog/pay-hotel-in-local-currency.html).

**12. Claim a best rate guarantee.** If you book a Marriott hotel direct and find the same hotel, room, dates and cancellation terms lower elsewhere, Marriott matches the rate and adds either 25% off or 5,000 Bonvoy points. The claim has to be made within 24 hours of booking and at least 24 hours before check-in ([Marriott, It Pays to Book Direct](https://www.marriott.com/online-hotel-booking.mi)). Other chains run similar programmes with their own rules; the guide to [how to get the best hotel rates](https://arbitrica.com/blog/how-to-get-the-best-hotel-rates.html) covers them.

## Two more checks before you pay: call the hotel and add cashback
Once you have the lowest total you can find, two small steps can take a little more off. Both work on top of the 15 ways above.

**Call or email the hotel.** In a Which? test published on November 5, 2026, contacting five UK hotels directly about a two-night weekend in November got a lower price than both the hotel's own website and Booking in four of the five cases. The gaps were £47 at Hotel Una in Brighton, £50.90 at The Resident in Edinburgh, £53.40 at Mollies in Manchester and £79 at Broad Street Townhouse in Bath; three of those four rates needed the hotel's free loyalty scheme or app ([Which?, November 5, 2026](https://www.which.co.uk/news/article/why-youll-pay-less-for-your-hotel-if-you-call-them-al2Y96d5opzc)). Name the room and dates, give the lowest total you have seen, and if the hotel will not move on price, ask for breakfast or an upgrade instead.

**Book through a cashback site.** Cashback sites such as TopCashback and Quidco pay back a share of bookings made through partner sites once the stay is over. On November 12, 2026 TopCashback listed 11% back on Hotels.com hotel bookings for new customers and 2% for existing customers in November and December, rising to 10% from January 2027, worked out on the price before taxes and fees ([TopCashback, Hotels.com](https://www.topcashback.co.uk/hotelscom/), checked on November 12, 2026). Rates change often, so check on the day you book.

## Does incognito mode get hotels cheaper? What the research found
**Short answer:** sometimes, by a little. Every list of hotel tips says to search in incognito mode or clear your cookies. The research shows the effect exists but is small next to the bigger levers, and that being signed in can lower the price rather than raise it.

- **Signed in can be lower, not higher.** A Northeastern University study of travel sites, presented at the Internet Measurement Conference in November 2014, found that members signed in to Orbitz and Cheaptickets were shown lower hotel prices than visitors who were not signed in, that Expedia and Hotels.com A/B tested users by steering some of them to more expensive hotels and others to less expensive ones, and that on Travelocity iPhone users saw prices about $15 lower on roughly 5% of hotels ([Science News, October 23, 2014](https://www.sciencenews.org/article/e-commerce-sites-personalize-search-results-maximize-profits), checked on November 18, 2026).
- **Browsing history can cost a few dollars.** A 2023 paper at the ACM Conference on Fairness, Accountability, and Transparency, "Your Browsing History May Cost You", found that on KAYAK flight searches across about 112 city pairs, incognito profiles generally got better prices than profiles with a browsing history, by a few dollars ([Crowd Dynamics Lab, June 6, 2025](https://crowddynamicslab.github.io/audits,/online/marketplaces/2025/06/06/browsing-history-cost/), checked on November 18, 2026).

So run two searches, one in a private window and one signed in to your Booking Genius, Expedia One Key or hotel loyalty account, and keep the lower final total. Neither search changes the country market you are shown, which is where the big gaps are: across 1,000 hotels searched from 100 countries, about 80% of countries showed a lower price than the traveller's own, with a typical gap of 20-40%. ## Package deals and member prices: do they get hotels cheaper?
**Short answer:** sometimes. Two tips on most lists are booking the hotel with a flight as a package and joining the member programme of a hotel chain or booking site. Both can lower the price, but neither is a fixed discount, so price the hotel on its own before you take either one.

- **Flight and hotel packages can be lower, with no fixed saving.** Expedia says its Bundle & Save deals "may cover the cost of your flights or one or more nights of your stay", and also that Bundle & Save pricing "varies and is not available on all packages"; it gives no percentage ([Expedia](https://www.expedia.com/product/bundle-and-save/), checked on November 19, 2026). Price the hotel alone and the flight alone for the same dates, then take the bundle only if its final total is lower.
- **Hotel chain member rates are small but real.** Marriott Bonvoy member rates are at least 2% off on weekdays and up to 5% off on weekends against the hotel's lowest public rate ([Marriott](https://www.marriott.com/en-gb/online-hotel-booking.mi), checked on November 19, 2026); the Hilton Honors Discount is "from 2% off" eligible rates and changes with the booking window, the country and the day of the week ([Hilton](https://www.hilton.com/en/hilton-honors/honors-discount-terms), checked on November 19, 2026). Neither can be combined with certain other offers.
- **Booking site member prices are larger.** Booking Genius takes 10-20% off at participating hotels, Expedia Member Prices 10% or more and AgodaVIP up to 25%, as set out in [why Agoda or Booking can be lower than the hotel's own website](https://arbitrica.com/blog/book-hotel-direct-or-booking-expedia.html).

All of these are prices for one country's market. The same member rate or package can have a different final total when the hotel is searched from another country: research across 1,000 hotels found a lower price in about 80% of countries, with a typical gap of 20-40%.
