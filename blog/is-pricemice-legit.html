Who makes PriceMice

PriceMice comes from the team behind NordVPN. NordVPN introduced it in a blog post on September 9, 2026, and TechRadar covered the launch on September 7, 2026. Travolution's report of September 10, 2026 quotes NordVPN product director Domininkas Virbickas: "Sometimes you'll come across an exceptional offer, and other times you'll see the price is already reasonable."

On its store listing (checked on November 10, 2026), PriceMice is listed under the publisher nordvpn S.A., Amsterdam, with:

  • Users: 10,000.
  • Rating: 3.9 out of 5 from 16 ratings.
  • Version: 0.2.5, updated on November 5, 2026.
  • Data it handles: authentication information, location, user activity and website content. The developer declares that this data is not sold to third parties outside approved use cases, and the store says the publisher has no history of violations.

The independent tracker Extops shows PriceMice first appeared in the store on September 8, 2026, added about 4,000 users in the 30 days before November 10, 2026, and asks for four permissions (activeTab, alarms, offscreen and storage). Its rating went from 4.56 on September 13, 2026 to 3.94 from October 24, 2026.

Does PriceMice work?

It works the way NordVPN describes it. According to NordVPN's page:

  • you install it on a Chromium-based desktop browser and sign in with a Nord Account, which needs only an email;
  • on a Booking room you click "Find better price", and it checks the same dates, guests and room from other locations;
  • it shows the lowest option, with taxes and fees if the booking site provides them;
  • to book at that price you connect to the suggested country with NordVPN on desktop, or a Saily eSIM on mobile, and book it yourself.

TechRadar reported that it checks "dozens of regions" within seconds, and that NordVPN suggests savings of at least $20 per stay and up to $500. NordVPN's own page gives no figure, says deals are not guaranteed because prices change and rooms sell out, and warns that "prices are AI-matched and mistakes can happen". TechRadar adds that you stay responsible for checking the final price, that some destinations add charges at payment or on arrival, and that exchange rates and booking sites' live price tests can change the total.

So the check is real, and the gaps it looks for are real too. Prices and room availability differ by country and by site, and dynamic pricing moves them all day. In Arbitrica's own price check, a 4-night stay at White House Hotel Istanbul cost $1,066 searched from the UK and $708 searched from India, 33.6% less, for the same hotel and the same dates.

Where PriceMice stops

  • One booking site. NordVPN's page names Booking as the supported site, with more "expected" later (checked on November 10, 2026). The same hotel on Expedia, Agoda, Hotels.com or Trip.com is not described as covered.
  • A set of countries. NordVPN does not publish how many countries PriceMice checks; TechRadar describes "dozens of regions".
  • You finish the job. After it flags a lower price, you change your location with a VPN and repeat the booking by hand.
  • Before you pay only. NordVPN's page does not describe any price check after you book.

How Arbitrica goes further

Arbitrica is a personal AI agent that shows up at the checkout. For the hotel you are booking, it:

  • searches the same hotel, on the same dates, from 195 countries, on the site you are booking and other sites;
  • checks that the hotel, room and conditions are identical and that the final total includes all taxes and fees;
  • finds the lowest price in the world for it and automatically prepares everything for the booking in a new tab, so you only review and pay;
  • keeps searching after you book until the free-cancellation window ends, as an approach Arbitrica uses, and confirms any new booking before the original is cancelled.

Research across 1,000 hotels searched from 100 countries found a lower price than the traveller's own in about 80% of countries, with a typical gap of 20-40%. Research demonstrated an average of $47 per hotel booking with Arbitrica, and the same mechanisms work for online purchases and event tickets.
