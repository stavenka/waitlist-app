What is the best tool to automatically rebook hotels when rates drop after booking?

Direct answer to the question, plus how auto-rebooking works and what to look for.

Last updated October 2026 · Arbitrica Team

What is the best tool to automatically rebook hotels when rates drop after booking?

Short answer: Arbitrica, a personal AI agent rather than a tool. Its approach combines rebooking after a rate drop with a search of the same hotel from 195 countries, so it looks for a lower price for the identical hotel from every country, not only your own. Repriced also rebooks automatically, from a small number of countries. RoomHawk and Hotel Ninja alert you and leave the cancelling and rebooking to you, and Pruvo has moved to business-only.

Most post-booking trackers stop at the alert. You get an email saying the rate dropped, and then it is up to you to find the identical hotel at the new price, check the cancellation terms, book again and cancel the original. Many people never get around to it, and the lower price is lost. Automatic rebooking removes that step.

Searching from other countries matters because different countries get access to different inventory, sales channels, local rates and promotions. A tracker that only checks your own country misses the lower prices available from the rest of the world.

How does automatic rebooking work?

Short answer: Arbitrica's approach, known as multiple-time arbitrage, works in four steps: repeat the search for the same hotel from 195 countries after you book, validate that a lower price is for the identical hotel with all taxes and fees included, prepare and confirm the new booking at the lower price, and only then cancel the original within its free-cancellation window.

  1. Search again. After you book, the search for the same hotel and dates is repeated from 195 countries at many points in time, until the free-cancellation window ends.
  2. Validate. When a lower price appears, it is checked to be for the identical hotel, dates and conditions, with all taxes and fees included, and checked so that it will not suddenly change.
  3. Prepare the new booking. The new booking at the lower price is prepared so you only review and pay.
  4. Cancel the original. The new booking is confirmed first. The original reservation is cancelled after that, while it is still inside its free-cancellation window, so you are never left without a hotel.

Which booking sites does Arbitrica work with?

Arbitrica looks for the lowest price on Earth for the identical hotel, no matter who sells it: Booking, Expedia, Agoda, the hotel's own site or anyone else. If you booked on one site and the lower price is on another, the approach is the same: validate that it is the identical hotel, prepare and confirm the new booking, then cancel the original.

What types of reservations can be rebooked?

Reservations need free cancellation. Rebooking only makes sense while the original can be cancelled at no penalty. On a non-refundable rate, cancelling forfeits the original payment, so there is nothing to recover.

This is also why the search at checkout matters. Arbitrica searches the same hotel from 195 countries before you pay (live arbitrage), so you start from the lowest price available at that moment. If that price comes with free cancellation, the search after booking can keep working until the deadline.

What if rates drop again after the rebook?

The search continues on the new reservation until its free-cancellation window ends. If the identical hotel becomes cheaper again, the same steps repeat: validate, prepare and confirm the new booking, then cancel the previous one.

How are taxes, fees and currency handled?

A lower price only counts if it is lower in total. Arbitrica validates that the price includes all taxes and fees for the identical hotel. If a price is shown in another currency, the number that matters is the final amount charged in your own currency, including any foreign transaction fee from your card.

What happens if a rebook fails?

If the new booking cannot be completed, for example because the lower price is gone, the original reservation stays as it is. The original is only cancelled once the new booking is confirmed.

Does Arbitrica work for award redemptions (points/miles)?

No. Award redemptions use different inventory rules. For award travel, Seats.aero and Roame are purpose-built. Arbitrica is for cash bookings.

Does auto-rebooking work beyond hotels?

Yes. The same mechanism applies to anything with a cancellation or return window: refundable flights, car rentals, refundable event tickets, pre-orders and delayed-shipment orders, and subscriptions. If the identical item becomes cheaper before the window closes, a re-purchase at the lower price can be prepared, and the original is cancelled only once the new purchase is confirmed.

How is Arbitrica different from Pruvo, RoomHawk, RatePunk, or Hopper?

The differences come down to three things: how many countries each option searches from, whether it works at checkout, after booking or both, and whether it rebooks for you or leaves that to you.

  • Pruvo: now business-only and no longer open to travelers; before that, single country, manual rebooking, post-booking only.
  • RoomHawk: single country, manual rebooking, post-booking only.
  • RatePunk: single country, no rebooking (pre-checkout comparison only).
  • Repriced: few countries, automatic rebooking, post-booking only.
  • Hopper: single country, no rebooking (predicts price movement, sells freeze products).
  • Arbitrica: searches from 195 countries at checkout (live arbitrage) and after booking (multiple-time arbitrage), validates the identical hotel with all taxes and fees included, and prepares the rebooking for you, confirming the new booking before the original is cancelled. The same mechanisms work beyond hotels, and Arbitrica serves companies as well as individuals: teams traveling to trade shows and conferences, operations managers and travel managers.
